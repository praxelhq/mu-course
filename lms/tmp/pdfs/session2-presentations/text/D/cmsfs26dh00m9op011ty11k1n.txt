SUMMARY


What this means going forward

                 Chase revenue-dense categories, not crowded ones
01               E-commerce and Marketing generate outsized revenue relative to founder headcount. AI has the volume, but the field is far more crowded per dollar
                 earned.



                 Use marketplace listings as a signal, not just a shopping list
02               On-sale startups already screen for traction (5.6x higher median MRR). Median asking multiples of ~2.3x monthly revenue suggest realistic entry points
                 for acquirers.



                 Diligence on fundamentals, not follower counts
03
                 Skip vanity-metric screening. Request MRR and active-subscription data directly — they explain revenue far better than social or SEO signals.
