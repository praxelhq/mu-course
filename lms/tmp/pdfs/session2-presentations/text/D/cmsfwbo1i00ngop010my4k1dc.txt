Not a Fluke


Robustness check applied
Each category's single largest company was removed to test
whether one outlier was inflating the median. Education's
median MRR dropped from $2,142 to $2,084 — still the
highest of any category.

This is a broad-based pattern across 30 companies, not a
single company skewing the average.
