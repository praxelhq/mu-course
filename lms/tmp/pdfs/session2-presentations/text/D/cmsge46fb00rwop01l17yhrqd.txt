 STRATEGIC IMPLICATIONS



Five takeaways for anyone building on — or buying from — this market
                                                                                                                                                        13
          Ignore the average.
01        With 57% of startups at $0 MRR and the top 1% holding 69.5% of revenue, benchmark against medians and cohorts, never the mean.



          Category size ≠ category efficiency.
02        AI leads on headcount, but E-comm and Social Media startups earn 2-3x more revenue per company — pick a lane by unit economics, not hype.



          Build authority, not just an audience.
03        Domain rating correlates with MRR 3x more strongly than X followers — SEO and content compounding outperform social reach.



          B2B still commands a premium.
04        B2B startups earn 2.4x the average MRR of B2C peers despite being outnumbered — pricing power favors business buyers.



          Undisclosed listings are the market's blind spot.
05        $8.1M in MRR sits behind unlabeled or stealth startups — more transparency here would materially improve buyer and benchmarking confidence.
