SETTING THE SCENE



What are we actually looking at?

                                                                             1 person or a tiny team
In plain terms: this is the world of solo founders and tiny teams who
build small software products — apps, AI tools, browser extensions —         Most “companies” here are one founder, sometimes two.
and sell them online. No investors, no big offices. Just a product, a
payment link, and real customers.


                                                                             Sold online, worldwide
Why trust the numbers?
                                                                             A product built in Paris can have paying users in Tokyo by
                                                                             lunchtime.
Every business here connects its real payment account (Stripe, RevenueCat,
and similar) to TrustMRR, which reads the actual revenue directly — not a
number the founder typed in. Think of it like a verified bank statement
instead of a resume.                                                         Priced in subscriptions


                                                                             Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) is the market’s scoreboard.

Why AI is the market leader right now



• Cheaper to build: AI coding assistants let one person ship a working product in days, not months.
• Clear, sellable value: “this saves you hours” is an easy pitch for automation and writing tools.
• Everyone needs it: from students to sales teams, AI tools sell across almost every audience type.
