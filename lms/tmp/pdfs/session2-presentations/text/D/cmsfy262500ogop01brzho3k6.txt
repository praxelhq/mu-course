RISK FACTORS

What This Screen Cannot Tell You — Diligence Before Capital

        01
                                                                            Revenue and MRR come from founder-connected payment APIs, not audited financials. Verify
                  Unaudited, Self-Reported Figures                          bank/processor statements directly before underwriting any specific name.



        02
                                                                            X/Twitter follower count shows ~0 correlation with revenue or MRR (r = 0.03) in this dataset. Don't
                  Vanity Metrics Don't Predict Revenue                      let social proof substitute for unit-economics diligence.



        03
                                                                            The 3.0x median multiple is drawn from 215 startups that chose to list — 21.5% of the base.
                  Small, Self-Selected Valuation Sample                     Founders selling into a soft category may under- or over-price relative to true market.



        04
                                                                            30-day MRR growth swings from -74% to over +300% inside single categories. Screen individual deals
                  Growth Averages Are Outlier-Driven                        on trend consistency and cohort retention, not one 30-day print.



        05
                                                                            This is a single frozen date (30 Jul 2026). Re-pull and re-run this benchmarking screen quarterly to
                  Snapshot, Not a Time Series                               catch category rotation early.

INVESTMENT IMPLICATIONS

Where This Screen Points the Fund

        01
                                                                            The power-law concentration (Slide 5) means sourcing volume matters less than conviction on
                  Go Deep, Not Wide                                         category leaders. Prioritize a shortlist of outlier candidates over broad small-check spraying.



        02
                                                                            AI commands the highest valuation multiple (4.15x) but among the lowest growth-consistency rates
                  Treat the AI Premium With Scrutiny                        (26% positive). Underwrite AI deals on defensibility and retention — not category momentum alone.



        03
                                                                            B2B startups post 5.7x the median MRR of B2C peers. All else equal, weight sourcing toward B2B /
                  Favor B2B and Hybrid Models                               “both” audience models for revenue durability.

                  Growth Thesis                                             strategy better than a venture-growth bet.

                  Institutionalize the Re-Screen                            verification before any term sheet.
