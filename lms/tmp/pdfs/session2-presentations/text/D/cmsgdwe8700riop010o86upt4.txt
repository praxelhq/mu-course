                     What we are asking the Board to do

                      TO NOTICE

                             1. Single-name concentration. One asset is 26.5 percent of MRR and there is no stated limit on single-asset exposure.
                             2. The tail is not free. 599 assets earn under Rs 1 lakh a month each, Rs 0.82 cr in aggregate, on the same overhead as the rest.
                             3. Half the revenue is outside the sector view. 274 untagged assets hold 56.5 percent of MRR. Every judgement on these pages is taken
                                against the remainder.


                      TO DECIDE

                             1. Whether to keep adding AI at 3.15x. 19.7 percent of the book by count, 13.4 percent of MRR, and growth concentrated in five names.
                                The same capital buys developer tools at 1.35x with 57 percent of assets growing.
                             2. Whether MRR remains the basis of carrying value. At a median 1.42x ARR to TTM, an MRR based mark overstates realised earnings on
                                nearly half the book.


                      TO APPROVE

                             1. A disposal programme covering mobile apps and the zero revenue tail, 96 assets with no MRR and no revenue in twelve months plus
                                the 29 listed mobile app assets, cleared at market rather than held for the quoted 8.0x.
                             2. An acquisition mandate in developer tools and analytics with a 2.0x TTM discipline, funded from disposals, with authority to approach
                                owners who are not listed given that listed supply is eight and seven assets respectively.
                             3. A data remediation deadline before the next capital cycle: sector tags on 274 assets, country of record on 168, and a decision on the
                                three payment rails where up to half of assets report nothing.
