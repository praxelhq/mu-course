Key Takeaways
What this means if you work in marketing




 1        Marketing-as-a-service is itself a proven, high-revenue category — not just a cost center for other businesses.




 2        Simple, clearly-tiered pricing shows up in almost every successful company here — confusing pricing is a growth blocker.




 3        B2B still dominates this space, so trust-building content (case studies, proof, direct outreach) outperforms broad awareness ads.




 4        Fast growth is achievable without huge followings — several top performers had modest social followings but strong retention.




                                                                                                                                              11
So What Should We Do With This?

   Position marketing work as a revenue driver, using these real numbers as proof it pays off.



   Borrow the pricing playbook: clear tiers, a free entry point, and one flagship price.



   Prioritize trust-building content for B2B prospects over broad brand awareness alone.
