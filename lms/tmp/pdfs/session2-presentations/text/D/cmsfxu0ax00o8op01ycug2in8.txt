 FOR THE FOUNDERS IN THIS ROOM


 Five takeaways to build on

                Sell to the practice or provider first — B2B health tools earn ~24x more per company than consumer apps in this data.




                Go narrow. Own one condition, life stage, or persona before you chase “general wellness.”




                Budget for a long runway — about half of health startups here are still under $1,000/month.




                Free trials and freemium open the door, but the winners move users to clear paid tiers quickly.




                If an exit is the goal, invest in retention early — it's what separates a 1x deal from a 4x+ deal.
