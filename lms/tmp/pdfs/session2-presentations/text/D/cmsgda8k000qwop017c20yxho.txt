The moral of the story
      Build for a few, not the crowd — the top 10 out-earn the bottom 900 combined.


      Businesses pay more than consumers, every time this data was cut.


      Followers are a vanity metric. Subscribers and search authority are the real ones.


      Depth beats reach — niche products monetize their visitors better.




Caveat, in the spirit of full disclosure: this is a self-selected sample of founders willing to list publicly, with real gaps in traffic and pricing data. Treat it as one
story TrustMRR tells — not the whole truth about startups everywhere.
