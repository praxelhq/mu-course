What this means for the next build

→   Pick one workflow, not a model

    Own “booking for X” or “outreach for Y” — not “AI for everything.” The fastest SaaS growers here are narrow by design.




→   Let AI be a feature, not the pitch

    Use it under the hood if it helps. Don’t make the category label carry the differentiation — 189 competitors already share it.




→   Price and position like a tool, not a demo

    Simple, visible pricing tied to the workflow it replaces — not “credits” or a novelty wrapper price.
Where this argument is thinner than it sounds

 Self-reported categories

 “AI” and “SaaS” are self-tagged by founders on TrustMRR — not a strict taxonomy, and the two overlap heavily in practice.




 One-month snapshot

 Growth figures are a single 30-day window (frozen 30 Jul 2026), not a trend line. Outliers move the average a lot at this sample size.




 Survivorship in the listing

 This is who chose to list on TrustMRR, not a random sample of all founders building today.
