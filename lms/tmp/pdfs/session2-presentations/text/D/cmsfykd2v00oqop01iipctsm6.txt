About the Data Source




What is TrustMRR?
It's a database where startups connect their real Payment Gateways like Stripe (or
similar) account, so their revenue shows up verified — not just claimed. That's why
investors and buyers use it to check if a founder's numbers are actually true before they
invest or acquire.

We pulled this same verified data and asked it a real question — not to sell a theory, but to see
what it actually says.
