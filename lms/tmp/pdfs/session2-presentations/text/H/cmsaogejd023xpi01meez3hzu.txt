What This Means For Deal Sourcing
Five takeaways from the frozen TrustMRR dataset



 1         Hong Kong is the deepest of the four benchmarked markets — over 2x Poland's 30-day revenue.



 2         Whop-only checkout setups show the highest per-company revenue density among providers compared.



 3         Named, transparent founders out-earn anonymous listings by ~4.6x in all-time revenue.



 4         3-month revenue is a weak predictor of current MRR (r = 0.145) — don't underwrite on it alone.



 5         Games' traffic doesn't convert — diligence monetization, not just audience size, before engaging.
