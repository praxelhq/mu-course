STRATEGIC TAKEAWAYS



Build Boring, Monetize Aggressively
— and don't chase followers.


 1
       Pricing model beats category choice
       Usage-based / credit pricing consistently out-earns subscriptions and freemium, regardless of vertical.


 2
       B2B is still the better business
       Fewer companies, but 58% of all panel MRR — better monetization per company than B2C.


 3
       Domain authority is worth investing in early
       The strongest single correlate of revenue found in the data (r ≈ 0.45 with MRR).


 4
       AI is crowded and commoditizing
       Highest founder activity, highest share on sale (27%), flat median growth — differentiation will decide winners, not category.


 5
       The secondary market is inefficient
       Wide multiple dispersion (22x–70x MRR) signals real opportunity for informed buyers.

Methodology Notes
Small print, but it matters.

       rather than enterprise SaaS.

       traffic-based conclusions as directional.

       Included in aggregate stats; excluded from named-company rankings.


4
       All medians used in preference to means throughout this deck, given the extreme right-skew of revenue and MRR across the panel.
