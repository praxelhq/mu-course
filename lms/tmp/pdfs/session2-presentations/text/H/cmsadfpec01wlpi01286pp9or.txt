14 · CONC LUSION




Thank You
Revenue in this market is concentrated, category hype is a poor proxy for value, and a handful of durable signals — audience
type, payment rail, domain authority — separate the businesses that matter from the listings that don't.


Revenue is winner-take-most — the top 10% of listings capture 82% of total MRR; the bottom 75% share just 3%.

Category hype misleads — AI is the largest cohort by count (n=197) but ranks below Education, Marketing and Analytics on median
revenue.

Signal beats noise — audience type, payment rail and domain authority predict revenue far better than category or social following.

Growth is the exception — only 3 in 10 startups are genuinely growing once small-base noise is excluded.

Health hides in plain sight — both the for-sale shelf and anonymous “stealth” listings skew healthier, not riskier.
