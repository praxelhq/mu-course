Executive Conclusion
• Success is highly concentrated – Most startups generate very little revenue, while a small number create a disproportionate share of value.
• Popularity ≠ Profitability – The most crowded categories are not necessarily the ones generating the highest revenue.
• One metric doesn't tell the whole story – 'For Sale' status or recent revenue alone cannot reliably predict business quality.
• Execution wins – Businesses that consistently convert customers into recurring revenue outperform those simply following market trends.
