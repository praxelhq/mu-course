COMPANY BACKGROUND



The trust layer behind indie SaaS revenue
      Verified, not screenshotted
 =
      Founders connect Stripe, RevenueCat, Paddle, Polar and other processors through read-only API keys, so the revenue shown is pulled live from the
      source — not a screenshot that can be edited.


      Born from build-in-public culture
 +
      It grew out of the indie hacker habit of sharing monthly revenue milestones, where trust in the numbers behind the milestone had become the
      biggest open question.


      A marketplace, not just a leaderboard
 $
      A meaningful share of listed profiles carry an asking price and revenue multiple, turning verified revenue histories into acquisition listings buyers can
      screen directly.


      What this analysis does with it
 >
      We look at where these verified startups actually win — by category, monetization approach, market, and domain strength — to find the patterns
      that hold up.
