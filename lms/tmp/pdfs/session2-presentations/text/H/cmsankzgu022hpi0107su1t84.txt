THE TAKEAWAY




This Is a Power-Law Market, Not an Average One

1     Revenue is concentrated            10 companies (1%) hold 61% of all revenue — the median startup is nearly irrelevant to the total.



2     Category matters                   AI startups out-earn and out-sell the rest of the market, commanding a real, measurable premium.



3     Fewer, larger buyers win           B2B startups earn 5.7x more than B2C peers despite being outnumbered nearly 2 to 1.
