CLOSING

Five things this dataset tells us

 1        Revenue is a power law — a few giants (Stan, Brand On Demand) carry the mean; the typical listing earns a few thousand
          dollars a year.


 2
          AI and the US dominate what's being built and where — but both leaderboards have a long, global tail.



 3
          B2B commands a real revenue premium over B2C, even though B2C startups are more numerous.



 4
          Extreme growth figures on small bases are noise, not signal — filter before benchmarking.



 5
          The for-sale market prices almost entirely off revenue (r = 0.84), at a typical ~2.3x multiple.
