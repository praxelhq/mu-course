Can You Actually Make
Money With a Startup?
We looked inside 1,000 real online businesses — their real, bank-verified income — to see what
actually happens after someone launches a startup. This is what the numbers say.




    In plain terms: “TrustMRR” is a website where small startup founders connect their real payment account (like Stripe) to publicly prove
    exactly how much money their business makes — no exaggeration allowed. We studied 1,000 of these public, verified profiles.

THE RECAP


13 Questions, 13 Answers
1     Most startups earn very little — median under $400/month — while a handful earn millions.


2     AI is the most-attempted category (1 in 5 startups), but building in it doesn't guarantee revenue.


3     Stripe powers 64% of startups, but higher-median-revenue founders often use niche gateways like Superwall.


4     Among founders who disclose it, 7 days is the standard trial before charging customers.


5     Real paying customers predict revenue — social media followers barely do (0.02 vs 0.80 correlation).


6     B2B founders earn 4x more than B2C founders — and growth is a coin flip (34% shrinking vs 35% growing).


7     The data itself is imperfect: 13% of founders stay anonymous, and averages can mislead — always check the median.
