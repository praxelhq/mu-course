PART 3 — THE “WHY”


Who they sell to explains the gap: B2B vs B2C
Of the top 8 revenue earners, 6 sell to businesses (B2B) and only 2 sell to individual consumers (B2C) — yet the B2C outlier, Stan, is still the single
biggest earner because of massive scale.
