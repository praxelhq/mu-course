AI is the largest category by count: 197 startups (≈1 in 5 of the 1,000).

among startups with category data).

Headline: lots of founders, small share of revenue — popularity ≠
payoff.

Location: Where the Revenue Actually Lives
                  U.S. — 258 startups (25.8%); $7.5M MRR (55.7% of total). Dominant in
                  both count and revenue concentration.

                  France — 79 startups (7.9%); $894K MRR (6.6%). Efficient second hub.

                  India — 59 startups (5.9%); $157K MRR (1.2%). High volume, low
                  revenue per founder.

                  UK — 50 startups (5.0%); $350K MRR (2.6%). Solid mid-level revenue
                  performance.

                  Canada — 29 startups; $366K MRR (2.7%). Small population, strong
                  revenue-per-founder.
