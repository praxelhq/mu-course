 1     The market is a power law                                                                                                                      SLIDE 6


       Roughly half of listings have no revenue; the top decile carries the overwhelming majority. Listing counts describe enthusiasm, not markets.



 2     The headline multiple annualises one month                                                                                                     SLIDE 16

 3
       Recurring revenue determines transferable value                                                                                                SLIDE 10

 4     Traffic does not equal revenue                                                                                                                 SLIDE 14

 5     Domain authority predicts revenue but not price                                                                                                SLIDE 18


       The one genuinely non-replicable asset in a micro-business is not being paid for.
