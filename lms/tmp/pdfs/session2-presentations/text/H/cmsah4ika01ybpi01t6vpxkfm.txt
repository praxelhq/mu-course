  Key takeaways

     AI, E-commerce and Marketing generate the most revenue overall — but B2B tools monetise far more efficiently per company than B2C.


     A small number of breakout startups account for the overwhelming majority of tracked revenue; the median company earns very little.


     Among domains with a reliable sample size, .ai carries the highest authority and revenue-per-company, while .com still dominates by volume.
