SO WHAT



What this actually means if you're building
              Distribution ≠ revenue                     X/Twitter followers barely correlate with monthly revenue (r ≈ 0.02) across all 1,000 profiles.
 1                                                       Build monetization, not just an audience.



              B2B pays better, on average                Same founder effort, roughly 4x the revenue per company. Consumer apps are more crowded for
 2                                                       a smaller payoff.



              Infra is a tell, not just a tool           Founders using dedicated monetization tooling (Superwall, Whop) sit in visibly higher-revenue
 3                                                       cohorts than plain payment API users.



              Verified ≠ vetted                          A live payment connection proves the number. It doesn't prove the price, the multiple, or the
 4                                                       business is sound — check the math yourself.



              Growth is the exception                    A third of “featured” startups are shrinking right now. Benchmark your own progress against
 5                                                       reality, not the leaderboard.
