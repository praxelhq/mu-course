Key Takeaways from TrustMRR Data

1. Category Matters: AI = 4.7x higher RPV than average

2. B2B > B2C: 2.5x faster growth, better unit economics

3. Geography: USA leads with $29K average MRR

4. Multi-Provider Works: +75% revenue vs single provider

5. Conversion: B2C = 60%, B2B = 15%, "Both" = 1.5% (pick one!)

6. Monetization Gap: High traffic regions under-monetized

7. Time to Scale: 5K-10K visitors = $10K MRR benchmark

8. B2B: Fewer customers, high value | B2C: Volume game

9. Best Market: USA = $29K MRR with 258 startups
Conclusions & Recommendations

For Founders:

    • Target B2B or AI - both show superior growth

    • Focus USA/Canada for revenue; India/Poland for scale

    • Get to 5K monthly visitors before advanced optimizations

    • Use Stripe + Superwall combo (75% more revenue)

For Investors:

    • B2B startups show 93% growth - target these

    • Watch for 5K-10K visitor milestone = $10K MRR signal

    • Category > Geography (AI > all other categories)

    • Track conversion rate > followers (PMF indicator)
