   CONCLUSIO N




   Monetization is a function of buyer type and
   purchase intent — not audience scale.

   Categories sold to buyers with a defined budget line — Analytics, Sales, Marketing, Education — monetize near-universally and at materially
   higher price points. Consumer- and audience-driven categories frequently show traffic or followings that fail to convert, and the incidence of
   this pattern (67% of large-audience companies flat or declining) is high enough to indicate a structural relationship rather than isolated cases.


   L IM ITATION S & S U GGES TED FU RTH ER AN AL YS IS

   •       Do the 29 high-follower, zero-revenue companies lack a paid tier entirely, or is this a pricing/positioning gap?
   •       For the 15 companies exhibiting revenue attrition, does the decline correlate with a pricing, platform, or payment -provider change?
   •       Is AI's low per-company MRR a category effect, or a cohort-age effect (newer listings, less time to convert)?
