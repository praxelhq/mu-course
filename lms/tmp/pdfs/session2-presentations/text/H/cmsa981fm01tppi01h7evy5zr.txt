THE TAKEAWAY



Real Revenue Is Rare, Concentrated,
and Unevenly Told
     Verified revenue is still the exception, not the norm — most listed startups earn under $300/month, and a tiny few carry the whole
     leaderboard.


     Geography and category are lopsided: the US and AI dominate volume, but neither guarantees the highest revenue per company.


     Fewer, better-paying B2B customers consistently outperform larger B2C audiences at the median.


     TrustMRR isn't just a leaderboard — with 262 companies for sale, it's also a live, transparent micro-M&A market.


     Some of the strongest performers choose to stay anonymous — transparency and visibility aren't the same thing.
