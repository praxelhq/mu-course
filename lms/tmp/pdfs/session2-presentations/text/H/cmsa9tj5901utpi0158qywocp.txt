Analysis of ~700 self-reported indie & bootstrapped startups (founders
list revenue, MRR, growth, subscribers, and asking price). Concise, data-
forward insights for investors, analysts, and founders.
What is TrustMRR?
                    TrustMRR is a public, self-reported registry where indie founders
                    list business metrics and (often) asking prices. The dataset used
                    here contains ~700 entries with fields including:

                       Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) / reported revenue
                       Growth % (monthly or annual when provided)
                       Subscriber / user counts
                       Payment processor and geography
                       Asking price or sale intent

                    Population skews toward solo founders and small teams — high
                    signal for bootstrapped marketplace dynamics.

The Long Tail of Revenue
Revenue is strongly power-law distributed. A handful of outliers
(examples: Stan≈ $2.8M/mo, Comp AI ≈ $575K/30 days, Postiz ≈
$177K/mo, Brand On Demand/Supliful ≈ $51M all-time) sit far above a long
tail of listings reporting $0–a few hundred dollars/month. Many rows
show 0 MRR or early-stage experiments.


      Implication: median metrics understate the economic impact of
      top outliers — treat mean and median separately and inspect
      tails.

The "For Sale" Market
Many founders list an asking price and revenue multiple. Observed
patterns:

   Typical indie SaaS multiples: ~1x–5x annual revenue for pragmatic
   listings
   Occasional extreme asks: 100x+ — often pricing on potential rather
   than trailing metrics
   Zero or 0x asks: fire sales, strategic transfers, or symbolic listings


      Valuation divergence reflects heterogeneous goals — hobby
      sellers, growth-seekers, and speculators coexist in the market.
