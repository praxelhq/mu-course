THE PLATFORM


What TrustMRR actually does
Launched by indie founder Marc Lou in late 2025, TrustMRR is a public database of software startups that connect a read-only API key from their
payment provider to prove real revenue — replacing the era of screenshotted, unverifiable “MRR flexes.”

TAKEAWAYS


What the numbers say

1           Verification concentrates around one rail.

            Stripe underwrites 87% of tracked revenue — “verified” today largely means “Stripe-verified.”



2           AI is the platform's center of gravity.

            The single largest category by both startup count and revenue, well ahead of Marketing and E-comm.



3           B2B is smaller but richer.

            A quarter of the base, yet median MRR nearly 6x that of B2C listings.



4           The leaderboard is becoming a marketplace.

            26% of startups are actively for sale at a median 2.3x revenue multiple.
