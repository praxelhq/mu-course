Strategic Recommendations
01

Prioritize SaaS & Subscription Models
Highest MRR stability, strongest multiples (11x ARR), and lowest churn risk make this
the highest-conviction category for capital deployment.


02

Target the $50K–$200K MRR Tier
This segment offers the best risk-adjusted entry point — proven traction, room to
scale, and 23% of total MRR with only 20% of companies.


03

Watch APAC for Early-Stage Bets
Fastest growth trajectory and lower entry valuations create asymmetric upside for
patient capital willing to wait 3–5 years.


04

Due Diligence on 14% "On Sale" Cohort
Active acquisition targets may represent distressed assets or strategic exits — requires
careful vetting but could yield quick-win portfolio additions.
