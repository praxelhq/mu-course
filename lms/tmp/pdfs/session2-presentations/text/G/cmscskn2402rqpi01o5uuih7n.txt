What Actually Predicts Value
Three signals that separate real revenue from vanity metrics



                      B2B commands a real premium
    4.5x
                      B2B startups post 4.5x higher median MRR than B2C ($1,639 vs $366, among companies with active subscribers) — and 4.4x more
                      revenue per subscriber ($47 vs $11). “Both” audiences land in between at $693 median MRR.



                      SEO authority beats social buzz
    0.51
                      Domain rating correlates 0.51 with asking price and 0.48 with MRR. X follower count correlates just 0.18 with revenue — a near-
                      vanity metric for gauging a business's real worth.



                      “Free” isn’t a growth engine here
      5x
                      Startups without a stated free tier show ~5x higher median MRR ($751) than those advertising one ($159) — freemium often
                      signals early-stage, pre-monetization products in this dataset.
