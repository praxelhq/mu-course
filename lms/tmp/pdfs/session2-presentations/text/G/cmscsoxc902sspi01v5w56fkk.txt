If You're Picking a Category
     Don't chase the crowd.
01   AI has the most companies and near-bottom typical revenue. Popular ≠ profitable.


     Explore Education, Content Creation, Marketing.
02   Higher typical revenue than AI, with far fewer competitors fighting for the same customers.


     Marketing may be the best all-rounder.
03   $54 per customer, 42 companies to learn from, and half of them are still growing.


     Copy the category's proven setup.
04   Match the norm — Stripe + B2B for Marketing/Sales, Stripe + B2C for Education.


     Judge companies by recurring revenue, not hype.
05   MRR and subscriber count show real size. A big growth % or follower count does not.
