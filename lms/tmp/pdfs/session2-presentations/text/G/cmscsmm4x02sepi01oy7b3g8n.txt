Where We Recommend Investing
Ranked by active-subscriber retention, validated by revenue scale and growth



                        AI — Scale AND stickiness

         1              46,160 active subscribers — the largest retained base of any category — sits on top of $1.81M in total MRR and 16.7% average MRR growth across 197
                        startups. This is the rare case where volume, retention, and momentum all agree.




                        Health & Fitness — Highest retention efficiency

         2              Just 34 startups generate 41,262 active subscribers — more retained users per company than any other category. Smaller revenue base today, but the
                        subscriber density signals strong product-market fit worth backing early.




                        Mobile Apps — Broadest reach, room to monetize

         3              24,752 active subscribers across 52 startups gives the widest distribution of the three. Revenue per subscriber trails AI and Health & Fitness today — an
                        upside lever rather than a red flag.



Recommendation: lead with AI for scale and near-term returns; allocate a smaller, earlier-stage stake to Health & Fitness for retention upside.
