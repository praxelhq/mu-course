Methodology & Data Notes

Dataset                                      TrustMRR Full Dataset, frozen 30 July 2026. 8,782 startup records across 29 fields including MRR, growth, category, geography, and sale status.




Filters applied                              "Revenue-generating" = mrr_usd > 0 (n = 3,757). Category and country cuts additionally require count > 20 listings to avoid small-sample noise.




                                             Parsed from the platform's listed "revenue_multiple" field (e.g., "2.1x" → 2.1); reflects asking-price-to-annualized-revenue ratio as listed by sellers,
Revenue multiple
                                             not closed transaction data.



                                             Figures reflect self-reported, platform-listed data at a single point in time; asking prices are not verified closed sale prices. ~57% of records report
Limitations                                  zero or unreported MRR and are excluded from revenue-based cuts. Category/audience fields are missing for some of the highest-MRR outliers
                                             (e.g., "Stan"), which are noted but not force-classified.




Undervalued screen (Slide 9)                 Growth > 5%/month AND multiple < 3.0x (sample median) AND MRR > $500, restricted to active for-sale listings (n = 995 eligible, 38 matched).
