Section 9 · Verdict                                                                                                 TrustMRR · 1,000-company snapshot, frozen 30 Jul 2026
Read these findings with a few limits in mind

 1        30 days is a snapshot, not a trend

          Growth figures reflect one month. A single strong or weak month can swing a small category's numbers.



 2        Category is unlabeled for 274 companies

          Over a quarter of the dataset has no category tag and isn't reflected in the comparisons above.



 3        Smaller categories and countries carry wider margins

          Groups like Fintech, Social Media, or Australia rest on 15–20 companies — a few outliers move the median.



 4        Self-reported revenue fields don't always reconcile

          12-month revenue is occasionally lower than the monthly rate implies, and subscriber counts may include free tiers.
