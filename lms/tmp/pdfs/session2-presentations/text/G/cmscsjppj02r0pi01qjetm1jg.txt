Putting It Together

 Viable categories     AI, SaaS, Mobile Apps lead on scale and sample depth




 Quickest to revenue   Marketing & Sales need fewer customers to monetize ($93 / $82 ARPU)




 Segment economics     B2B monetizes ~5.7x better per company than B2C




 Exit market           26% of listings are for sale; Education & Marketing command top multiples




 Momentum              Recruiting & HR and Dev Tools are the only categories with real growth
