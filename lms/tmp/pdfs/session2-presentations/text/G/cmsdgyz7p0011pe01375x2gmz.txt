Why this deserves a first call


•     Analytics is a category with above-median revenue quality (category median MRR $1,294) — this listing is well above that bar.

•     At 1.3x monthly MRR (vs. a 38x market median), the price implies the seller either has an undisclosed problem or is simply mispricing a real asset — both

Diligence focus: churn drivers behind the mild decline, customer concentration, and reason for sale.

I NVESTMENT THESI S



This is a micro-PE roll-up opportunity, not a venture-style bet


     NOT THIS: venture-style growth hunting                                  THIS: disciplined cash-flow acquisition




                                                                             •   Acquiring small, profitable, already-operating businesses at a
                                                                                 discount to fundamentals
     •     Betting on a handful of outliers reaching massive scale
                                                                             •   Screening on verified subscriber counts and processor data, not
     •     Underwriting pre-revenue potential over verified cash flow
                                                                                 vanity metrics
     •     Chasing category hype (e.g. AI) for narrative, not fundamentals
                                                                             •   Prioritizing categories with proven revenue density (Customer
                                                                                 Support, Marketplace, Education) over hyped, crowded ones

Bottom line


TrustMRR is a viable sourcing funnel — but only with a strict,
fundamentals-first screen. Buying at asking price without adjusting for
growth and revenue quality would be value-destructive in most cases.
