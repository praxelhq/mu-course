Key takeaways
 1         Revenue is winner-take-most: a handful of named startups (Stan, Brand On Demand, Stack Influence) anchor the top of every revenue window,
           30-day through 12-month.


 2
           Category size and category earnings are different stories — AI has the most startups (197) but Education and Marketing carry higher median MRR.


 3
           Geography is lopsided: the US alone holds roughly three-quarters of all tracked MRR despite being a quarter of the listings.


 4
           B2B is the smaller but stronger segment — fewer startups (255 vs. 401 B2C), yet a 5.7x higher median MRR.


 5         Freemium is the default motion (53% of stated pricing models), and positioning language is dominated by AI-powered automation and content-
           generation framing.


 6         Treat this as a self-reported, partially-populated leaderboard, not a census — roughly a third of positioning fields are blank, and 274 rows are
           unclassified or intentionally anonymized.
