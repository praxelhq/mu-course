CONCLUSION
The micro-startup economy is a power law world - and the levers that separate winners from the long tail are knowable.



 1         The top 1% of startups hold 48% of all MRR. This is a winner-takes-most ecosystem, not a democratic one.



 2         B2B beats B2C by 5.7× at the median. The customer segment matters more than the product category.



 3         59% of startups haven't crossed $1K MRR. The hardest step is first - but growth compresses sharply once scale begins.



 4         26% of startups are for sale, with accessible price points below $35K median. Micro-M&A is a real and underserved market.



 5         US, B2B, Education, and Marketing positioning are the highest-signal factors for durable revenue - more than AI labeling.
