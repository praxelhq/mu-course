T H E TA K E


Don’t underwrite the category. Underwrite the
company.

01                 Category size is a crowding signal, not a quality signal.

                   AI holds 19.7% of startups but only 13.4% of revenue — the label alone tells you little about the business.




02                 Check concentration before you check the pitch.

                   The top 5% of AI startups here earn two-thirds of the category’s revenue — ask where a target really sits in that curve.




03                 Score traction, not audience.

                   Follower count only weakly tracks revenue (r = 0.30) — diligence on actual MRR beats diligence on social proof.
