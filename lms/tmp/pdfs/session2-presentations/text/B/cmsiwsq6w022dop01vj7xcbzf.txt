WHAT TO DO WITH THIS


Three conclusions, one caveat

 1        Stop underwriting the AI label
          It describes 54% of the field and captures 24% of the revenue. It predicts nothing.



 2        Underwrite pricing structure instead
          B2B per-subscriber revenue runs 5.2x B2C — the largest and best-evidenced gap in the dataset.



 3        Recompute every published multiple
          The listed figure ranks deals at 0.59 against TTM. Never compare two listings on it.




     THE CAVEAT


     Self-listed, Stripe-skewed sample. This reads the micro-SaaS long tail, not the AI market. Venture-scale companies do not list here.
