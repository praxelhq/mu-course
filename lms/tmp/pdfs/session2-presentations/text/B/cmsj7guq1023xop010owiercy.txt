INVESTMENT THESIS




Followers measure attention.
Fund monetization.

Across 1,000 startups, social reach showed a near-zero raw correlation with revenue and no
relationship with growth rate. Diligence should weight MRR, growth, retention, and pricing power —
not audience size.
