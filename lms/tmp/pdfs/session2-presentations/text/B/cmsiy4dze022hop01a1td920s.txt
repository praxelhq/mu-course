                          Build Surgical Tools, Not General Platforms



1. Direct Financial ROI: Software that recovers lost revenue, automates compliance, or
plugs data gaps sells effortlessly.

2. Disregard Vanity Metrics: Audience size has negligible impact on B2B retention.
Prioritize workflow utility over viral growth.

3. Rapid Iteration with AI: Keep build cycles under 4 weeks to maintain velocity and
validate willingness-to-pay early.
