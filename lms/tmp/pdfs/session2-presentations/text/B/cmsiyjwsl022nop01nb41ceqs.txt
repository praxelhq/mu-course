How We Built the Lie Detector
Delusion Score = Ask Percentile − Growth Percentile

For every startup listed for sale, we rank it two ways: how aggressive its asking multiple is versus every other listing, and how much its revenue and MRR actually
grew last month. Subtract the growth rank from the ask rank. Positive means they're asking for more than their growth backs up — negative means the business
is quietly underpriced.


  Across the whole marketplace, asking price and growth are barely related — correlation of just −0.14. Sellers are pricing on vibes, not fundamentals.
