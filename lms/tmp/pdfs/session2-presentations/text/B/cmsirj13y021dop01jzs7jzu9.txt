THE INVESTMENT THESIS




Price on this market reflects seller psychology—not
business quality.

 1        Screen by momentum, not by sticker multiple.

          The listed price tells a buyer almost nothing about what they're actually getting.


 2        Underwrite the fundamentals directly.

          Revenue trend, retention, and channel concentration—not the seller's asking price—set the real value.


 3        The edge is discipline, not access.

          Every number here is public. Almost no one is reading it this way.



Buy the signal. Ignore the price tag.
