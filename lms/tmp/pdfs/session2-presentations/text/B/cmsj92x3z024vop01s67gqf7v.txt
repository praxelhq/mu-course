T H E P L AY B O O K



What this means for a buyer

          Screen by revenue, not by tag
  1
          Set a hard revenue floor before you ever filter by category. A label is not a cash-flow guarantee.




          Price against the peer, not the label
  2
          SaaS listings show 46% more trailing revenue than AI listings, yet trade at under half the multiple (1.2x vs 3.15x). Check what a non-AI peer
          would cost first.




          Hunt where the label doesn't help
  3
          Education, Marketing and Content Creation out-earn AI per startup, often at a comparable or lower multiple. Boring beats buzzy on the ledger.
