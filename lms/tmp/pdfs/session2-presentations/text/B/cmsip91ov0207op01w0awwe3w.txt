    The verdict
    Three working theories on why silence pays:


               They're already big.
    01
               Real, established businesses being sold quietly — through brokers or holding companies — don't need a public bio. Anonymity is a
               privacy choice made possible by scale.


               They're protecting a moat.
    02
               High-revenue, low-competition products often hide their playbook on purpose — a visible name invites copycats faster than an
               invisible one.


               It's a listings artifact.
    03
               M&A platforms often anonymize the highest-value deals until a buyer signs an NDA — so “Stealth Company” may simply mark the
               listings worth protecting.
