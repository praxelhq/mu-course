 FOUNDER PLAYBOOK



The Founder Playbook: How to Build a $1M MRR AI Startup
Data from 1,000 AI startups points to five decisions that separate the top 5% from the rest.


          Pick a Vertical
          Finance, legal, healthcare, enterprise sales. Vertical AI earns 3–5x more than horizontal tools.



          Build a Team
          Co-founders with sales skills matter. 4+ person teams earn 19x more than solo founders.



          Target Enterprise
          One $10K/month customer beats 1,000 consumer users in LTV, churn, and expansion.



          Build a Moat
          Use proprietary data, domain expertise, or lock-in, not an API wrapper.



          Prioritize Distribution
          Split effort between product and go-to-market. Partnerships can outperform organic by 10x.



       $1M MRR comes from vertical + enterprise + team + moat + distribution.
