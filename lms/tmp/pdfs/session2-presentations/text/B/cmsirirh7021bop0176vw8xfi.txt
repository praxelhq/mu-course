09 — Strategic Recommendations: If You're Building
Five data-backed moves to optimize your bootstrapped or micro-SaaS venture for success and maximize monetization potential.


         Prioritize B2B Value
         B2B customers offer 5x higher median MRR per account. Direct your efforts towards business clients for substantially greater financial returns.



         Freemium as Baseline
         With 52.7% of companies already offering a free tier, freemium is a market expectation, not a differentiator. Focus on conversion strategies from free to paid.



         Invest in Search & Content
         Domain Rating has a 0.48 correlation with revenue. Start building organic search authority early; the average DR of 16.7/100 indicates a significant untapped
         opportunity.



         Don't Overvalue Social Reach
         Social media following has a negligible 0.02 correlation with MRR. While community is valuable, raw audience size rarely translates directly to financial success.



         Scrutinize Category Monetization
         Some categories, like Marketplace/Games, show 40%+ zero-revenue companies. Contrast this with Analytics/Sales, which have much stronger monetization profiles.
         Choose wisely.
