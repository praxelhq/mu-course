Before the money moves



Four questions this dataset
cannot answer.

     1 Monthly churn                                Every growth figure here is a single 30-day reading. A good month and a compounding business are not the same claim.
                                                    Ask for twelve months of cohort retention.




     2 Cost to acquire                              82% of the dataset never disclosed traffic, all four picks included. Without CAC there is no payback period and no
                                                    defensible entry price.




     3 Revenue concentration                        Cometly at 307 customers and Apiframe at 425 could each carry a client worth a fifth of revenue. Ask for the top-ten
                                                    customer table.




     4 Billing reconciliation                       GojiberryAI's MRR runs 28% ahead of collected cash. Either annual contracts booked early, or a refund problem. Resolve
                                                    before funding.


Four of a thousand. If only one question can be asked, ask for churn.
