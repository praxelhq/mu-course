WHERE THE MISPRICING SITS


Two traps and one window.
Implied multiples by deal size are not a smooth curve. The sub-$10k tier is the most expensive in the market — those listings ask 6.3x against a
median of $263 of annual revenue. Above $1M, competitive tension from professional buyers pushes multiples back up to 6.1x. The band between
them is where price discipline is still available.
