    P LA YB O O K



Five moves every idea here needs in India
   1               Price in ₹, think in ₹99
                                                                Anchor entry tiers under ₹500/month or credit-packs under ₹100 — Indian buyers convert on micro-commitments, not annual
                                                                contracts.




   2               UPI & wallets, not just cards                Route payments through Razorpay/Cashfree with UPI + wallets as default — card-only checkout quietly kills conversion.




   3               WhatsApp is the interface                    Notifications, onboarding, even support should live on WhatsApp — it out-performs email and in-app inboxes here.




   4               Vernacular from day one                      Hindi + one regional language (Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Bengali) in UI and support unlocks tier-2/3 India cheaply.




   5               Trust before scale
                                                                GST invoicing, RBI-compliant payments, and visible local support build the trust that a foreign brand name doesn't buy
                                                                automatically.

      R E A LI T Y C H EC K



What could go wrong

                   Data reflects global usage, not Indian demand
        !
                   All revenue proof comes from non-Indian traction (mostly US/EU). India fit here is directional judgment, not a validated local metric.




                   IP & non-compete constraints
        !
                   These are existing, branded businesses — treat this as market-inspiration and category validation, not a blueprint to clone verbatim.




                   Price-down markets compress margins
        !
                   Winning on ₹-pricing means thinner unit economics — volume and low CAC (WhatsApp, community) have to do the work margin can't.




                   Payment & compliance overhead
        !
                   GST invoicing, RBI data-localization, and DPDP Act compliance add real early-stage engineering and legal cost — budget for it upfront.
