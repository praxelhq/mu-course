SURPRISING FINDINGS


Businesses for sale are stronger, not weaker, than the market
   The secondary market is behaving counterintuitively — sellers skew toward proven, higher-revenue operators rather
   than distressed side projects.

S T R AT E G I C R E C O M M E N D AT I O N S


Underwrite execution, not category exposure
    The sourcing and diligence playbook below converts this analysis into concrete partner-level actions, prioritized top to
    bottom.

                                                          1. Prioritize B2B, workflow-embedded AI
                                                          over consumer content-gen AI, where crowding risk is highest


                                               2. Weight organic authority & conversion efficiency
                                               over social proof — followers are a weak predictor here


                                     3. Favor subscription-first monetization
                                     over one-time or lifetime pricing, which is less durable


                          4. Underwrite durability separately from momentum
                          before paying an AI premium on any acquisition target


                5. Mine the secondary market for proven operators
                treat for-sale listings as curated deal flow, not distress




              W H Y T H IS M AT T ERS       this is the operating checklist the fund should apply to every AI-adjacent opportunity that crosses the desk this cycle.
