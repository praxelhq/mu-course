BOTTOM LINE




Chase B2B-heavy growth,
priced fairly.
Marketing, Analytics, and Dev Tools combine the growth, stickiness, and entry pricing that a disciplined allocator
wants. Treat AI as a volume market that needs per-deal selectivity, and steer around sectors showing contraction or
oversupply — Content Creation and Mobile Apps chief among them.
