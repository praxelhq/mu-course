K E Y TA K E A W AY S

What the numbers are telling us

           Power law, not bell curve.
           Half of all revenue sits with just 10 companies out of 1,000 — the median startup earns $282 in MRR.




           AI is the default category now.
           More than 1 in 4 categorized startups build with AI at the core — 3× the next-largest category.




           The US still writes the biggest checks.
           56% of tracked MRR is American, even though founders are shipping from 65 countries.




           A quarter of the map is for sale.
           262 startups are listed at a median 2.3× revenue — real acquisition comps, not theory.
