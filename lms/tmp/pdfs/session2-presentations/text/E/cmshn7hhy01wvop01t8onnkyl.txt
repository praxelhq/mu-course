RECOMMENDATION



Conditional yes — structure the deal around the risk, not the headline


1    Request 3 months of raw Stripe cohort data

     Verify the 253% MoM figure isn't a single spike; check month-2 retention of month-1 cohorts specifically.



2    Negotiate an earn-out structure

     e.g. $300K upfront + $200K contingent on 6-month MRR retention above a set floor — shifts durability risk back to the seller.



3    Independent founder reference check

     1 review is not enough signal — talk to past collaborators or customers directly before wiring funds.



4    Budget for a paid-acquisition dependency

     Plan post-acquisition spend assuming growth is largely paid/creator-driven, not organic, until proven otherwise.
