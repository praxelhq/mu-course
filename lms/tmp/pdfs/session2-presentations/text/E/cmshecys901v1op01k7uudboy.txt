WHAT THIS MEANS FOR INVESTORS


Five Theses This Data Supports

01               Monetization structure beats audience size.          B2B out-earns B2C 5–6× in this cohort. Audience size is a vanity metric; pricing and buyer
                                                                      authority are the real value drivers.




02               SEO compounding beats social following.              Domain authority predicts revenue nearly 2× more strongly than follower count — diligence
                                                                      should weight search, not social.




03               AI is a stock-picker's category.                     Highest variance of any vertical in the dataset — a field for name-by-name diligence, not a
                                                                      blanket sector allocation.




04               Visibility and execution are inversely correlated.   The strongest operators in this dataset are frequently anonymous. Public profile is not a quality
                                                                      signal here.




05               The secondary market is inefficiently priced.        A 0.1–76× ARR multiple spread on one marketplace is a standing arbitrage for disciplined, cash-
                                                                      flow-first buyers.
