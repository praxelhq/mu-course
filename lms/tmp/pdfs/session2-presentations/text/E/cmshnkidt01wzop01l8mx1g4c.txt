Before you pay for a tool, know if it's actually real
As a marketer, you get pitched a new AI tool every week. Slick landing page, big promises, but no way to tell if it's a real business or two people and a
demo. That's the gap this data fills.

TrustMRR connects to each tool's payment processor (like Stripe) and reads their MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue) directly. It's the same number the
founder sees on their own dashboard, not a number from their marketing page.

Three things worth remembering

   1          “Powered by AI” isn't special anymore

   2          Small doesn't mean bad — but check first

              Plenty of great tools are still small. Just make sure they're growing before you depend on one.
