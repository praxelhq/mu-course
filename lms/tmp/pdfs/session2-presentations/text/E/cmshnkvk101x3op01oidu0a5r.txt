What Is TrustMRR?
                                                                    1   A verified revenue database. Every MRR and revenue figure listed is authenticated directly
                                                                        through a connected payment provider (Stripe, RevenueCat, Polar, Lemon Squeezy and others)
                                                                        — not a self-reported screenshot.




                                                                    2   An acquisition marketplace. Founders list their startups to prove traction publicly; investors
                  Verified Revenue                                      and acquirers browse the same listings to find profitable, revenue -verified startups that are
                                                                        actively for sale.
