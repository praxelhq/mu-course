Valuation Multiples:
Handle with Care
Median Multiple: 2.9×
The central tendency — most defensible anchor for valuation
benchmarking. Use this as the baseline before any adjustments.



Mean Multiple: 8.82× — Badly Skewed
Inflated by extreme outliers in the 60×–327× range. These are near-
zero-MRR listings where any positive MRR creates an astronomical
multiple. Noise, not signal.



Trimmed Mean (Top 5% Removed): 4.15×
Cleaner, but still inflated by small-MRR listings in the dataset.
Recommend applying an MRR floor before using any multiple-based
valuation — see next card.
