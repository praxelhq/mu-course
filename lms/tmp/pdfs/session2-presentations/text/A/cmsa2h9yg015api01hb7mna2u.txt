EXECUTIVE SUMMARY


Five data-backed insights
        1. Revenue follows a power law.
        The top 10% of businesses capture 84% of all revenue in the dataset.


        2. Growth is concentrated among very few companies.
        1 in 3 businesses shrank last month; a handful drove nearly all reported growth.


        3. Category leadership does not guarantee performance.
        AI leads in company count (197) but ranks 6th of 13 categories by average revenue.


        4. Monetization efficiency matters more than traffic.
        Traffic explains only a fraction of revenue variance; revenue-per-visitor is the real driver.


        5. Top performers win broadly, not narrowly.
        Top-quartile businesses out-perform on revenue, subscribers, and monetization at once.
