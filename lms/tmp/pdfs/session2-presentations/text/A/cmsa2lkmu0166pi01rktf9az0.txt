What the Data Says
Six patterns from 1,000 startups worth factoring into your next move


01      AI is crowded, not profitable per startup
        197 listings, $446 median MRR — highest volume, mid-tier earnings

02      B2B earns 5.7x more per startup than B2C
        Median MRR: $1,036 (B2B) vs $181 (B2C) — fewer users, more revenue

03      Customer Support, Education, Marketing, Analytics lead on MRR
        Median MRR $1.3k–$5.2k — less competition than AI or SaaS

04      57% of startups are flat or declining
        Growth is the exception — filter for positive 30-day momentum

05      262 startups are for sale at 2.8x median multiple
        Median asking price $35k — buying revenue is a viable path

06      Stripe processes 87% of all MRR on the platform
        636 listings, $11.7M total MRR — the dominant payment rail
