   THE INFERENCE




   Revenue in this market is a volume problem
   wearing a pricing problem's clothes.


                                                                  MRR = subscribers × $20

   Median ARPU is $19.50 and it does not move. Across a 650× range of company size, from $29 MRR to $18,854 MRR, the typical customer pays the
   same twenty dollars a month. The entire variance in outcome is explained by how many customers a company reaches — not by what it charges
   them.

Nobody in this market is winning
because they charged more.
