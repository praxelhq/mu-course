Narrative spine: TrustMRR exists to make indie-hacker revenue credible.
Read all 1,000 verified rows at once, and the most credible thing the
platform proves is that the median builder never makes it. The deck earns
the right to that claim slide by slide, then closes with the only defensible
way to play.

Dataset: 1,000 startups, TrustMRR, 30 July 2026. Revenue is API-verified
through connected payment providers, not self-reported.

The rules the data supports:
       Sell to businesses, or to consumers you can reach through search



       Charge from day one — no free tier



       Stripe by default; RevenueCat only for mobile



       Invest in domain authority before audience



       Set a 12-month clock, not a 90-day one



       Kill rule: domain rating under 10 and MRR under $250 by month nine



   "TrustMRR set out to prove that indie revenue is real. It succeeded. What it also proved, without meaning to, is how rarely it is enough — and that is the more useful finding."
