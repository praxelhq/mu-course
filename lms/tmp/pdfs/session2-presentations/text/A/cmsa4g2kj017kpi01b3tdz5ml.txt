10 · CONCLUSION




The Market Rewards Business Model
and Credibility — Not Buzz

Recurring revenue here behaves like a barbell. A small set of durable, well-indexed B2B businesses holds most of the real value, while a
long tail of AI-labeled, early-stage products generates attention without cash flow. The headline growth rates and deal multiples
amplify that illusion, and a meaningful slice of the market's true value stays invisible until someone asks for it directly.
