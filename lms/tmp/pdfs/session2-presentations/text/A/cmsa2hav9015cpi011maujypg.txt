 B2B Charges More — Even With Fewer Customers

      B2B                                                           B2C                                                          Both
      255 startups                                                  401 startups                                                 56 startups


      $1,036                                                        $181                                                         $352
      median MRR                                                    median MRR                                                   median MRR




        What it means

        B2B startups earn roughly 5.7x the median MRR of B2C peers, despite representing far fewer listings (255 vs 401). This tracks with classic SaaS economics — B2B
        customers tolerate higher price points and lower churn.


        On the rails side, Stripe powers 64% of listings and carries the deepest revenue base ($11.8M of $13.5M total MRR), while RevenueCat (19%) dominates mobile-app
        subscription billing — a clean signal that most of this dataset is web-first SaaS, with a distinct mobile-subscription cluster layered on top.

 RECAP


 Five Things This Dataset Tells Us

        1           It's a long tail, not a bell curve             20% of startups earn $0 MRR; just 1.6% clear $100K. Always lead with the median.




                                                                   Highest listing count, only mid-pack median MRR — Education & Marketing monetize better
        2           AI is crowded, not necessarily lucrative       per listing.




        3           B2B out-earns B2C by ~5.7x                     Fewer B2B listings, but sharply higher median MRR — pricing power beats volume.




        4           Conversion beats reach                         Active subscriptions & revenue-per-visitor predict MRR far better than followers or raw traffic.




        5           The US anchors the money, not just the count   24% of listings, ~56% of total MRR — geography concentrates value even more than volume.
