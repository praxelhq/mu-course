E X E C UTIV E S U M M AR Y


Being findable predicts revenue; having an audience does not

               Building is no longer the constraint
 01
               AI tooling has collapsed the cost of shipping software. The dataset's bottleneck is monetisation, not construction: median mo nthly revenue across 1,000 verified
               startups is $282.


               Domain authority tracks revenue at every level
 02
               Median MRR climbs monotonically across domain-authority bands — $9 to $23,536 — and the top band holds 4% of companies but 63% of all 30-day revenue.




               Audience size does not separate the cohorts
 03
               31.6% of founders earning $10K+/month have zero X followers, versus 30.6% of those under $1K. The distribution of followers is effectively identical across outcomes.




               The implication is a curriculum, not a slogan
 04
               Discoverability is learnable and currently untaught. Reallocating founder effort from audience-building to findability is the single actionable move this data supports.




      S O W H AT        Treat distribution as a core competency to be trained, not a personality trait founders either have or lack.
