THE FINAL MESSAGE




Build for the top 10%,
not the market average.
This is a power-law market where the average product fails to matter. The visible winners
cluster around AI, Stripe, and B2B with a real, disclosed pricing model — but over half the
money is hiding in unlabeled “stealth” listings. Copying what's visible may just mean copying
the crowded, already-obvious plays.


Go find out what the stealth companies are doing that we can't see.
