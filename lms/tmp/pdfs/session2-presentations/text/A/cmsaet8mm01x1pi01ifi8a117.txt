11 · CONCLUSIONS


Five things this dataset actually says

            $133.8M describes ten companies, not a thousand.
   01
            The top 10 hold 50.6% of revenue and the mean is 44× the median. Read medians or be misled.



            The crowd and the money are in different categories.
   02
            AI holds 19.7% of companies for 13.8% of revenue; Marketing holds 4.2% for 10.9%.



            B2B is a pricing decision, not a scale decision.
   03
            Median B2B and B2C companies serve 16 and 19 customers. B2B earns 5.7× more by charging more per customer.



            Half of these companies are not growing.
   04
            344 up, 337 down, 305 flat — and 30 went to zero this month.



            So 26% of them are for sale.
   05
            Median ask $35,000 at 2.3× revenue — with no measurable premium for B2B.
