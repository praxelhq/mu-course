     Why It Matters

     Mean MRR ($13,472) is ~48x the median ($282) — a handful of outliers (Stan, Brand On Demand, GojiberryAI) pull the average far above what a typical
     startup earns. The top 10 startups alone account for ~48% of all MRR on the platform. Median active subscriptions is just 16, vs. a mean of 501 — the
     same skew shows up in traction, not just revenue.
