So what should a founder take away?
    Expect a power law, not a bell curve.
1
    Ten startups hold ~48% of all MRR. Median success looks nothing like average success — plan for a long, thin runway before any breakout.


    Category crowding ≠ category profitability.
2
    AI has the most founders (197) but not the best average unit economics. Less-hyped categories like E-commerce and Analytics monetize better per business.


    Know which lever you're pulling.
3
    B2B wins on deal size; B2C wins on conversion efficiency. Pick a monetization model that matches your actual customer, not the trend.


    Geography still shapes outcomes.
4
    The same idea monetizes very differently in the US vs. emerging markets — factor this into TAM and pricing assumptions early.


    Building to sell is now mainstream.
5
    A quarter of indie startups are already listed for sale at a median 2.3× revenue — exits at modest scale are a real, common outcome.
