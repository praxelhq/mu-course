The Long Tail of Indie SaaS
What 1,000 TrustMRR listings reveal about a marketplace that looks bigger than it is
   T HE QU ES T IO N




What this deck is actually testing

     “TrustMRR aggregates 1,000 self-reported indie / micro-SaaS startups with public revenue dashboards. Does this
     dataset describe a healthy, diverse startup ecosystem — or a highly concentrated, US-and-Stripe-dependent market
     where a small number of outliers explain most of the revenue and most listed companies are stagnant or dying? This
     presentation uses the dataset itself to test that question.”

   T HE VE R DI CT




A concentrated long-tail market — not a diverse ecosystem
Every dimension pointed the same way:

• Revenue: 10 of 1,000 listings hold half of it
• Growth: the median company grew 0%
• Geography: 25.8% of listings, 63.1% of revenue
• Infrastructure: 64.8% on a single payment vendor
• Pricing: a 2.3× median multiple on a $35,000 ask




   T H E ON E N U M B E R T O R EM E M B E R



   $400 vs $15,596
   Median vs mean 30-day revenue. If you only keep one figure, keep this one —
   it reframes every other number in the deck.




   So what? If you are benchmarking yourself against TrustMRR, benchmark against the median, not the average — the average describes nobody. If you are shopping for an
   acquisition, the 2.3× median multiple is the real market; the long tail is where the listings are, not the revenue.
