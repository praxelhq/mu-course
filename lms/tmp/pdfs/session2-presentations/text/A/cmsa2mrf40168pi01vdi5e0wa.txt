FINAL TAKEAWAY




Most Startups Don't Fail.
They Just Stay Small.
59% of this dataset's startups have never crossed $10,000 in lifetime revenue, while a
concentrated few scale into the millions. The ecosystem isn't a ladder every company climbs — it's
a pyramid very few reach the top of. The data doesn't say why any single company succeeds. It
does say, plainly, how rare it is.
