EXECUTIVE SUMMARY


Five findings from 1,000 verified-revenue companies support a
disciplined micro-SaaS acquisition strategy
01         The supply is real and liquid                                   262 of 1,000 companies were listed for sale at a single point in time — a
                                                                           continuously replenished deal pipeline.


02         Selection is the entire game                                    The top 1% of companies hold 49.6% of all revenue. Sourcing is trivial; identifying
                                                                           the survivors is not.


03         Sellers list into weakness                                      39.7% of listings are declining, against 31.6% of companies not for sale — a
                                                                           persistent, exploitable adverse-selection gap.


04         The mispricing is structural                                    Multiples compress from 8.1× at the bottom to 1.5× at the top. Small sellers price
                                                                           on narrative, not arithmetic.


05         We have a signal others ignore                                  Domain authority predicts revenue three times better than social following — and it
                                                                           is public, free and checkable pre-LOI.


Source: TrustMRR dataset, 1,000 companies with payment-processor-verified revenue, frozen 30 July 2026.                                                         1
