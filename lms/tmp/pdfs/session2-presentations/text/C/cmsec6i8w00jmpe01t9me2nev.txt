   Conclusions
                This is a power-law market, not a normal one. Median company revenue is $3,620; mean is $334,000. 61% of all revenue sits with 10 companies out of
    1
                1,000.



                “Best sector” depends on the question. By total revenue, E-comm and Marketing look strongest — but that's one whale company each. AI is the sector
    2
                with genuine breadth: 197 companies, highest total MRR, no single dominant outlier.



                Growth is flat for half the dataset. Median 30-day growth is 0%. Extreme swings (-100% to +7,900%) exist but are the exception, not the norm.
    3



                Revenue quality varies — not everyone is pure-subscription. MRR × 12 diverges from 12-month revenue by 73% typically, pointing to mixed one-time
    4
                and recurring revenue models.



                Model log-revenue, not raw revenue. Log-transforming closes 91–97% of the normality gap, but ~11% of companies at exactly $0 revenue still need
    5
                separate treatment.
