   Six takeaways, in one place
    1         Revenue is a power law — top 1% of startups hold half of all revenue.


    2         AI is the most crowded category but not the best per-startup monetizer.


    3         Country matters enormously — median MRR spans a 200x+ range.


    4         Social following is a weak-to-nonexistent predictor of revenue.


    5         'On sale' listings out-earn the rest — sellers are cashing out winners.


    6         Missing data isn't random — anonymized listings withhold systematically more.
