  1     Sell to businesses — or sell consumers a career or income outcome, not a lifestyle habit.


  2     Invest in search rankings, not social followers.


  3     Buyers pay for durability, not for a hot month.


  4     “AI” is a crowded label; the winners sell a specific, paid workflow underneath it.
