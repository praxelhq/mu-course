ACT 1 · THE MYTH


A big following is not a big revenue signal

We compared follower count to revenue across 1,000 startups, at three different time
frames (last 30 days, 3 months, and 12 months). In every case, the two barely move
together — a bigger following just doesn't predict bigger revenue.


                                                                                                               Followers vs. Revenue, across all three time frames


    Virtually No Connection
    Between follower count and revenue — at every time frame we checked




Stan and Brand On Demand — two of the dataset's very top earners — built revenue with comparatively modest followings, while several large-audience accounts
monetize poorly.



    WHAT THIS MEANS: A big social following looks impressive, but it doesn't tell us how much money a startup will make. When evaluating a startup, focus
    on how it sells and prices — not how many followers it has.

ACT 2 · THE REAL DRIVER


Paying subscribers are what really drive revenue

                                                                     Unlike followers, the number of paying subscribers a startup has closely tracks
                                                                     how much revenue it makes. A small number of huge outliers can hide this
                                                                     pattern at first glance — but once you look past them, the trend is clear and
                                                                     consistent.



                                                                         Strong, Reliable Link
                                                                         Between subscriber count and revenue — the clearest pattern in the whole dataset




                                                                     But two startups with the same number of subscribers can still earn very different
                Active Subscribers vs. 3-Month Revenue               amounts — because what they charge each subscriber matters just as much.


   WHAT THIS MEANS: Paying subscribers are the best single sign of how much a startup earns. But pricing matters too — it's why two startups with the
   same number of subscribers can end up in very different places.
