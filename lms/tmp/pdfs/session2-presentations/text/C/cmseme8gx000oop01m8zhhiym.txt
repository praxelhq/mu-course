 APPENDIX



 Methodology notes and confidence levels

 SAMPLE                       1,000 startup listings; verified Monthly Recurring Revenue only — projections, valuations, and self-reported traffic excluded from the
                              core analysis.




 HIGH-CONFIDENCE FINDINGS     B2B multiplier and stealth-revenue share are drawn from the full 1,000-company dataset and are directly actionable.




 FINDINGS REQUIRING CAUTION   Freemium vs. paid-first conclusion is internally inconsistent with its own figures; infrastructure-stacking finding rests on n=19. Both
                              should inform hypotheses, not hard screening rules, until re-verified.




 EXCLUDED AS NOISE            Domain-speculation listings ($0 MRR, up to $1.5M asking price) and low-volume traffic metrics (e.g., a per-visitor figure based on 16
                              visitors) were excluded as statistically unreliable.
