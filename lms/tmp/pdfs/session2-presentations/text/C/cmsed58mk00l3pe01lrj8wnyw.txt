Business insight

Revenue is highly skewed: a few winners capture outsized MRR while hundreds of affordable startups hold validated recurring
revenue and customer relationships.


Recommendation

Combine targeted acquisitions with selective product builds — buy to access customers and MRR, build where markets show
stronger monetization potential.
Market opportunity
Revenue is concentrated among top performers while the majority of firms are early-stage and modestly monetised. Many small
companies already deliver recurring revenue at low asking prices.

High-growth markets
Fastest growth observed in Entertainment, Games, Analytics, Sales tools and AI-enabled solutions — driven by adoption and rising
willingness to pay.


Business insight

High-growth markets offer upside but also attract rapid competition; product differentiation and execution speed determine
winners.


Recommendation

Balance: pursue BUILD or BUY in high-growth segments only when you have a defensible differentiator; otherwise prefer tactical
acquisitions that provide distribution or customer lists.

Conclusion — final business
recommendation
Decisions should pivot on three criteria: quality of recurring revenue,
acquisition valuation, and competitive intensity. The highest-return strategy
blends targeted acquisitions for immediate MRR with focused builds in
markets that demonstrate strong monetisation.


      The smartest growth strategy is often to combine data-driven
   acquisitions with disciplined product investments to accelerate scale,
   reduce risk, and maximise long-term return.



Final takeaway

BUY verified, affordable MRR to accelerate—and BUILD selectively in
high‑MRR markets; WAIT in saturated spaces until differentiation or
consolidation opportunities arise.
