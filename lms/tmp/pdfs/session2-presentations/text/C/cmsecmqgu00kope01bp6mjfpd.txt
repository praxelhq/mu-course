        Four takeaways from the data

         1
                 Revenue is winner-take-most. Top 10 companies hold 48% of all MRR — being merely "in the market" isn't enough; category leadership compounds.



         2
                 B2B monetizes 5.7x better than B2C at the median. Same founder effort, structurally higher revenue ceiling — worth weighing before defaulting to a
                 consumer product.


         3
                 Subscriber count predicts revenue; followers don't. r=0.91 for active subscriptions vs r=0.02 for X followers — audience-building alone doesn't
                 convert to MRR.


         4
                 A single outlier can define a whole cohort. One company (Supergrow) is 50.7% of all Indian MRR in this dataset — sample-level conclusions need a
                 wide base before generalizing.
