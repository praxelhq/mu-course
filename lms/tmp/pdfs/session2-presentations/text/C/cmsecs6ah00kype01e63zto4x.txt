       WHY THIS MATTERS FOR ALLOCATION

       •      B2B is 36% smaller in company count than B2C, yet holds 62% more total MRR ($3.35M vs $2.05M) — each B2B company is worth roughly 2.6x a B2C peer

       •      B2B also grows more consistently: 41% of B2B companies added MRR last month vs 23% of B2C — a sign of stickier, budget-backed spend rather than
              impulse consumer purchases.
       •      Action: inside every "Star" category from the matrix, prioritize the B2B-flavored plays first — they carry the model's economics, not just the category's.
