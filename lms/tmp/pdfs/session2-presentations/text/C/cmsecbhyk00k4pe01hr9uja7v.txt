 RISK ASSESSMENT


 Four risks compound each other
 None are catastrophic alone, but they stack — the biggest account is also the biggest data gap.


       HIGH          Single-account concentration

                     Top 1 account = 26.5% of MRR; top 10 = 48%. A churn event on the #1 account is a material portfolio event.


       HIGH          Category blind spot

                     56% of MRR is untagged. Every category-level decision today is based on <44% visibility into the business.


       MED           Declining & volatile segments

                     Content Creation trending negative (-2.2%); Sales (-11.1%) and Security (-9.5%) are the weakest performers.


       MED           Payment infrastructure concentration

                     87% of MRR depends on one processor (Stripe) — a platform risk as international mix grows.
