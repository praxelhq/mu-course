 Reading the multiple



 A 2.3x revenue multiple means a typical buyer is pricing these micro-SaaS businesses at roughly 2-3x their annual revenue — well below the 4-8x+ multiples seen for
 larger, more established SaaS companies. This is consistent with a market of small, high-churn-risk, single-founder-dependent businesses, where buyers price in the risk
 that revenue doesn't survive an ownership transition.

CLOSING


What this dataset really shows


    1            This is a snapshot of the indie/micro-SaaS economy, not venture-scale startups — median MRR of $282 says it plainly.




    2            AI and SaaS aren't just popular labels — they carry the most aggregate revenue too, suggesting real monetization, not just hype tagging.




    3            The resale market (26% of listings) prices these businesses cautiously — around 2x revenue — reflecting key-person and durability risk.




    4            Stripe's dominance (66%) and RevenueCat's mobile niche (20%) map cleanly onto where these founders actually sell: web SaaS vs. mobile apps.
