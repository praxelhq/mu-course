 BACKGROUND



What TrustMRR Is — and Why This Dataset
TrustMRR is a marketplace where founders list SaaS and digital businesses for sale, with self-reported or platform-verified revenue, subscriber, and traffic
metrics. Because every listing carries a consistent revenue metric (MRR), the dataset is unusually well-suited for comparative analysis.

This dataset suits the hypothesis because it contains both popularity fields (X followers, domain rating, category size) and structural fields (audience type,
category, country) alongside a consistent revenue metric, letting us compare which set of signals better separates high- and low-revenue businesses.

One caveat upfront: data completeness varies by field. Followers and domain rating are populated for ~70% of listings; category and country for ~73–76%. All
downstream comparisons use only the rows with valid data for that specific test.

 CONCLUSION



Structural Positioning Wins
Direct answer to the hypothesis: structural positioning — specifically who a business
sells to (B2B vs. B2C) — is the strongest predictor of revenue in this dataset,
outperforming both popularity signals and category crowding.

Practical implication: if using popularity metrics as a proxy for business quality,
audience type is a more reliable — and much simpler — signal to check first.
