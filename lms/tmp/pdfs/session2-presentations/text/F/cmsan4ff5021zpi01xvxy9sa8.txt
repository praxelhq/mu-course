04   · CONCLUSION

The market is not short of builders. It is short of
businesses.
     What the data says                                                                               Limitations worth stating

                                                                                                      •   Self-selected directory: firms opt in, so failures are
     •   A thousand firms produced $15.6M in a month — and ten of them produced half of it.               under-represented and success is likely
                                                                                                          overstated.
     •   The cohort grew 29% against its own yearly average but only 3% against last quarter: the
                                                                                                      •   Single snapshot: trend is inferred from nested
         surge has cooled into a plateau.
                                                                                                          revenue windows, not observed month by month.
     •   Crowding does not pay. The most populated category earns the least per firm.                 •   Traffic missing for 82% of rows — no conversion or
                                                                                                          CAC analysis is possible.
     •   The gap between a $400 month and a $24,000 month is not effort or luck. It is who you sell
         to, what you charge, and whether you own your distribution.                                  •   Association, not causation: no lever here is proven
                                                                                                          to cause revenue.




     If you take one thing away: stop optimising the product and start optimising who it is sold to, what it costs,
