 O P P O R T U N I T I E S — S T R AT E G I C W H I T E S P A C E

Category was the one axis not already concentrated — that's exactly
where to source next
 You can't structurally out-diversify company, geography, or ownership concentration — they're inherent to how this market works. Category is the one axis still
 open.



              Tilt sourcing toward B2B
 1            B2B earns 5.7x the median MRR of B2C ($1,036 vs $181) on far fewer entrants (255 vs 401) — better economics, less noise to filter.




              Prioritize Education and Marketing
 2            Both post top-4 total category revenue with a fraction of AI's headcount — a less saturated, higher-willingness-to-pay buyer base.




              Underwrite the sub-1x acquisition list
 3            Several listed startups combine 100%+ growth with sub-1x multiples — even conservative diligence should clear a subset as accretive buys.
