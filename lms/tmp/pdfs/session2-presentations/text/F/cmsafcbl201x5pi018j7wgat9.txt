 BEING UPFRONT



 Where this analysis is weaker than it looks

              27.4% of records have no category tag
   !

              Category-level conclusions are built on the 726 tagged records, not the full 1,000 — the untagged group could shift the picture.



              "Stealth Company" entries recur across the dataset
   !

              Several of the highest-growth and highest-revenue rows are anonymized founders, so those specific outliers can't be traced or verified.



              Growth-rate outliers are extreme (up to 12,983%)
   !

              Median, not mean, is used throughout — a handful of viral 30-day spikes would otherwise distort every category comparison.
