         Focus investment on these high-ceiling categories which show the strongest revenue potential ($18k-$20k mean MRR).



02 OPTIMIZE       SUBSCRIPTION DEPTH
   Shift product roadmaps to prioritize recurring features over one-time sales to maximize LTV (0.91 correlation with revenue).

   Develop scalable growth playbooks to move the 90% "long tail" startups from the $282 median toward the $10k+ MRR bracket.

   Standardize visitor and funnel tracking across all startups to eliminate current strategic blind spots (82% missing data).



05 MITIGATE       CONCENTRATION RISK
   Diversify the ecosystem by supporting high-growth mid-tier startups to reduce reliance on the top 1% revenue capture.
