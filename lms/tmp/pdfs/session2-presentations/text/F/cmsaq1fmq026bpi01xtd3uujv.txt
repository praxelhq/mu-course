Read the data with these caveats
Data quality notes worth keeping in mind


    Sparse traffic data

    visitors_30d and revenue_per_visitor_usd are missing for 82% and 86% of rows — treat revenue-per-visitor comparisons as directional only.


    Self-reported fields

    Category, pricing model, target persona, and audience type are missing for 27–36% of listings; category totals are shares of the full dataset, not of known-category listings.


    “Stealth Company” placeholders

    A repeated placeholder name for founders who haven't disclosed a public startup name — several are among the highest earners.


    Self-reported revenue

    MRR figures come from connected Stripe/RevenueCat accounts and were not independently verified for this summary.


    Frozen snapshot

    Data is frozen as of 30 July 2026 and does not reflect any changes since that date.
