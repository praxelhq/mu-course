 EXECUTIVE SUMMARY


 Five things the founders need to know

      01                Revenue is a power law, not a bell curve
                                                                           The top 5% of listings (50 companies) generate 73% of all revenue. This is a whale-driven marketplace —
                                                                           GMV, not listing count, is the real growth lever.




      02                Volume and quality point at different categories
                                                                           AI leads on total revenue ($2.36M) simply on count (197 listings). Marketing monetizes 7x more
                                                                           efficiently per subscriber ($353 vs. AI's $51) — a much better acquisition target per unit of sales effort.




      03                Geography is wildly uneven, in a good way
                                                                           Singapore's 9 listings out-earn Germany's 24 by 4x on a per-company basis. A small, curated regional
                                                                           expansion can beat broad geographic scaling.




      04                Payment provider is a quality signal
                                                                           Superwall-processed businesses earn 2.4x the revenue of Paddle peers on similar volume — worth
                                                                           treating as a soft underwriting signal, not just plumbing.




      05                The best inventory is hiding in plain sight
                                                                           Anonymous / stealth listings earn 2.5x more on average than named ones ($32K vs. $13K). Anonymity
                                                                           correlates with premium, not risk, in this sample.

 STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATIONS


 If we were running TrustMRR, we would do this

      1             Protect and grow the top decile
                                                                         Build retention and success programs around the ~10% of listings that drive 84% of revenue — treat
                                                                         them as key accounts, not self-serve listings.




      2             Build a curated Singapore sourcing motion
                                                                         Deliberately recruit SG-based sellers; the region shows a 4x revenue-per-company advantage over
                                                                         comparable markets like Germany.




      3             Court Marketing and Security sellers, not just AI
                                                                         These categories monetize 3-7x more per subscriber than AI — a better fit for any program that rewards
                                                                         revenue quality over listing volume.




      4             Close the SEO and category tagging gap
                                                                         Fix or enrich the ~28% of listings missing domain rating and category tags before they reach buyers —
                                                                         this data gap is actively suppressing good inventory.




      5             Design outreach specifically for anonymous sellers
                                                                         This segment already earns a 2.5x revenue premium; a dedicated, discreet onboarding path could unlock
                                                                         disproportionate GMV.
