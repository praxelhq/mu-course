 T H E C O R E R E L AT I O N S H I P


 Subscribers predict revenue—strongly

 Pearson r = 0.802
 across 736 startups with both active subscriptions and
 30-day revenue reported



 For a subscription-driven marketplace, this is the strongest
 single signal in the dataset. Traffic (visitors) and social
 reach (followers) are far noisier—and mostly unreported—
 making subscriber count the most trustworthy quick proxy
 for revenue when evaluating a listing.

      Anonymity is a signal, not noise.
            Stealth listings skew larger and more revenue-dense—likely pre-exit businesses protecting their identity, not hobby projects.



            Active subscriptions is the single most reliable revenue proxy in this dataset (r = 0.802); prioritize it over followers or traffic
            when screening listings.
