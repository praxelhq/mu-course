 READ THIS BEFORE WE ACT


 Three caveats on the data

                     Self-selected listing, not a market census
         1
                     TrustMRR only includes founders who chose to list — often to sell. It skews toward small, bootstrapped products, not VC-backed or stealth companies, and likely
                     undercounts failures that never got listed.




                     One snapshot in time
         2
                     This is a frozen cut from 30 Jul 2026. “Success” at $1,000 MRR says nothing about retention, churn, or whether that revenue survives 12 months out.




                     Category sample sizes vary widely
         3
                     Content Creation's lead rests on 36 startups vs. 197 for AI. The ranking is directional, not a statistically airtight verdict — treat the top 3 as a cluster, not a single
                     winner.

     R E C O M M E N D AT I O N



     Prioritize sourcing in Content Creation, Marketing, and
     Education

     •       Treat Content Creation, Marketing, and Education as one leading cluster (57-58% hit $1,000+ MRR) — all three clear a
             materially higher bar than AI (43%) or SaaS (38%) broadly.

     •       Use Education and Marketing's larger samples and higher median MRR to de-risk sourcing while Content Creation's ceiling
             plays out.

     •       Before committing capital, validate with a second, non-listing-biased dataset — this read is a strong prior, not a standalone
             thesis.
