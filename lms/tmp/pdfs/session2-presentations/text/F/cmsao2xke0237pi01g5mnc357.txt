revenue is verified directly through their payment processor — not self-                         ✓

(verified) money moving through it.

What We Learned
1     Revenue is concentrated, not evenly spread

      A handful of large listings — and one dominant category, AI — account for a hugely disproportionate share of total revenue.


2     Scale isn't the same as typical success
      AI wins on total dollars through sheer volume; categories like Education and Customer Support show stronger typical (median) performance per
      company.


3     Anonymity correlates with higher earners
      “Stealth” and unnamed listings are a small minority but earn well above the per-company average — anonymity looks more like a choice made
      after success, not before it.


4     Subscriptions are a reliable revenue signal; followers aren't

      Active subscriptions correlate strongly with revenue (r = 0.802); X follower counts show no such pattern in this data.


5     The data has real gaps

      Domain Rating, traffic, and growth fields are blank for a large share of listings — genuine diligence is still required before acting on any single row.
