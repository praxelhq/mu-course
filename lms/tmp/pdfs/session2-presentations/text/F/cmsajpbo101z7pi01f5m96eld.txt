        STEP 2 · THE REVEAL




What This Told Us


           Dodo Payments is built for local GST compliance and emerging-market payment rails. Stripe still leads everywhere else —
           including the US, at 86% — but in India it drops to 22%, overtaken by a provider almost nobody else on the platform uses.




That's not noise. It's a sign that India might behave like a genuinely different market — worth a dedicated look, not just a footnote in
the global numbers.


So that's exactly what we did next.

        STEP 2 · THE VERDICT




Not Yet

     India's median revenue runs 60–125x below the US's across every window, and average revenue is still 13–17x lower.
     Combined with a sample of just 59 startups — 14 of them at literally $0 in the last year — there isn't enough signal yet
     to build a standalone India analysis.




Decision: set India aside for now — not ruled out, just not enough data yet — and go find out what the highest, most consistent
earners in the full dataset actually look like.


We'll come back to India within that lens.
