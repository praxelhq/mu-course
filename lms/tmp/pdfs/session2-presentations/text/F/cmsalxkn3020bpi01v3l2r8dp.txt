EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

A concentrated $13.5M MRR base, where “total” and “typical” tell different stories


                Scale is real, ownership is narrow.
   01
                1,000 tracked startups generate $13.47M in combined MRR, but 16 startups (1.6%) hold 55% of it and a single listing (“Stan”) alone is 26.5%.


                Total ≠ typical: AI wins on volume, not on the typical company.
   02
                AI is #1 of 29 categories by total MRR ($1.81M) but ranks 13th by median company MRR (just $446). Customer Support, Marketplace, Education, Marketing and Analytics show the
                strongest typical per-company performance.

                Anonymous listings are a measurable, non-trivial slice.
   03
                130 listings with a blank name, “Stealth Company,” or “Hidden Business” — carry 28.1% of MRR ($3.79M) and $4.19M of last-30-day revenue (26.9% of the $15.6M total).


                Subscription count predicts revenue; follower count doesn't.
   04
                Active subscriptions correlates strongly with 30-day revenue (r = 0.802, all 1,000 rows). X followers barely correlate with 3-month revenue (r = 0.025, 984 complete pairs), vanity
                metrics aren't a revenue signal here.

                A concrete, evidence-backed action list exists.
   05
                Data-quality fixes, a named risk (a top-2 listing down 74% in 30 days), and a defined Now/Next/Explore agenda are ready for sign-off.
