STRATEGIC IMPLICATIONS

Five takeaways for anyone building on — or buying from —
this market
01       Ignore the average.
                                                  With 57% of startups at $0 MRR and the top 1% holding 69.5% of revenue, benchmark against
                                                  medians and cohorts, never the mean.




02       Category size ≠ category efficiency.
                                                  AI leads on headcount, but E-comm and Social Media startups earn 2-3x more revenue per
                                                  company — pick a lane by unit economics, not hype.




03       Build authority, not just an audience.
                                                  Domain rating correlates with MRR 3x more strongly than X followers — SEO and content
                                                  compounding outperform social reach.




04       B2B still commands a premium.
                                                  B2B startups earn 2.4x the average MRR of B2C peers despite being outnumbered — pricing
                                                  power favors business buyers.




05       Undisclosed listings are the market's
         blind spot.
                                                  $8.1M in MRR sits behind unlabeled or stealth startups — more transparency here would
                                                  materially improve buyer and benchmarking confidence.
