      HIGH

       Conclusion

       This dataset paints a consistent picture: TrustMRR is a power-law market. A small elite of companies — disproportionately US-based,
       B2B, Stripe-processed — captures most of the revenue, while the broad base of listed businesses earns modestly. Popularity metrics
       like social following carry almost no predictive weight; fundamentals like domain authority, audience type, and category fit matter far
       more.


       Next Steps
       1        Rebuild valuation benchmarks on median, outlier-floored multiples

       2        Stand up a tiered coverage model around the top revenue decile

       3        Replace follower-based scoring with domain-rating and audience-type signals

       4        Close funnel-data gaps and de-duplicate listings before the next reporting cycle
