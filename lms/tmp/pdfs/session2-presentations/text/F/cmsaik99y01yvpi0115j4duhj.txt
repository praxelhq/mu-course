 How Much Should You Trust Each Claim?
 All four inferences are cross-sectional stories imposed on a single snapshot, ranked here by evidence strength from strongest to weakest.



       STRONG                  Category crowding vs. profitability                    Large sample, consistent gap across every category pairing.



       MEDIUM                  Domain authority as a revenue moat                     Strong correlation, but causal direction is untested.



       MEDIUM                  Growth-rate headlines are outlier-driven               Clear in the data, but the top bucket is only 15 startups.



         WEAK                  Payment stack signals maturity                         No time series per company; premium-tool sample is very small (n=17).




 Treat this as a hypothesis set to test further, not a settled verdict.
