        fields.

 SO WHAT


 Strategic implications for sourcing & screening
               Prioritize AI and Mobile Apps for deal flow
   1                                                                                                                                                    High impact / Low effort

               Largest listed supply (197 and 52 respectively) combined with above-median multiples (2.65x, 2.30x) — best balance of volume and
               pricing signal.



               Screen on medians and scale tiers, never on category averages
   2                                                                                                                                                    High impact / Low effort

               A single $3.5M MRR outlier can swing a category mean by 10–20x. Build screening thresholds off median MRR and the revenue-tier
               buckets shown here.



               Rebuild every quoted multiple before an LOI
   3                                                                                                                                                  High impact / Medium effort

               Independently calculate asking price ÷ trailing revenue for any target before benchmarking or negotiating — do not cite the
               platform's printed multiple.



               Treat Dev Tools and SaaS as value opportunities, not weak categories
   4                                                                                                                                                 Medium impact / Medium effort

               Lowest median multiples (1.35x, 1.10x) likely reflect higher perceived churn/build-vs-buy risk — worth a closer look for contrarian
               value, not automatic pass.
