WHAT TO REMEMBER


Five things the revenue data tells us

  1          Concentration is real Top 3 categories = 31% of revenue; 26 others share the rest.


  2          Volume ≠ wealth AI leads on total revenue but earns only $12.0K per startup.


  3          E-comm is the quiet winner $58.5K per startup from just 19 companies.


  4          Subscribers, not followers Subscriptions predict revenue (0.80); followers don't (0.02).


  5          The market is liquid 26% are for sale at ~$335.1K on average.
