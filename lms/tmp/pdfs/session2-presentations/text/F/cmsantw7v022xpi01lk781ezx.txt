   SUMMARY



   What Did We Learn?

       ✓         Monetization matters more than growth metrics alone.



       ✓         Active subscribers are the real revenue driver (r = 0.80).



       ✓         Social media following is a misleading proxy for revenue (r = 0.02).



       ✓         The startup market is highly unequal — a few winners, many near-zero performers.



       ✓         Valuations are inconsistent, ranging from under 1× to over 300× revenue.
