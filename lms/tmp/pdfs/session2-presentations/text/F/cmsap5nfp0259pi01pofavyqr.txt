EXECUTIVE SUMMARY



A large, noisy market — with a very concentrated core
The dataset tracks 8,782 revenue-generating startups synced live from payment providers. The headline finding: total activity is large, but value is captured by a
small elite, and how a company monetizes (B2B vs. B2C, geography, category) matters more than how visible it is.

     What this means for decision-makers

     • Averages mislead in a power-law market — benchmark against the median and the top decile, not the mean.
     • B2B monetization efficiency and category conversion rates are stronger signals of quality than follower counts or domain authority.
     • The acquisition market (22% of listings) prices rationally on realized revenue — asking price tracks MRR with 0.81 correlation.
