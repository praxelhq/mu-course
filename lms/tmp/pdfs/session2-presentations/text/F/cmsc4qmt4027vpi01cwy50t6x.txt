IN CLOSING


Four conclusions, three actions
WHAT THE DATA SAYS
                                                                             WHAT WE SHOULD DO

 1     The market is a power law, not a distribution.

       21% of companies hold 95% of revenue. Averages mislead.
                                                                             Weight owned distribution over audience-building.

                                                                             Treat domain authority as a leading indicator in our own reporting.
 2     Distribution is the differentiator.

       Search visibility predicts revenue; audience size doesn't.
                                                                             Screen the 65 revenue-generating listings.

 3     Scale doesn't guarantee growth.
                                                                             Prioritise B2B assets with domain authority above 40.
       Median growth is 0%, even above $10k MRR.


                                                                             Underwrite on cash generation, not growth.
 4     AI is crowded, not yet lucrative.

       40% of companies, 25% of revenue.                                     Require company-specific evidence; the base rate is zero.

APPENDIX A2 · METHOD


How to read this data
Flag these before anyone quotes a figure externally


  1       Verified, but self-selected                    Companies opt in by connecting a payment processor. This is a population of founders willing to disclose revenue — not a
                                                         random sample of startups.



  2       Incomplete segment fields                      Country missing for 24% of records, category for 27%, audience type for 29%. Segment cuts are directional. Several of the
                                                         largest companies are unlabelled 'Stealth' records.



  3       Traffic data is thin                           Visitor counts exist for only 178 of 1,000 companies. Revenue-per-visitor is not reliable at market level and was excluded from
                                                         this deck.



  4       Growth rates are volatile                      Month-over-month figures range from −100% to +12,983%. Medians are used throughout; means are not meaningful on this
                                                         variable.



  5       Point-in-time snapshot                         Frozen 30 July 2026. No time series — all trend statements are single-month changes, not trajectories.
