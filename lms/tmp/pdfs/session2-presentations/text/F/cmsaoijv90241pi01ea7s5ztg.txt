 ACTION PLAN


 Your move, starting tomorrow morning

                   Pick Education, Marketing, or Content Creation
        1
                   Highest median revenue per founder, and the lowest odds of ending up at $0 — better unit economics than chasing the AI wave.




                   Price your idea against buying one instead
        2
                   $35K medians buy a business already earning $1,074/month. Run that math before writing a line of code.




                   Build on Stripe from day one
        3
                   It's the market standard for a reason — 63.6% adoption and the strongest revenue profile among high-adoption rails. Add RevenueCat only if you ship mobile.




                   Ignore your follower count; watch your revenue per visitor
        4
                   Audience size barely correlates with revenue (r=0.02) — track conversion, not vanity metrics.
