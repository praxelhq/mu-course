       Rating is 0. For a large slice of this directory, the product is not the business. The listing is the marketing.

So what?
Three conclusions the verified data supports — and one it does not.


      For founders                      Stop optimising the audience. Optimise the denominator.

                                        Domain Rating moves the odds of $1k MRR 14x; followers move them 2.4x. Recurring pricing triples the odds versus lifetime deals.



      For India                         The constraint is monetisation design, not engineering talent.

                                        Equal attention, 5.7x worse conversion. $12 ARPU against $20. The build layer is solved; the pricing and distribution layers are not.



      For buyers                        Recompute every multiple before you believe it.

                                        The displayed badge annualises 30 days. Against real trailing revenue the median listing is 6.95x, not 3.6x.



    The limit: this is a self-selected directory, not a census. It proves what these 8,782 businesses earn — not what all startups earn.
