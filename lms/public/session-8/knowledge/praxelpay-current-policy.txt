Version: 2.1

PRODUCT PLANS
Starter costs ₹1,499 per month and supports up to 10 team members.
Pro costs ₹4,999 per month and supports up to 75 team members.
Enterprise pricing is custom and begins with a human sales review.

REFUNDS
Monthly subscriptions may be refunded within 7 calendar days of the first payment when the customer has processed fewer than 25 transactions.
Annual subscriptions may be refunded within 14 calendar days of the first payment when the customer has processed fewer than 100 transactions.
Renewals are not refundable, except where required by law.

SUPPORT
Starter support target: 2 business days.
Pro support target: 8 business hours.
Enterprise support target: 2 business hours.

CONTACT AND APPROVAL
No prospect may be contacted without recorded consent.
Enterprise requests, teams of 100 or more, and any account carrying a risk flag require human approval before a draft may be used.
The classroom lead-draft tool prepares drafts only. It does not send email, update a CRM, promise a discount, or approve an account.
