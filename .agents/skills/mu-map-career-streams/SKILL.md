# MU Career Evidence Mapper

Keep one operating course while making its evidence legible across all nine later career choices.

## Resolve the current contract

Read `lms/docs/build/SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md`, COT v3, the active session brief, scoring methodology and current artifact/profile model. Root career-stream material is optional research, not controlling course architecture.

The current longitudinal spine is:

`industry understanding × real company/process × consequential build × verified evidence × trustworthy profile`

Do not replace it with the superseded operator/advisor portfolio contract.

## Map evidence across the whole course

Use nine display rows:

1. Consulting;
2. FOCOS / Founder's Office / General Management / Chief of Staff / Strategy;
3. Product Management;
4. Finance, with IB / VC / Corporate Finance anchors checked separately when Finance is consequential;
5. Human Resources;
6. Supply Chain and Operations;
7. Sales;
8. Marketing;
9. Data.

For each stream identify:

- credible target roles and job-like decisions;
- course artifacts/rehearsals that demonstrate relevant judgment;
- the exact inspectable evidence and public/private location;
- one quantitative or structured rule where authentic;
- likely recruiter question and a two-minute proof action;
- missing evidence, overclaim risk and mitigation;
- fairness/access risk and whether technical polish is being over-rewarded.

A shared artifact may serve several streams through different proof angles. Do not duplicate work just to fill a matrix.

## Gate role-specific variants

Create a variant only when at least three of these materially change:

- input/data schema;
- business metric or calculation;
- decision rule;
- consequential failure;
- action/output;
- accountable human boundary;
- verification evidence.

Otherwise keep the common task and add a concise career-proof prompt. When variants are approved, calibrate time, evidence burden and support; run a noun-swap check to reject cosmetic skins.

For any student-selected project require a named user/customer, narrow job, measurable acceptance test, public or authorised evidence, failure/stop condition, no false affiliation, and a proof a recruiter can inspect without privileged company access.

## Output

Produce or update:

- course-wide career-evidence matrix;
- artifact-to-stream crosswalk;
- project approval rubric;
- approved variant briefs only where needed;
- Finance subpath anchors when applicable;
- two-minute proof cards;
- uncovered or misleading career claims.

Hand session changes to `$mu-plan-lessons`, LMS evidence requirements to `$mu-design-lms`, and the complete map to `$mu-validate-learning-assets`.
