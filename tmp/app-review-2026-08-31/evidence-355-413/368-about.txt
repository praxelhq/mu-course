- heading "The name" [level=2]
- paragraph:

  - emphasis: Ritu
  - text: "is season — the same word Sanskrit uses for the turning of the year and for the menstrual cycle. That double meaning is the whole app, so it became the name: StreeRitu."
- heading "Why a wheel, not a calendar" [level=2]
- paragraph: A calendar tells you what day it is. A wheel tells you where you are. The four arcs are sized to your actual phase lengths rather than tidy quarters, because your follicular phase is genuinely longer than your bleed and pretending otherwise flattens the thing we're trying to show. You can see the whole month at once, and see that you are somewhere specific in it.
- heading "Why Ayurveda" [level=2]
- paragraph: "Ayurveda has a long, practical vocabulary for what a body needs when it is cold, or hot, or depleted — warmth, oil, timing, taste. It is guidance, not diagnosis, and StreeRitu keeps it in that register: a suggestion of sesame oil and a warm lunch, never a claim about your health. Anything with real pharmacology, like shatavari or ashwagandha, comes with a note to ask a practitioner first."
- heading "Why these four colours" [level=2]
- paragraph: The palette is dyed, not digital — pigments before screens.

- heading "One honest caveat" [level=2]
- paragraph: StreeRitu predicts from averages you give it. It is a companion and a mirror, not a medical device and not contraception. If something feels wrong in your body, please talk to a clinician.
