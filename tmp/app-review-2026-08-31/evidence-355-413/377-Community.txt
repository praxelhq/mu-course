    - heading "The rental scam I nearly fell for in my first month" [level=3]
    - paragraph: "A listing near Novena was priced about 30% below everything comparable. The \"agent\" had photos, a floor plan and a very believable story about the owner being overseas. He asked for a one-month holding deposit by bank transfer before any viewing. Two things saved me. First, I asked for his CEA registration number and checked it on the public register — it did not match his name. Second, I insisted on viewing in person; he immediately became unavailable. What I do now, every time: verify the agent's registration, never transfer money before a physical viewing, and pay deposits only against a signed agreement with the landlord's name on it. If a price looks unusually good for the area, treat that as the warning sign, not the opportunity. This is my personal experience, not legal advice — verify current rules and registers independently."
