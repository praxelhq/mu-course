However, because rainy periods correlate with higher mosquito activity, the National Environment Agency (NEA) actively monitors Dengue fever risks. If you are packing insect repellents containing DEET or Picaridin, ensure they comply with your airline's liquid and aerosol limits for flight safety. *Verify current rules independently* with your specific airline and the Singapore Customs portal before flying. **Community Experience:** Expats and locals advise against heavy, thick waterproof raincoats; the intense tropical humidity will make you sweat uncomfortably inside them.
