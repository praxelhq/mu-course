  - generic: STANDARDIZED MEASUREMENT SPECIFICATION · V1.0
  - heading "SQI Methodology & Mathematical Rules" [level=1]
  - paragraph: Service Quality Intelligence (SQI) replaces opaque user-generated star ratings with an open, mathematically deterministic scoring framework grounded in operational parameters and evidence quality weights.
  - text: 01 · FOUNDATIONAL PREMISE
  - heading "The Structural Failure of Star Ratings" [level=2]
  - paragraph: "Consumer review platforms aggregate subjective 1-to-5 star ratings into raw arithmetic means. This produces critical distortion:"
  - heading "Volume Conflation" [level=4]
  - paragraph: Platforms treat a 4.8★ with 30 reviews identically to a 4.8★ with 30,000 reviews, hiding statistical uncertainty.
  - heading "Dimensional Invisibility" [level=4]
  - paragraph: A single rating merges food quality, wait times, hygiene, and value into one number, hiding operational failure points.
  - heading "Recency Blindness" [level=4]
  - paragraph: Management or chef departures 3 months ago are washed out by 5 years of historical review momentum.
  - text: 02 · MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION
  - heading "Deterministic Calculation Architecture" [level=2]
  - generic: SQI = Σ ( DimensionScore_i × DimensionWeight_i ) / Σ ( ActiveDimensionWeights )
  - generic: "Where each DimensionScore is computed from its measured parameter evidence:"
  - generic: DimensionScore = Σ ( ParamScore_j × EQ_j × Indep_j × Temp_j × ParamWeight_j ) / Σ ( EQ_j × Indep_j × Temp_j × ParamWeight_j )
  - strong: Evidence Quality (EQ)
  - text: Score between 0–100 evaluating excerpt directness, verified stay corroboration, and descriptive depth.
  - strong: Independence Factor (Indep)
  - text: Discounting multiplier applied to promotional, influencer, or internal brand marketing claims.
  - strong: Temporal Relevance (Temp)
  - text: Time decay weighting prioritizing recent operating quarters over stale multi-year archives.
  - text: CRITICAL GOVERNANCE MANDATE
  - heading "The Missing Data Rule" [level=2]
  - paragraph: "If a parameter or dimension lacks sufficient evidence:"
  - list:
    - listitem: DO NOT assign zero.
    - listitem: DO NOT assign an arbitrary penalty score.
    - listitem: DO NOT artificially suppress the venue’s observed quality.
    - text: Mark status as
    - code: INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE
    - text: and proportionally redistribute available weight across measured parameters.
  - paragraph: Missing evidence reduces coverage and confidence, NOT quality.
  - text: 04 · ORTHOGONAL MEASUREMENT AXES
  - heading "Confidence Is Not Quality" [level=2]
  - paragraph: SQI strictly separates quality (how good the service is) from confidence (how certain we are of the measurement).
  - text: SQI Quality Score (0–100)
  - paragraph: Represents the expected caliber of service delivered during a standard visit. A new venue can have an SQI of 95 if every verified diner experiences exceptional craft.
  - text: Confidence Index (0–100)
  - paragraph: Function of evidence volume, source diversity, temporal consistency, and cross-source agreement. A high-quality venue with 50 reviews will have high SQI (94) but moderate confidence (72).
