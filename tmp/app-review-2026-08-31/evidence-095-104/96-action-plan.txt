  - term: Creative fit
  - definition: CAROUSEL/STATIC. High-quality imagery or short, engaging videos demonstrating usage, lifestyle integration, and benefits will perform well. Carousels can be particularly effective for highlighting multiple features or stages of use.
  - term: Why
  - definition: Instagram's visual-first nature is ideal for showcasing the aesthetic appeal and practical use of a kitchenware product. The ICP's age range (25-44) is highly active on Instagram, making it a key placement for capturing attention and driving sales through aspirational or problem-solution content.
  - term: What to change
  - definition: Focus on visually appealing content. If using carousel, ensure each card tells a part of the sales story. Use concise, benefit-driven captions and clear calls to action. Leverage Instagram's shopping features if available.
  - heading "Instagram Stories" [level=3]
  - generic: WORTH TESTING
  - term: Creative fit
  - definition: VIDEO (short, vertical). Dynamic, fast-paced videos showcasing the lunchbox's convenience, leak-proof nature, and ease of use. A strong visual hook in the first few seconds is critical.
  - term: Why
  - definition: Stories offer an immersive, full-screen experience and are excellent for quick, impactful, and authentic content. For a cold audience, short, snappy videos demonstrating the product in a relatable, user-generated style can grab attention and drive immediate purchase interest for a practical item like a lunchbox.
  - term: What to change
  - definition: Content must be highly engaging and to the point, with text overlays and direct calls to action (e.g., 'Swipe Up to Shop'). Use native Instagram features like polls or questions to boost engagement if relevant.
  - heading "Facebook Stories" [level=3]
  - generic: NEEDS ADAPTATION
  - term: Creative fit
  - definition: VIDEO/STATIC (vertical). Short, benefit-focused videos or visually engaging static images. Could test content slightly more focused on direct problem/solution or feature highlights.
  - term: Why
  - definition: While similar to Instagram Stories, Facebook Stories often cater to a slightly different audience demographic or consumption habit. Content may need to be slightly more informative or direct compared to Instagram's typically more 'aesthetic' focus, to drive sales for a cold audience.
  - term: What to change
  - definition: Adapt content to be slightly more explicit about the problem solved and the benefit offered. Ensure clear branding and a strong, direct CTA to 'Shop Now'. Test variations of messaging to see what resonates best with this audience.
  - heading "Audience Network" [level=3]

  - term: Creative fit
  - definition: STATIC. Simple, clear product shot with a direct headline and CTA. Avoid complex messaging.
  - term: Why
  - definition: Audience Network placements can extend reach but often have lower engagement quality for direct sales conversions from a cold audience, especially for a product requiring some explanation of benefits and objection handling.
