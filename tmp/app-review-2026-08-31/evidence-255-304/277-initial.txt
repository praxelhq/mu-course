- paragraph: The engine
- heading "Decisions from rules. Explanations from AI." [level=2]
- paragraph: The training engine is deterministic. It looks at your seven-day and twenty-eight-day load, the ratio between them, how many sessions you actually completed, how far off target your paces were, and how hard the last block felt. Then it maintains, progresses, eases intensity, shortens the long run, or inserts recovery.
- paragraph: Only after the decision is made does the AI coach get involved — to put it in plain language. It sees the decision as a fact it must explain, and it has no ability to edit your plan.
