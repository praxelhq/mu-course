  - heading "Overview" [level=2]:
    - text: Overview
  - paragraph: "Football (soccer) is the world’s most popular sport: two teams of eleven aim to move a ball into the opposing goal using any part of the body except the arms and hands. It rewards teamwork, spatial awareness and endless technical refinement, yet needs nothing more than a ball and some space to begin."
  - heading "Core rules" [level=2]:
    - text: Core rules
  - list:
    - text: Only the goalkeeper may handle the ball, and only inside their own penalty area.
    - text: A goal counts when the whole ball crosses the goal line between the posts.
    - text: "Offside: an attacker ahead of the ball and the second-last defender when the ball is played to them."
    - text: Fouls award a free kick or, inside the box, a penalty. Serious fouls draw yellow or red cards.
    - text: Matches restart with throw-ins, corners and goal kicks when the ball leaves play.
  - heading "How to get started" [level=2]:
    - text: How to get started
  - list:
    - listitem: Get comfortable with a ball at your feet — juggle and dribble daily.
    - listitem: Learn to pass and receive against a wall or with a friend.
    - listitem: Join a local 5-a-side or park kickabout to play in real situations.
    - listitem: Pick a position that suits your strengths, then specialise.
  - heading "Terminology" [level=2]:
    - text: Terminology
  - term: Nutmeg
  - definition: Playing the ball through an opponent’s legs.
  - term: One-two
  - definition: A quick give-and-go pass to beat a defender.
  - term: Cross
  - definition: A pass played from wide areas into the box.
  - term: Clean sheet
  - definition: Conceding no goals in a match.
  - term: Press
  - definition: Coordinated pressure to win the ball back quickly.
