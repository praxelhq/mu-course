  - generic: "Correct Academic Answer:"

  - generic: "Why this happens (Concept Derivation):"
  - paragraph: Sulfur has 6 valence electrons. In SF₄, 4 electrons form 4 σ-bonds with Fluorine, leaving 1 lone pair. Steric Number = 4 (bonds) + 1 (lone pair) = 5 → sp³d hybridisation.
