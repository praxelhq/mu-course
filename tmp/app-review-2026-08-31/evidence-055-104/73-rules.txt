  - heading "How to play" [level=1]
  - heading "Setup" [level=2]
  - list:
    - listitem: • 3 or more players, each with a name. Turn order is fixed and rotates.
    - listitem: • Pick a difficulty deck (easy, medium, hard or mixed), an optional turn timer and your target score.
  - heading "On your turn" [level=2]
  - list:
    - listitem: • Draw a word card. It shows a structure clue such as “starts with E” or “has a double S”.
    - listitem: • Roll the die. It lands on purple, pink or yellow.
    - listitem: • Purple asks a meaning question, pink asks a usage question with a blank, yellow asks a sound or spelling question.
    - listitem: • Type your guess. Spelling is forgiving — casing and punctuation are ignored.
  - heading "Hints" [level=2]
  - list:
    - listitem: • Each player gets 3 hints for the entire game (adjustable in Settings).
    - listitem: "• Each hint reveals one more clue: word length, then the first letter, then the first three letters."
    - listitem: • At 0 hints remaining, that player plays unaided for the rest of the game.
  - heading "Scoring" [level=2]
  - list:
    - listitem: • Correct guess = 3 points, minus 1 for each hint used that turn, with a minimum of 1 point.
    - listitem: • Incorrect guess = 0 points and the turn passes.
    - listitem: • The instant any player reaches the target score (21 by default), the game ends and they win.
