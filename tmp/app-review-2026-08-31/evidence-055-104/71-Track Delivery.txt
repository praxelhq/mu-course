  - heading "How to make your blooms last 7 to 10 days" [level=2]
  - paragraph: Simple tips tailored for Indian weather and tap water conditioning
  - heading "Trim at 45° Angle" [level=3]
  - paragraph: Snip 1-2 cm off the bottom of each stem under running water immediately after unboxing. This prevents air bubbles from blocking water intake.
  - heading "Add Sachet & Clean Water" [level=3]
  - paragraph: Pour 1 Gulshan eco-food sachet into lukewarm water. Strip any lower leaves that might touch water to prevent bacterial cloudiness.
  - heading "Keep Away from Direct AC" [level=3]
  - paragraph: Never place fresh flowers directly under the cold blow of an air conditioner or next to ripening fruits (which release ethylene gas).
  - heading "Vastu Ishanya Placement" [level=3]
  - paragraph: Place yellow marigolds and white rajnigandha in North-East (Ishanya) corners for auspicious clarity, peace and uplifting energy.
