- main:
  - heading "How to Play" [level=1]
  - heading "Search & guess" [level=2]
  - paragraph: A random Pokémon is hiding. Search for any Pokémon by name and submit it as your guess — there is no guess limit.
  - heading "Green means exact" [level=2]
  - paragraph: A green cell with a check means that attribute matches the hidden Pokémon exactly.
  - heading "Red means no" [level=2]
  - paragraph: A red cell with a cross means that attribute does not match.
  - heading "↑ aim higher" [level=2]
  - paragraph: "For numbers (Pokédex #, Stage, Height, Weight, BST), an up arrow means the answer is higher than your guess."
  - heading "↓ aim lower" [level=2]
  - paragraph: A down arrow means the answer is lower than your guess.
  - heading "Types are positional" [level=2]
  - paragraph: Type 1 is only compared with Type 1, and Type 2 with Type 2. A Fire/Flying guess against a Flying/Fire answer scores red on both — order matters! Single-type Pokémon show None as Type 2.
  - heading "Keep going" [level=2]
  - paragraph: Keep guessing until you solve it — or hit Give Up to reveal the answer. Every round draws from a shuffled bag of all 1025 Pokémon, so you'll see them all before any repeats.
