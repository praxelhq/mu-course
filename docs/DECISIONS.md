- 2026-09-04 · Room-composite Egress records AUDIO ONLY (`audio_only=True`, `INTERVIEW_EGRESS_AUDIO_ONLY=0` to restore video); the MP4 container and the reserved S3 key are unchanged · LiveKit bills composite egress at $0.02/min for video and $0.005/min audio-only, so across 516 students the recording was the single largest cost in the system — larger than every model call combined — and a measured 7-minute interview produced a 157 MB object, roughly 81 GB of S3 for the cohort against ~2.5 GB for audio. Nothing downstream reads the video: grading works from the transcript, and a human reviewing a flagged interview needs to hear it. Keeping the container and key means the reserve/commit path, `videoS3Key`, and DPDP erasure all work untouched · Rejected: switching to an OGG audio file type (would change the key extension and break the reserve/commit binding), and dropping the recording entirely (a flagged interview must be reviewable).
- 2026-09-04 · `INTERVIEW_MAX_ROOMS=15`, down from the default 30 · The default exceeded two vendor ceilings at once: LiveKit's Ship plan allows 20 concurrent AGENT SESSIONS (not participants — 1,000 of those), and Sarvam's Tier 1 Starter allows 20 concurrent streaming-STT websockets. At 30 the 21st student would have joined a live room whose agent could never be dispatched, which is exactly the "agent never joins" failure already seen once. 15 leaves headroom for reconnects, draining sessions and agent restarts, and caps throughput at ~90 students/hour — so a 516-student cohort needs staggering by section regardless · Rejected: 20 (no headroom for a reconnect that transiently doubles a session) and raising the LiveKit plan for one week.
- 2026-09-04 · STT and TTS run through LiveKit's `FallbackAdapter` with Sarvam first and Deepgram/ElevenLabs behind it, and `INTERVIEW_VOICE_PROVIDER=sarvam|legacy` pins one · Selection previously returned Sarvam whenever `SARVAM_API_KEY` was present, so the legacy pair — installed, keyed and paid for — was unreachable in production: the "fallback" only covered someone forgetting to set the key, never the vendor being down, rate-limiting or timing out, which is the failure that actually happens to a 516-student cohort on a Tier 1 Starter plan. The adapter fails over mid-session with per-provider retries, so a Sarvam outage costs a few seconds rather than the interview · Rejected: a startup-time health probe (proves nothing about the next fifteen minutes) and deleting `SARVAM_API_KEY` to force the fallback (loses the way back, and needs a human awake at the time).
