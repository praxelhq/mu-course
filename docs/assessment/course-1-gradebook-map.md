# Course 1 Gradebook Map

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** internally frozen for build; Masters' Union approval pending  
**Purpose:** name exactly what is graded, once; who scores it; when evidence freezes; and how lesson checkpoints feed the five course components.

## Non-duplication rule

Lesson rubrics are formative checkpoints unless this map names their frozen evidence in a component. A single evidence item may inform more than one feedback conversation, but its points are counted in only one course component. No lesson score is averaged into a component by default.

## Scoring workflow

1. The LMS freezes the component evidence packet and attaches validator facts, versions and an evidence index.
2. AI may summarize logs or point to possible criterion evidence, but emits no final criterion score.
3. A calibrated human scorer inspects the bounded packet, applies the common and stream anchors, cites evidence, and records each criterion score.
4. Safety, integrity, borderline, anomaly and student-contested cases enter moderation.
5. At least 10% of every major component is selected across section, stream/subpath, starting band and score band for blind second scoring.
6. Material disagreement is reconciled before release; reliability and drift are reported by criterion and stream.
7. The released grade retains submission, validator, rubric, scorer, moderation and appeal versions.

## Staffing planning baseline

At 480 students, bounded first-pass scoring is budgeted at 8 minutes for the evidence brief, 10 minutes for the prototype packet, 8 minutes for transfer and 6 minutes for recruiter proof: approximately 256 primary-scoring hours course-wide. A 10% double score adds about 26 hours, excluding flags and appeals. Eight trained section scorers therefore carry roughly 32 primary hours each across the course, with a separate cross-section moderation pool. This is a planning baseline—not a staffing approval—and must be recalibrated during the three golden-journey pilots.

No evaluation model may be sent as final until Masters' Union confirms scorer capacity, turnaround SLA, moderation owner and academic policy.
