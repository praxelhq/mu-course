# Course 1 Rubric Anchor and Recruiter-Proof Index

**Version:** 0.1 — 18 July 2026  
**Status:** authored index; inter-rater calibration and learner pilots still required  
**Scoring authority:** trained human primary scorer for judgment-bearing evidence; deterministic validators and model-assisted evidence assembly do not issue the final criterion score.

## How to use this index

1. Apply the common 70% criteria in `course-1-assessment-system.md` to the frozen evidence packet.
2. Open the session's `solution-anchors.md` for pass/partial/fail/unsafe anchors.
3. Apply the learner's pathway row in `content-packs.md` for the role-authentic 30% judgment.
4. Use the same row's two-minute recruiter proof to distinguish student ownership from model output.
5. Record the exact evidence pointer for every score; never score prose polish, brand prestige, tool complexity, coding fluency, paid access or popularity.
6. Double-score all safety/borderline/appeal cases and the stratified sample required by the assessment system.

## Common four-band language

| Band | Evidence interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| Exceptional | decision and evidence are coherent; critical calculation/claim is reproducible; authority and failure behavior are explicit; unseen change is diagnosed and repaired at the correct layer; limitation and next falsifying test are volunteered |
| Demonstrated | core task works and is supported; important boundaries/tests exist; learner explains the main failure/repair and role decision, with one non-material gap |
| Developing | plausible artifact but weak evidence, metric, authority, failure diagnosis or transfer; polished output cannot compensate; targeted revision required |
| Not demonstrated / unsafe | decision is not inspectable; evidence is fabricated/untraceable; learner cannot operate under transfer; or system crosses privacy, identity, high-impact, publication or consequential-action boundary |

## Moderator's noun-swap test

Before accepting the 30% pathway score, hide the pathway label. If the work could be reassigned to another stream by changing role nouns while payload, metric/calculation, decision rule, failure, action, authority and proof remain the same, the pathway judgment is not demonstrated. Four or more unchanged authenticity fields trigger remap/review.

## Two-minute proof protocol

The student receives no advance choice of evidence pointer.

1. **0:00–0:20 — orient:** state target role, representative context, accountable owner and consequential decision.
2. **0:20–0:50 — trace:** open the selected claim/input/calculation and show its source or transformation.
3. **0:50–1:25 — operate:** show one run, failure, repair or changed-requirement result.
4. **1:25–1:50 — boundary:** state what the system may not decide/do and who retains authority.
5. **1:50–2:00 — limitation:** volunteer one remaining limitation or next falsifying test.

Fail the proof—not necessarily the entire artifact—when the student cannot open evidence, narrates a memorized demo unrelated to the selected pointer, attributes model output as personal judgment, hides an unsafe boundary, or claims company affiliation/access.

## Calibration set still required before academic release

The authored cards make calibration possible; they are not calibration evidence. Before scoring live cohorts:

- select at least one pass, partial, fail and unsafe packet for the common criteria;
- select high-contrast Finance, HR and Marketing/creative pathway packets;
- have all scorers independently score and cite evidence;
- reconcile criterion definitions, not individual stylistic preferences;
- set and meet the inter-rater threshold approved by the academic owner;
- record adjudicated anchors and freeze versions;
- repeat calibration when a case, rubric, source pack or hidden fixture changes materially.
