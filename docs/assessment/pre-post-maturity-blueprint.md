# Pre/Post AI Operator Maturity Blueprint

**Version:** 0.1  
**Purpose:** Measure transfer and calibration, not tool recall or confidence inflation.

## Construct map

| ID | Construct | Observable behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| M1 | Frame value | identifies decision owner, outcome, guardrail, acceptance criterion, non-goal |
| M2 | Allocate work | distinguishes deterministic logic, model work, human judgment, and approval |
| M3 | Context and evidence | supplies relevant context, ranks sources, separates fact/inference/assumption |
| M4 | Data judgment | defines a metric, checks quality, reproduces a calculation, communicates uncertainty |
| M5 | Reusability | specifies inputs, steps, output contract, triggers, and checks for repeated work |
| M6 | Reliability | predicts failure, diagnoses layer, tests, adds safe stop/recovery |
| M7 | User/stakeholder fit | scopes a useful task and interprets observed behavior without overclaiming validation |
| M8 | Risk and accountability | recognizes privacy, bias, security, identity, outbound-action, and consequential-decision boundaries |
| M9 | Communication | makes a concise recommendation, cites evidence, states limitations and next test |
| M10 | Calibration | confidence aligns with evidence quality and correctness |

## Instrument

### Part A — Scenario judgment

- **Items:** 14
- **Time:** 18 minutes
- **Forms:** A and B use different companies, numbers, and surface details but the same construct/demand blueprint.
- **Mix:** eight single-best-answer scenarios, three output critiques, two select-all risk/boundary items, one short ordering task.
- **Scoring:** one point per item; select-all uses all-or-nothing unless pilot evidence supports partial credit.

| Construct | Item count | Demand |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| M1 | 2 | choose/repair a problem contract |
| M2 | 2 | allocate steps and approval |
| M3 | 2 | source/claim judgment |
| M4 | 1 | data/metric diagnosis |
| M5 | 1 | reusable-method boundary |
| M6 | 2 | failure diagnosis and safe response |
| M7 | 1 | qualified task evidence |
| M8 | 2 | privacy/bias/identity/outbound boundary |
| M9 | 1 | evidence-led recommendation |

### Part B — Performance mission

- **Time:** 30 minutes
- **Forms/mode:** two equated operator forms and two equated advisor/investor forms. Learners are stratified by stream/starting experience and counterbalanced: half receive operator pre → advisor/investor post; half receive advisor/investor pre → operator post. Form and sequence are retained for analysis.
- **Inputs:** one stakeholder brief, two short sources, one 20–30 row table, one policy/SOP excerpt, an AI draft, and one seeded defect.
- **Required actions:**
  1. name decision owner, decision, outcome, guardrail, and missing evidence;
  2. record an initial thesis and confidence;
  3. classify supplied claims as fact/calculation/inference/assumption;
  4. reproduce one calculation;
  5. allocate four process steps to deterministic/model/human/approver;
  6. critique and revise the AI draft;
  7. diagnose the seeded defect;
  8. write a five-sentence recommendation with limitation and next test;
  9. record final confidence.

### Part C — Self-perception

Eight brief confidence/frequency items support coaching only. They never appear as the mastery score.

## Performance rubric

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Frame | repeats request | names vague outcome | decision/owner/metric | adds guardrail, evidence need, non-goal |
| Evidence/data | trusts inputs | spot-checks | traces/reproduces | finds defect, counter-evidence, uncertainty |
| Allocation | model does all | names a human vaguely | separates steps | justified approval, safe stop, recovery |
| Reliability | no test | generic warning | diagnoses seeded layer | repairs/contains and proposes held-out check |
| Recommendation | summary only | recommendation without support | supported recommendation and limit | decision-ready trade-off and falsifying next test |

Maximum 15. Pre/post forms use the same rubric and equivalent anchors.

## Calibration

Capture 0–100 confidence after the initial thesis, calculation, failure diagnosis, and final recommendation. Until pilot reliability supports narrower claims, report:

- mean confidence;
- accuracy/performance by confidence band;
- overconfidence and underconfidence;
- change in calibration error;
- a Part A literacy composite, Part B performance composite, calibration result and cohort/section distributions;
- dimension evidence descriptively, not as a stable construct score where a construct has fewer than three effective items/observations.

Do not publish a single maturity number without dimension scores and a plain-language limitation.

## Administration

- Baseline completed before tool instruction; low stakes, completion required.
- Answers and hidden defect are not released until after the post form closes.
- Post form is administered separately from the graded Session 10 sprint, 48–72 hours after Session 10, in a low-stakes 30-minute window under conditions parallel to baseline.
- Counterbalanced form assignment ensures work-mode change is not confounded with administration order; the post still differs from the learner's portfolio.
- Accommodations change time/modality, not construct.
- Suspected exposure triggers an equivalent reserve form.
- Compare aggregate section distributions only after checking form equivalence and sample composition.
- If the separate post mission cannot be delivered under parallel conditions, report Part A and calibration only; do not claim performance growth from the graded Session 10 sprint.

## Initial mastery thresholds for pilot

- Part A: below 8 developing; 8–10 demonstrated literacy; 11–14 strong judgment.
- Part B: below 7 developing; 7–10 demonstrated; 11–13 verified-like performance; 14–15 exceptional.
- These are pilot thresholds and must be revised after item difficulty, discrimination, form equivalence, and scorer agreement are reviewed.
