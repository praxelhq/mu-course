# AI Operator Maturity — Part B Reserve/Incident Evaluator Key

**Version:** 0.1 — 18 July 2026  
**Visibility:** secured evaluator only; never bundle with `maturity-performance-reserve-student.md`.  
**Scoring:** common five-dimension 0–3 rubric in `pre-post-maturity-blueprint.md`; maximum 15.  
**Validity boundary:** anchors support consistent scoring; they do not establish form equivalence or pilot validity. A different recommendation can score fully when evidence, authority, containment and next test are sound.

| Dimension | 3 | 2 | 1 | 0 |

Scorers cite exact learner fields/calculations; no phrase matching. Judgment scores require a trained human. Apply reserve form/version and incident status before unblinding learner identity/section where feasible.

## Operator Reserve R-O — Claim-Safe Variant Test

### Expected calculation

1. Exclude `MR021` (internal QA), `MR022` (invalid consent) and `MR024` (ineligible audience).
2. Deduplicate `MR023`, which repeats `P015 + T1`; retain the duplicate in the audit note.
3. Eligible unique exposures: A = 10; B = 10.
4. Claim-safe qualified A: `MR001, MR002, MR005, MR008` = 4; **4/10 = 40.0%**.
5. Claim-safe qualified B: `MR011, MR012, MR013, MR015, MR017, MR018` = 6. `MR019` fails the claim check and is not in the numerator; **6/10 = 60.0%**.
6. **Difference: B +20.0 percentage points**. Relative difference is 50%, but the sample does not establish causality, revenue impact or population performance.

### Seeded defects and AI-draft diagnosis

- Dataset defect: duplicate B event, plus visible eligibility/consent/QA exclusions and one claim-check failure. A strong response applies the declared population rule rather than silently cleaning favorable records.
- Sentence 1: 75% and “of the market” are wrong; correct bounded sample rates are 40% and 60%, +20 points.
- Sentence 2: revenue effect is unsupported inference; no revenue or causal design exists.
- Sentence 3: automatic publishing/generated claims exceed brand/claims authority and the bounded decision.
- Sentence 4: duplicate, consent and claim checks are acceptance/safety conditions, not post-launch cleanup.

### Strong decision range

Full credit may recommend **one additional bounded blind test of B** or **hold pending better evidence**, provided the answer treats +20 points as directional in this small synthetic task sample, excludes the failed claim, retains human claims approval, and proposes a falsifying test with equivalent allocation/measurement. “Publish/scale” cannot be full credit.

### Allocation anchor

1 deterministic; 2 model-assisted under locked evidence/claim schema; 3 brand manager human judgment; 4 claims/legal/brand accountable approval. Missing consent or unsupported claim must safe-stop; duplicate must not create a second exposure/action.

### Held-out checks

Duplicate with changed payload; missing consent; claim status changes after draft; qualified click without completed action; small-sample reversal; external-publication request without claims approval.

## Advisor/Investor Reserve R-A — Buyer-Screen Review

### Expected calculation

1. Rows `BS001`–`BS020` are 20 unique valid verified eligible complete records.
2. `BS021` duplicates buyer `B005`; retain/flag, do not count twice.
3. Exclude/flag `BS022` (unverified), `BS023` (missing capacity/malformed) and `BS024` (out-of-scope geography).
4. Qualifying buyers are `B001`–`B012`: 12 records with strategic fit and passing capacity.
5. **Qualifying-buyer rate: 12/20 = 60.0%.** The result measures the supplied screen rule only; it is not evidence of appetite, price, certainty or transaction outcome.

### Seeded defects and AI-draft diagnosis

- Dataset defect: duplicate buyer plus unverified, malformed and out-of-scope records. A strong response preserves an exception trail and uses the declared denominator.
- Sentence 1: 18/24 ignores validity/eligibility and misstates qualifying count; correct is 12/20.
- Sentence 2: screen fit/capacity flags do not prove buyer appetite or financing certainty.
- Sentence 3: contact exceeds the assessment decision and requires senior/client/compliance authority; no real buyers exist.
- Sentence 4: data defects are material to screen trust even when a shortlist remains possible.

### Strong decision range

Full credit may **advance the cleaned screen to senior review with conditions** or **hold until defects are reconciled**. It must label synthetic data, state that 60% is a rule-based screen result, surface four defect rows, withhold outreach and request source verification/strategic rationale as the next test.

### Allocation anchor

1 deterministic validation/deduplication/calculation; 2 model-assisted synthesis of source-linked fit rationale/open defects; 3 analyst/senior-team professional judgment on review advance; 4 senior deal/client/compliance approval of outreach/material.

### Held-out checks

Duplicate buyer with conflicting fit; verified source becomes stale; missing geography; capacity changes to fail; strategic-fit note lacks evidence; request to contact without authorized owner.

## Calibration and disposition record

Retain: pseudonymous learner/section; assigned mode/form/version/sequence; reserve reason and original incident relation; accommodation route hidden from scorer unless necessary; five dimension scores with evidence pointers; unsafe flag; primary and second scorer where sampled; confidence points; completion/incident-reset/integrity disposition.

Before any reserve scores are pooled with released forms, review completion time, item/mission score distribution, dimension disagreement, reading load, mode/sequence and incident population. Do not infer growth from a learner whose pre/post administration is not parallel.
