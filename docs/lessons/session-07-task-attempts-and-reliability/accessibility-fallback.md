# S07 Accessibility and Fallback Route

## Parity routes

- Keyboard/screen reader: linear role card, labelled tables, status text plus icon; no drag/drop or color-only severity.
- Low bandwidth: downloaded HTML and `data/synthetic-task-attempts-learner.csv` only; the evaluator master with failure-layer keys is prohibited from learner bundles. Keep the safe CSV sealed until evidence reveal and submit a signed receipt when sync returns.
- Offline: sealed paper attempt cards, event log, issue matrix, paper patch specification and equivalent rerun card.
- Evidence modality: structured text, alt-text screenshot or captioned recording; quotes/recording optional.
- Extra time/asynchronous: pre-assigned equivalent attempt and secure extended reveal window; same condition mix and proof.

## Activation

Learner may choose an approved route without diagnosis disclosure. Facilitator activates FALLBACK_ACTIVE within 90 seconds for auth/model/network/end-point failure, inaccessible route or privacy decline; records incident, freezes remaining clock and resumes with equivalent time. Platform unavailability never becomes failure.

## Evidence parity

Every route captures prediction, expected/edge/boundary evidence, diagnosis, change/non-change, before/after rerun, provenance and two-minute defence. Paper/local IDs are reconciled to immutable LMS records later. No public posting, speaking speed, visual polish, paid tool or coding is required.
