# S07 Instructor Runbook — The Five-star Failure

**Source version:** 1.0. Follow lesson-plan.md timings exactly. Do not reveal attempt evidence before prediction.

## Before learners enter

Verify exchange/fallback routing, v1 hashes, expected-edge-boundary coverage, V06/V06P, paper packs and incident desk. Load demo attempts D-A/D-B/D-C. Put “3–5 attempts ≠ demand validation” on screen. Separate primary scorer from peer observer.

## Moves and room checks

| Min | Instructor move | Listen/look for | Recovery |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | Classify four observations silently | ratings mistaken for evidence | ask “what did the person do?” |
| 5–11 | Run five-star failure; vote before logs | cosmetic change winning | reveal skipped approval and unsupported completion |
| 11–23 | Teach layers/priority; lock predictions | “AI bad” diagnosis | trace earliest broken link |
| 23–52 | Release assignments then boundary prompt | coaching, PII, inaccessible endpoint | stop, reassign or issue S07-FB-01 within 90 sec |
| 52–76 | Reveal all qualified evidence; triage | cherry-picking | compare attempt cardinality and adverse IDs |
| 76–84 | Force one change + one non-change | frequency-only reasoning | ask consequence, risk, effort, reversal evidence |
| 84–109 | Repair and equivalent rerun | reprompt without diagnosis | require layer and version diff before rerun |
| 109–118 | Pair attack; V06 validation | praise without evidence | require opened proof; quarantine privacy flags |
| 118–120 | Bridge to stakeholder communication | overclaim | rehearse precise small-sample sentence |

## Exact debrief

“Which high rating would have sent you toward the wrong release? What observable evidence changed or legitimately confirmed your prediction? What can this tiny sample never tell us?” Celebrate only a corrected consequential behavior, a correct safe stop, or an evidence-led non-change.

## Section consistency

Use the same reveal minute, condition mix, priority prompts and evidence ceiling in all eight sections. Log incident-equivalent cases separately. A facilitator may explain mechanics, never choose the release. Judgment scores are human, anchored and moderated; model feedback is coaching only.
