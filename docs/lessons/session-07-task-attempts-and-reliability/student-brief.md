# S07 Student Brief — The Release Meeting

**Role:** accountable prototype owner. **Stakes:** one bad change wastes the next release; one missed boundary can harm the decision. **Non-affiliation:** independent educational project using public/course-supplied synthetic evidence; no endorsement or internal access is implied.

## Mission

Use your assigned attempts to decide one v2 change and one deliberate non-change. You must not coach the tester, remove adverse evidence or claim validation from three to five attempts.

## Inputs

Frozen acceptance contract/v1; role card; expected, edge and boundary attempts; S07-FX-ATTEMPTS-01 fallback; issue and priority worksheets; pathway card from content-packs.md.

## Constraints and success

- Predict before evidence reveal.
- Cite an attempt/evidence ID for every material issue.
- Diagnose the earliest plausible failing layer: evidence/data, method, model, deterministic logic, workflow, interface, policy or user fit.
- Protect the human approval/safe-stop boundary.
- Rerun the failed or equivalent task after a versioned change.
- Success is a justified improvement or correct safe escalation; “users liked it” is neither evidence nor success.

## Submit

S07-PRED, S07-ATTEMPTS, S07-ISSUES, S07-DECISION, v2 checkpoint, S07-RERUN, S07-REFLECTION and provenance. Public view may show sanitized failure/fix; raw attempts remain private. Run V06 and V06P before freeze.
