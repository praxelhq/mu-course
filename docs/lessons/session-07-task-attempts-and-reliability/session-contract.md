# S07 Session Contract — Earn Evidence from Task Attempts

**Version/state:** v1.0 · Authored 18 July 2026 · awaiting independent validation, pilot, LMS load and room rehearsal  
**Capability/outcomes:** C6, C8 (supporting C1, C5); L07-O1–L07-O5  
**Validators:** V06 author pack; V06P tester participation  
**Common mechanism:** Observe → classify → prioritize → change → rerun

## Learner promise

A five-star rating can hide an unsafe product. In 120 minutes, the learner will use three to five qualified task attempts to find the failure that matters, ship one evidence-led change, deliberately reject one tempting change, and prove the repair on a fresh run.

## Observable evidence and portfolio contribution

- immutable pre-reveal release prediction and confidence;
- three to five attempts covering expected, edge and boundary conditions (incident-equivalent evidence allowed);
- issue trail with attempt/evidence pointers and failure layer;
- selected change, deliberate non-change, guardrail and reversal evidence;
- v1→v2 checkpoint and equivalent rerun;
- 120–180 word failure-and-fix entry and two-minute recruiter proof.

This becomes the reliability chapter of the AI operating prototype and private proof pack. It never supports a market-validation claim.

## Prerequisites and dependencies

Session 6 acceptance contract, replayable prototype v1, observability receipt and two received attempts; quiz families S07-D-01 and S07-R-01..03; pathway card S07-*-PACK-01; fixture S07-FX-ATTEMPTS-01. If exchange matching or endpoints fail, activate the calibrated static pack in accessibility-fallback.md without reducing the evidence standard.

## Release boundaries

No confidential/PII input, friend/popularity scoring, unsafe autonomous action or model-only high-stakes score. Human primary scoring applies to judgment; deterministic feedback checks structure. Supported and offline routes have identical prediction, diagnosis, decision, repair and proof requirements.
