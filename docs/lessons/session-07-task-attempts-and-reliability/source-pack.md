# S07 Source Pack and Provenance

Learner evidence comes from the frozen Session 6 acceptance contract/prototype and LMS-assigned task attempts. Every attempt binds role/input/case versions, consent, privacy class, evidence pointer, v1 hash and qualification result. Raw peer identity, prompt history and private payload are not source material for public use.

Course fixture S07-FX-ATTEMPTS-01 and D-A/D-B/D-C are fictional, synthetic instructional assets authored 18 July 2026; see data/README.md. Evaluator-master and learner-safe CSVs are separate; only the safe view may be distributed. Hidden/fallback rows rotate across sections and never support market/population claims. Pathway cards synthesize the approved stream matrix and use no confidential company data.

Any learner-added public source needs URL/identifier, publisher, accessed date, archive/snapshot where permitted, usage/license note and claim ID. Any execution evidence needs attempt/event/run ID, timestamp, version and redaction status. Quotes require consent; observable behavior is sufficient.

At T-30/T-2 days verify source links, fixture checksums, role/input equivalence, privacy wording, v1/replay access and that demo/quiz inputs do not duplicate assessed variants. Exposed, stale or defective fixtures are retired with version history; live evidence is never silently edited. Platform-generated/calibrated fallback is visibly labelled by evidence class.
