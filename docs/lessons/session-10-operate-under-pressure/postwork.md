# S10 Post-work — Audit, Calibration and Safe Publication

**Expected learner time:** 55–70 minutes, plus a bounded defence only when sampled/flagged. The low-stakes maturity forms account for 48–53 minutes.

No strategic revision to the frozen transfer packet unless incident review grants an equivalent reassessment.

Within 48–72 hours:

1. Respond to moderator/random defence request using frozen evidence; written, recorded/captioned or live modes are equivalent.
2. Review criterion feedback and contest through the frozen evidence/appeal route if needed.
3. Complete the assigned low-stakes Part A post-literacy form and separate Part B post performance mission; together they support growth reporting and do not affect the 25% sprint.
4. Update capability transcript and private proof only after audit. Public case page changes only when the transfer claim is accurate and V09/publication approval still passes.
5. Complete delayed retrieval quiz family after all sections finish; retake supports retention, not grade replacement.
6. Write 100 words: capability transferable now; evidence; limit; next deliberate practice.

Program completes human primary scoring, stratified double score, moderation, incident correction and appeals before grade release.
