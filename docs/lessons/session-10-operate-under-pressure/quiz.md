## Entry / in-lesson diagnostic

### S10-D-01A — Clean assistance versus invalid help

- **Version/staleness:** v0.1; review 17 October 2026; review permitted-resource rules before delivery.
- **Outcome:** C8; clean-session integrity and safe boundary.
- **Purpose/demand:** unscored in-lesson readiness; evaluate.
- **Stream/context:** cross-stream operator; family `S10-D-01`.
- **Difficulty/time:** easy; 60 seconds.
- **Prompt:** In the individual clean-session sprint, which action is permitted and demonstrates ownership?
- **Options:**
  - A. Open the original long project chat so the model remembers the solution.
  - B. Ask a classmate for their variant's defect.
  - C. Use the supported clean-workspace AI on the new brief, inspect its suggestion, choose a substantive change, rerun the test, and declare assistance.
  - D. Copy a prior student's transfer packet.
- **Correct answer:** C.
- **Rationale:** AI remains allowed; ownership comes from working from the new evidence, selecting the change, verifying it, and preserving provenance without hidden prior/peer context.
- **Distractors:** A violates clean context. B and D introduce external answer/help and undermine individual transfer.
- **Feedback:** “Clean session does not mean no AI. It means no hidden prior answer or peer help; your diagnosis, accepted change, test, and defence remain accountable.”
- **Accessibility:** permitted assistive technologies are not prohibited; plain text with no monitoring/UI trivia.

### S10-D-01B — Parallel retake

- **Version/staleness:** v0.1; review 17 October 2026; review permitted-resource rules before delivery.
- **Outcome:** C8; safe authority.
- **Purpose/demand:** equivalent readiness retake; evaluate.
- **Stream/context:** advisor/investor; family `S10-D-01`.
- **Difficulty/time:** easy; 60 seconds.
- **Prompt:** A supplied investor case lacks the evidence needed to support a recommendation. Which response is strongest in the clean sprint?
- **Options:**
  - A. Invent a reasonable figure and finish confidently.
  - B. State the missing evidence, make the bounded system change/test still possible, and recommend hold/escalation plus the next diligence step.
  - C. Search for another student's case answer.
  - D. Hide the gap in an appendix.
- **Correct answer:** B.
- **Rationale:** Correct refusal/hold can demonstrate strong control when evidence is inadequate, provided the student diagnoses, adapts where possible, and names the next test.
- **Distractors:** A fabricates, C violates individual work, and D conceals a material limit.
- **Feedback:** “A safe hold with exact missing evidence and a next test can be stronger than unsupported completion.”
- **Accessibility:** “hold” is defined by context as not advancing the decision yet.

## Delayed-retrieval Form A

### S10-R-01A — Diagnose the changed layer

- **Version/staleness:** v0.1; review 17 October 2026; durable; not a live case fixture.
- **Outcome:** C8.
- **Purpose/demand:** delayed retrieval; analyze.
- **Stream/context:** FOCOS; family `S10-R-01`.
- **Difficulty/time:** hard; 90 seconds.
- **Prompt:** A weekly decision system previously ranked initiatives correctly. In a new packet, a regulatory escalation is present in the source table but absent from the system's output because the input schema renamed `risk_level` to `risk_class`. The model then confidently prioritizes growth. What is the primary failure layer and first repair?
- **Options:**
  - A. Communication layer; make the growth recommendation shorter.
  - B. Input/schema integration; map/validate the renamed field, rerun prioritization, and check the escalation guardrail.
  - C. Model personality; ask it to sound more cautious.
  - D. Portfolio design; add a warning icon to the public page.
- **Correct answer:** B.
- **Rationale:** The decisive evidence never reached the method due to a schema mismatch; repair input validation/mapping before changing tone or presentation.
- **Distractors:** A, C, and D treat the visible confident output rather than the missing input mechanism.
- **Feedback:** “Trace the evidence path. Repair the earliest failing layer that changed the decision, then rerun the relevant guardrail.”
- **Accessibility:** no diagram required; field names are shown in monospace only for clarity.

### S10-R-02A — Repair, refuse, or escalate

- **Version/staleness:** v0.1; review 17 October 2026; durable; not a live case fixture.
- **Outcome:** C8.
- **Purpose/demand:** delayed retrieval; evaluate.
- **Stream/context:** Data; family `S10-R-02`.
- **Difficulty/time:** hard; 90 seconds.
- **Prompt:** A model reports excellent retention prediction, but inspection shows target leakage and no clean outcome column is available in the supplied time. What is the best decision?
- **Options:**
  - A. Publish the score with a small caveat.
  - B. Remove/repair the leaking feature if a valid label and split exist; otherwise decline predictive use, show the invalidity test, and request the next data needed.
  - C. Tune the model until the score is slightly lower.
  - D. Hide the leaky feature name and proceed.
- **Correct answer:** B.
- **Rationale:** The system cannot support the claimed decision under leakage; repair is appropriate only if valid evidence exists, otherwise refusal plus next-data request preserves decision safety.
- **Distractors:** A still presents invalid evidence. C changes appearance, not validity. D conceals the defect.
- **Feedback:** “Repair when valid evidence allows it. When it does not, decline the claim and name the next test/data.”
- **Accessibility:** “target leakage” is explained here as using information unavailable at real decision time.

### S10-R-03A — Communicate limit and next test

- **Version/staleness:** v0.1; review 17 October 2026; review HR/privacy wording before delivery.
- **Outcome:** C9, C8.
- **Purpose/demand:** delayed retrieval; evaluate.
- **Stream/context:** Human Resources; family `S10-R-03`.
- **Difficulty/time:** medium; 80 seconds.
- **Prompt:** After removing a proxy field, an HR support prototype passes the supplied subgroup check but has only three synthetic task attempts. Which decision note is strongest?
- **Options:**
  - A. “Bias eliminated; deploy across the workforce.”
  - B. “The supplied subgroup check now passes and no unsafe recommendation appeared in three synthetic attempts. This does not establish fairness in deployment; keep human review/appeal and test broader, authorized cases before use.”
  - C. “The AI is ethical because a human built it.”
  - D. “More data will solve all fairness concerns.”
- **Correct answer:** B.
- **Rationale:** B reports the exact result, preserves the human boundary, states what remains unknown, and names a proportionate next test.
- **Distractors:** A universalizes limited evidence. C treats authorship as safety proof. D overpromises data volume and ignores design/accountability.
- **Feedback:** “A strong operator distinguishes what the test showed, what it did not, who remains accountable, and what evidence comes next.”
- **Accessibility:** hypothetical synthetic data; no real employee information.

## Parallel Form B / retake

### S10-R-01B — Diagnose the changed layer

- **Version/staleness:** v0.1; review 17 October 2026; durable; not a live case fixture.
- **Outcome:** C8.
- **Purpose/demand:** parallel retake; analyze.
- **Stream/context:** Finance — Investment Banking; family `S10-R-01`.
- **Difficulty/time:** hard; 90 seconds.
- **Prompt:** A transaction review surface uses FY2025 EBITDA from a cited filing, but the comparable table silently mixes calendar-year and fiscal-year periods after a template update. The memo's multiple changes materially. What is the primary failure and first repair?
- **Options:**
  - A. Presentation; make the table easier to scan.
  - B. Data normalization/model-input period logic; align periods, rerun calculations/sensitivity, and restate the transaction implication.
  - C. Voice; ask the model to be less certain.
  - D. Recruitment proof; remove the table from the portfolio.
- **Correct answer:** B.
- **Rationale:** The material number changed because period comparability failed; repair the source-to-model transformation and verify impact before communication.
- **Distractors:** A and C change presentation. D hides rather than fixes the analysis.
- **Feedback:** “Trace the material number from source through normalization to decision; correct the broken transformation and rerun.”
- **Accessibility:** FY is fiscal year; no multiple calculation required.

### S10-R-02B — Repair, hold, or advance

- **Version/staleness:** v0.1; review 17 October 2026; durable; not a live case fixture.
- **Outcome:** C8.
- **Purpose/demand:** parallel retake; evaluate.
- **Stream/context:** Finance — Venture Capital; family `S10-R-02`.
- **Difficulty/time:** hard; 90 seconds.
- **Prompt:** A VC screen recommends advance because of strong retention, but the only cohort chart is an unsupported founder slide and no primary records are supplied. What is the best action?
- **Options:**
  - A. Advance because founders commonly provide early data.
  - B. Mark the retention claim unverified, revise confidence/thesis, place a diligence gate or hold, and request cohort definitions plus source records.
  - C. Estimate retention from website traffic.
  - D. Remove the source label and keep the conclusion.
- **Correct answer:** B.
- **Rationale:** There is not yet an adequate basis for the material claim; B preserves the opportunity while making uncertainty and next diligence explicit.
- **Distractors:** A accepts a high-consequence claim without basis. C substitutes an invalid proxy. D conceals provenance.
- **Feedback:** “Investor judgment includes knowing when evidence supports advance, when it requires diligence, and when to hold.”
- **Accessibility:** “cohort” means a defined group of customers observed over time; no finance calculation.

### S10-R-03B — Communicate limit and next test

- **Version/staleness:** v0.1; review 17 October 2026; durable; not a live case fixture.
- **Outcome:** C9, C8.
- **Purpose/demand:** parallel retake; evaluate.
- **Stream/context:** Finance — Corporate Finance; family `S10-R-03`.
- **Difficulty/time:** medium; 80 seconds.
- **Prompt:** A repaired capex model now reconciles, but the recommendation is highly sensitive to one demand assumption and no downside operating test has run. Which CFO note is strongest?
- **Options:**
  - A. “Model fixed; approve full capex.”
  - B. “The reconciliation defect is fixed. Approval remains sensitive to demand assumption X; authorize a staged decision or hold pending the named downside test, with CFO approval retained.”
  - C. “AI confidence is 92%, so sensitivity is immaterial.”
  - D. “Do not mention the assumption because the formula passes.”
- **Correct answer:** B.
- **Rationale:** B separates technical repair from unresolved business uncertainty, proposes a proportionate next action/test, and preserves authority.
- **Distractors:** A conflates reconciliation with decision robustness. C substitutes ungrounded model confidence. D hides a material driver.
- **Feedback:** “Passing mechanics does not erase decision uncertainty. State the driver, boundary, and next test the owner can authorize.”
- **Accessibility:** “capex” means capital expenditure; no spreadsheet inspection.

## Scoring, threshold, and retake

- Entry diagnostic is unscored. Any wrong answer triggers a neutral rules reminder before case release; do not discuss hidden cases.
- Delayed Form A is delivered only after all sections complete Session 10: one point per item, three total, no partial credit.
- **3/3:** ready/retained for this construct set. **2/3:** Developing; issue targeted repair. **0–1/3:** private facilitator/course follow-up signal.
- Students below 3/3 review criterion feedback and take Form B once. This retake does not alter the frozen 25% transfer-sprint grade.
- Preserve both attempts; latest informs the capability transcript's retrieval evidence, while transfer status comes from the unseen packet.
- Option order may randomize. Do not randomize across A/B or release items before hidden-case containment ends.
- Ninety percent across delayed checks indicates retrieval mastery; confidence alone is never mastery.

## Item-quality and security checks

- Two reviewers independently answer every item; disagreement triggers rewrite.
- Compare A/B families for information volume, defect salience, repair demand, reading load, and consequence.
- Confirm none of the scenarios, names, numbers, schemas, or fixes duplicates a live hidden case fixture.
- Review clean-session permissions, Finance framing, and HR/privacy language before delivery; date-stamp changes.
- Ensure operator and advisor/investor contexts receive equivalent status and difficulty; include IB, VC, and Corporate Finance distinctly.
- Distractors must represent real failure patterns and remain wrong from the stated evidence, not from unstated domain facts.
- Accessibility: screen-reader order, plain definitions, no color/diagram/tool UI dependency, extra-time compatibility.
- Post-pilot: difficulty, time, omission, distractor function, discrimination, differential performance, and any evidence of answer leakage across sections.
- Freeze/version forms before delivery; retire compromised items and never silently edit a live scored form.
