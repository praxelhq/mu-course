# S10 LMS Copy and Events

Entry: “New context. Clean workspace. AI allowed; hidden prior answers and peer help are not.”  
Prediction: “State the quality bar, likely layer and human boundary before tools unlock.”  
Freeze: “Your strategic packet is now immutable. A mechanical retry can fix packaging, not rewrite judgment.”  
Partial: “A safe partial result with exact remaining evidence is stronger than unsupported completion.”  
Incident: “Platform fault recorded. Your clock/variant will be restored without penalty.”  
Defence: “Open the selected proof; explain evidence, change, result, authority and limitation.”  
Close: “Completed is not transferable. Your audit distinguishes the two.”

Evidence schema: clean_workspace_id, case/variant/equivalence/version, pathway_pack_id, allowed_resource_version, prediction/sprint_confidence, evidence_pointer/layer, patch_hash/diff, tests/results, decision/owner/boundary, provenance, V10 result, freeze/repair version, defence prompt/receipt, incident/accommodation-private relation, proof attachment. Sprint confidence is formative and excluded from maturity-growth reporting. Simulator-spec.md defines transitions/events. Idempotent retries never overwrite.

Alerts: original-context/peer signal (human review, not automatic misconduct); case/key leak; unsafe action; missing substantive diff; test platform failure; accessibility block; section exposure. Safety/leak pauses; platform/access activates equivalent incident path. No AI detector, keystroke surveillance or accommodation disclosure.
