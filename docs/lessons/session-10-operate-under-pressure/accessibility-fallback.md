# S10 Accessibility and Fallback

Cases, tables, logs, controls and prompts are keyboard/screen-reader accessible; charts include data; images have alternatives; status is not color-only. Approved text-to-speech, speech-to-text, screen readers, alternate input and extra time are permitted—not integrity flags. Defence may be written, audio with transcript, captioned recording or live; speed/accent/camera are not assessed.

Offline route uses sealed accessible case, CSV/table, system-state/patch workspace, deterministic test cards and random defence envelope. It still requires prediction, substantive modification, before/after verification, role decision and human boundary. If network/model/LMS/power fails, facilitator records incident, pauses clock and issues equivalent route/variant. Vendor failure never becomes learner error.

Accessibility status is private and excluded from peer/score comparisons. Public posting is not required. A student who cannot use an inaccessible prototype receives a compatible replay, not a reduced task. Facilitators support mechanics only and do not reveal strategy.
