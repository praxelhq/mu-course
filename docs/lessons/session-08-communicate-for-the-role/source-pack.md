## Staleness and rights review

At T-30 days and T-2 days, instructor checks public links, usage rights, brand/policy wording, model disclosure capability and accessibility output. Volatile tool screenshots/instructions are date-stamped and replaceable; capability/validator rules remain stable. Missing license, consent, source snapshot or claim lineage blocks public use and activates supplied assets.

Never scrape restricted/private systems, upload confidential records or imitate a living person without explicit recorded consent. Synthetic and adapted assets must be visibly labelled.
