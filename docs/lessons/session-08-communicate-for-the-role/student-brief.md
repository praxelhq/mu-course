# S08 Student Brief — Make Someone Decide

**Role:** pathway-specific operator/advisor communicating to the accountable stakeholder. **Stakes:** an attractive artifact that triggers the wrong action—or hides uncertainty—is a business failure. Independent educational work; no employer endorsement or internal access.

## Mission

Turn verified S07 evidence into the format your stakeholder can use. At minute 62 a changed audience, legal, brand, risk, data or accessibility constraint will arrive. Predict what breaks, revise, and preserve the evidence lock.

## Inputs and rules

Use your S07 evidence, S08 pathway card, S08-FX-CLAIMS-01, communication contract, format template and approved media assets. Artifact v1 and v2 must make action, evidence, limit and human boundary findable. Generated media needs provenance, rights, disclosure, captions/transcript and alt text. Visual spectacle earns no credit.

## Submit

S08-CONTRACT; FORMAT-DECISION; CLAIM-LOCK; v1; CONSTRAINT-PREDICTION; v2/changelog; asset/model/rights ledger; blind-scan record; accessibility version; public/private decision; V07 receipt. A blind reviewer should correctly state the action, strongest evidence, limitation and question without coaching.
