# S08 Post-work — The 60-second Stakeholder Test

**Time:** 20–30 minutes · **Receipt:** S08-POST-01

1. Give the final artifact to an assigned reader who sees only the role card; use text/asynchronous route if needed.
2. Capture their stated action, evidence, limitation and open question. Do not ask whether they liked it.
3. Make one evidence-supported revision or explicitly reject the suggested change.
4. Freeze claim/asset/model/rights/accessibility ledger and complete quiz.md retrieval item.
5. Draft Session 9 recruiter lead signal: role + decision + verified result + one proof object. Mark the communication public, private or excluded and why.

Optional: render a second channel from the same claim lock and compare loss of meaning; no grade bonus for media/tool sophistication.
