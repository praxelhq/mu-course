Start: “You are not making content. You are helping one accountable person act.”  
Commit: “Choose the stakeholder, action and format before creation tools unlock.”  
Claim lock: “These three facts may be reframed, never silently improved.”  
Reveal: “The stakeholder changed. Predict what breaks before you edit.”  
V07 error: “Claim CLM-__ appears in the artifact but has no evidence/uncertainty link.”  
Rights stop: “This asset cannot publish until source, permission and disclosure are resolved.”  
Success: “Your reviewer found the intended action and its proof without coaching.”

Canonical evidence: communication_id, pathway_pack_id, stakeholder_role, approver, action, format, claim_lock_version, artifact_v1/v2_hash, constraint_id, prediction, blind_scan_id, asset_ledger, access_version, V07_result, portfolio_receipt. Event sequence is normative in simulator-spec.md. Idempotent retries create versions; a constraint is revealed once per attempt.

Alerts: claim mutation; private/unlicensed media; missing caption/alt text; reviewer coaching; media latency; pathway-format mismatch. Claim/privacy alerts block freeze and route a human; media outage activates static assets without penalty.
