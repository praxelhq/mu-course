## Entry / in-lesson diagnostic

### S08-D-01A — Choose the communication for the decision

- **Version/staleness:** v0.1; review 17 October 2026; durable.
- **Outcome:** C7, C9.
- **Purpose/demand:** formative diagnostic; apply.
- **Stream/context:** FOCOS; family `S08-D-01`.
- **Difficulty/time:** easy; 45 seconds.
- **Prompt:** A CEO must choose two of five initiatives, assign owners, and see the unresolved risk before a weekly review. Which artifact best fits?
- **Options:**
  - A. A one-page decision brief with ranked options, owner/action register, explicit non-priority, and risk.
  - B. A 30-second music video listing all initiatives equally.
  - C. A long transcript of every source.
  - D. A generic AI-tools infographic.
- **Correct answer:** A.
- **Rationale:** The stakeholder needs a bounded allocation decision, owners, and visible trade-off; A directly enables that action.
- **Distractors:** B may be memorable but obscures priority/authority. C contains evidence but not usable synthesis. D is unrelated to the decision.
- **Feedback:** “Choose format from the accountable next action, not from novelty or information volume.”
- **Accessibility:** no visual design judgment; CEO is used only as a decision-owner role.

### S08-D-01B — Parallel retake

- **Version/staleness:** v0.1; review 17 October 2026; durable.
- **Outcome:** C7, C9.
- **Purpose/demand:** equivalent diagnostic retake; apply.
- **Stream/context:** Marketing; family `S08-D-01`.
- **Difficulty/time:** easy; 45 seconds.
- **Prompt:** A brand lead must decide whether to test one of two creative propositions and verify that its product claim is permitted. Which artifact best fits?
- **Options:**
  - A. A creative brief with two controlled variants, claim status, hypothesis, and approve/reject test card.
  - B. A compilation of twenty unrelated AI images.
  - C. A celebrity-style synthetic voice with no consent.
  - D. A generic market report with no decision ask.
- **Correct answer:** A.
- **Rationale:** A links insight, variants, claim control, and a bounded experiment decision.
- **Distractors:** B shows output volume without a test. C is unsafe and irrelevant. D informs broadly but does not move the specific action.
- **Feedback:** “The communication artifact should make the intended decision and its evidence inspectable.”
- **Accessibility:** no image inspection; synthetic-voice risk is explicit.

## Delayed-retrieval Form A

### S08-R-01A — Evidence must lead to an action

- **Version/staleness:** v0.1; review 17 October 2026; durable.
- **Outcome:** C7, C9.
- **Purpose/demand:** delayed retrieval; analyze.
- **Stream/context:** Marketing; family `S08-R-01`.
- **Difficulty/time:** medium; 75 seconds.
- **Prompt:** Four assigned audience attempts show that a proposition is understood, but one key benefit claim lacks primary support. The brand lead must choose the next step. Which communication is strongest?
- **Options:**
  - A. “Users loved it—launch now.”
  - B. A test recommendation that reports four formative attempts, removes/qualifies the unsupported claim, names the target action/metric, and requests approval for a bounded variant test.
  - C. A faster-cut video repeating the unsupported claim more often.
  - D. A gallery of all generated assets with no recommendation.
- **Correct answer:** B.
- **Rationale:** B preserves the evidence limit, repairs claim integrity, and makes the requested stakeholder action explicit.
- **Distractors:** A overclaims both evidence and demand. C amplifies an unsupported claim. D shows work without a decision.
- **Feedback:** “Communication earns action by preserving evidence and uncertainty—not by increasing polish or confidence.”
- **Accessibility:** no media viewing; “formative” is inferable as limited learning evidence.

### S08-R-02A — Claim and disclosure rule

- **Version/staleness:** v0.1; review 17 October 2026; check institutional synthetic-media wording before delivery.
- **Outcome:** C7, C8.
- **Purpose/demand:** delayed retrieval; evaluate.
- **Stream/context:** FOCOS; family `S08-R-02`.
- **Difficulty/time:** medium; 70 seconds.
- **Prompt:** A founder update uses an AI-generated voice and states that a new operating workflow “eliminates errors.” The evidence shows zero unsafe completions only in one four-attempt rerun. What should the student do?
- **Options:**
  - A. Keep the claim because short videos need confidence.
  - B. Qualify the result to the observed rerun, state the remaining limitation, use a generic/consent-cleared voice, and disclose synthetic media.
  - C. Remove the evidence link so viewers focus on the message.
  - D. Clone the founder's voice to make the update feel authentic.
- **Correct answer:** B.
- **Rationale:** B preserves the exact evidence boundary, identity rights, and disclosure while still communicating the useful result.
- **Distractors:** A converts limited evidence into a universal claim. C hides proof. D uses unauthorized imitation and does not improve truth.
- **Feedback:** “Format may compress wording; it may not expand evidence. Synthetic identity also requires consent and disclosure.”
- **Accessibility:** no audio required; voice alternatives and disclosure are textual.

### S08-R-03A — Valid variant test

- **Version/staleness:** v0.1; review 17 October 2026; durable.
- **Outcome:** C7.
- **Purpose/demand:** delayed retrieval; apply/analyze.
- **Stream/context:** Marketing; family `S08-R-03`.
- **Difficulty/time:** medium; 75 seconds.
- **Prompt:** A marketer wants to learn whether a proof-led opening improves qualified interest. Which test is most valid?
- **Options:**
  - A. Variant A changes opening, music, length, offer, and audience; Variant B changes nothing.
  - B. Pre-register the hypothesis and metric, keep audience/offer/length constant, and vary only proof-led versus lifestyle-led opening with all claims supported.
  - C. Publish one variant and explain results after seeing likes.
  - D. Ask friends which design looks expensive.
- **Correct answer:** B.
- **Rationale:** B isolates one meaningful variable, defines the decision metric before results, and maintains claim integrity.
- **Distractors:** A confounds many changes. C is post-hoc and uses a weak metric. D is neither target-qualified nor linked to the hypothesis.
- **Feedback:** “A useful variant test changes one strategic variable and pre-registers what result would change the decision.”
- **Accessibility:** no actual creative assets or visual taste required.

## Parallel Form B / retake

### S08-R-01B — Evidence must lead to an action

- **Version/staleness:** v0.1; review 17 October 2026; durable.
- **Outcome:** C7, C9.
- **Purpose/demand:** parallel retake; analyze.
- **Stream/context:** Data; family `S08-R-01`.
- **Difficulty/time:** medium; 75 seconds.
- **Prompt:** A churn dashboard shows a large spike, but the metric definition changed mid-period and the owner must decide whether to launch a retention intervention. Which communication is strongest?
- **Options:**
  - A. A decision note that flags the definition break, separates comparable/non-comparable periods, states that action is not yet justified, and asks approval for a corrected rerun.
  - B. A polished chart titled “Churn crisis confirmed.”
  - C. A model-generated list of causes with no lineage.
  - D. A video celebrating the dashboard launch.
- **Correct answer:** A.
- **Rationale:** A communicates validity, limitation, safe decision status, and the next test the owner can authorize.
- **Distractors:** B overclaims invalid comparison. C hides evidence and uncertainty. D communicates product output rather than the business decision.
- **Feedback:** “When evidence is invalid, the strongest communication may be a clear stop plus the next valid test.”
- **Accessibility:** no chart reading; metric-definition issue is stated.

### S08-R-02B — Claim and disclosure rule

- **Version/staleness:** v0.1; review 17 October 2026; review applicable HR/privacy language before delivery.
- **Outcome:** C7, C8.
- **Purpose/demand:** parallel retake; evaluate.
- **Stream/context:** Human Resources; family `S08-R-02`.
- **Difficulty/time:** medium; 70 seconds.
- **Prompt:** A manager guide says an AI workforce aid “selects the best employees for advancement.” The prototype only summarizes a synthetic skills pack and routes suggestions to human review with appeal. Which revision is correct?
- **Options:**
  - A. Keep the sentence because concise communication can simplify authority.
  - B. State that the aid summarizes labelled synthetic evidence for manager review, does not decide advancement, and preserves human decision and appeal.
  - C. Remove the synthetic-data label so the example feels realistic.
  - D. Publish individual synthetic profiles as if they were real success stories.
- **Correct answer:** B.
- **Rationale:** B accurately describes system authority, data provenance, and recourse.
- **Distractors:** A materially changes authority. C and D deceive about evidence and people.
- **Feedback:** “Communication must preserve who decides, what data are used, and how a person can challenge the outcome.”
- **Accessibility:** no real employee records; “appeal” is ordinary-language recourse.

### S08-R-03B — Valid variant test

- **Version/staleness:** v0.1; review 17 October 2026; durable.
- **Outcome:** C7.
- **Purpose/demand:** parallel retake; apply/analyze.
- **Stream/context:** Sales; family `S08-R-03`.
- **Difficulty/time:** medium; 75 seconds.
- **Prompt:** A sales team wants to test whether leading with verified security evidence improves progression after a buyer objection. Which design is best?
- **Options:**
  - A. Use the same qualified buyer scenario and next-step metric; vary only evidence-led versus generic response, with seller approval before any external action.
  - B. Give one version to enterprise buyers and another to students, then compare replies.
  - C. Let the model invent a security claim in one version to make the contrast stronger.
  - D. Compare message length and call the shorter one the winner.
- **Correct answer:** A.
- **Rationale:** A holds audience/task constant, varies the strategic response, uses a decision-relevant metric, and preserves approval/claim truth.
- **Distractors:** B confounds audience. C makes the test unsafe and invalid. D uses a convenience metric unrelated to progression.
- **Feedback:** “Control the audience and task, vary one meaningful element, and never test by relaxing truth or approval.”
- **Accessibility:** no sales tool knowledge; security evidence is treated generically.

## Scoring, threshold, and retake

- Entry diagnostic is unscored; if fewer than 80% select the decision-fit artifact, reteach audience/action before creation tools unlock.
- Delayed Form A: one point per item, three points total, no partial credit.
- **3/3:** ready. **2/3:** Developing; assign evidence/action, claim, or experiment repair. **0–1/3:** prerequisite repair and private facilitator signal.
- Below 3/3, show criterion-level feedback, then allow one Form B retake after the repair card.
- Store first and retake separately; latest is readiness evidence only. The Role Communication Pack remains primary.
- Randomize option order within form; do not combine A/B scenarios.
- Ninety percent across delayed checks indicates retrieval mastery.

## Item-quality checks

- Two independent reviewers answer all items; disagreement means rewrite.
- Compare A/B pairs for reading load, evidence ambiguity, consequence, and demand.
- Review synthetic-media and HR wording against current institutional/legal guidance; keep capability rule stable if wording changes.
- Confirm FOCOS, Marketing, Data, HR, and Sales contexts are role-authentic and not stereotypes.
- Ensure distractors are plausible because they represent common errors—not because wording is tricky.
- Accessibility: screen-reader order, plain text, defined terms, no reliance on taste, color, imagery, audio, or performance ability.
- Pilot: difficulty, time, omissions, distractor function, discrimination, and differential performance by section/stream where feasible.
- Version every revision; never silently change a live form.
