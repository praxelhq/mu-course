## S04-CASE-01 — When polish becomes operational risk

`Northstar Kitchens` runs a weekly operating review. A generic assistant turns incomplete initiatives into decisive CEO recommendations. The incident is fictional but the control problem is common: language fluency can hide absent fields, stale evidence, non-trigger misuse, and unclear approval authority.

### Decision question

What must be explicit so the method can travel to a clean runner without inventing context or authority?

### Supplied evidence

- `data/s04-demo-weekly-pack-synthetic.csv`: six synthetic initiatives and three seeded defects.
- `data/s04-stream-fixtures-synthetic.json`: normal/messy/non-trigger/hidden records for eleven pathways.
- `data/s04-fixture-schema.json`: field dictionary and permitted status values.
- `content-packs.md`: role, calculation/rule, action, authority, and proof contract.

### What the pack does not prove

It does not establish that a method is production-safe, that a model is reliable across domains, or that the fictional priorities are strategically correct. The assessed claim is bounded portability and failure handling.
