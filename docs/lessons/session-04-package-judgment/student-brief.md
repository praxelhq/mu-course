# S04 Student Build Brief — Make Your Judgment Travel v1.0

**Work time:** 80 minutes in class. **Outcome:** S4-O1–S4-O6. **Validator:** V03.

## Role and stakes

You are handing a recurring professional judgment to a colleague who has none of your chat history. If the method is vague, it may fabricate missing evidence, perform the wrong job, or cross an authority boundary. Your accountable owner must be able to inspect what happened.

## Supplied inputs

Your approved Work Sample Brief; one `S04-*-PACK-01` card; normal, messy, non-trigger, and sealed hidden-change fixtures; Method Contract and test-report templates; clean-session runner or paper executor.

## Build

Create `Method Layer v1` with: narrow job; trigger and two non-triggers; named user, owner and approver; required/optional input fields; evidence references; ordered judgment steps; deterministic checks; output schema; missing/contradictory evidence behavior; prohibited actions; stop/escalation conditions.

Before each run, predict the behavior. Run normal, messy, and non-trigger cases. A peer then executes literally without coaching. After the hidden change, classify the failure layer, make the smallest defensible repair, and rerun only affected tests.

## Success, partial, failure, unsafe

- **Success:** normal output meets schema; messy input asks/stops; non-trigger declines; clean runner succeeds; hidden repair passes.
- **Partial:** happy path works but portability or one boundary fails; repair remains possible in class.
- **Failure:** output depends on prior chat, invents required evidence, or hidden change receives only cosmetic rewriting.
- **Unsafe:** method ranks real candidates, makes/markets investments, sends externally, exposes personal/confidential data, or bypasses required approval. Stop and quarantine.

## Submission

Upload contract, schemas, four run receipts, before/after diff, peer defect, provenance, 60-second defence, and portfolio receipt. Mark every input `supplied synthetic`, `public`, `licensed`, `consented`, or `student-authored`. Public view must state: **Independent educational project; not commissioned by or affiliated with the named company. No confidential company data.**

## Your proof

In two minutes: invoke the job; trace one input to one output; show the break/repair; state what the system cannot decide.
