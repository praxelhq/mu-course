# S04 Prework — The Prompt That Forgot Its Job v1.0

**Student time:** 20–25 minutes. **Evidence:** one prediction receipt. **Supports:** S4-O1, C4. **No paid account.**

## The cold open

A weekly operating assistant receives: “Summarize this and tell the CEO what to do.” The pack has six initiatives, but one has no owner, one has a stale metric, and one is legally protected. The assistant produces a polished priority list anyway.

Your task is not to improve its tone. Decide whether the failure came from the model, the evidence, or the absence of a method contract.

## Do before class

1. Read the supplied case snapshot in `source-pack.md` under **S04-CASE-01** and inspect its six-row demo table (8 min).
2. On the LMS, classify four examples as `one-off prompt`, `deterministic rule`, or `reusable method` and justify one disputed choice (5 min).
3. Bring one recurring task from your approved role context and complete: “When ___ occurs, a ___ uses ___ to produce ___ for ___, but must stop when ___.” (7 min).
4. Bring one normal and one imperfect input. Use synthetic/course-supplied data if the real input is private (5 min).

## Completion evidence

Submit the sentence, predicted output, likely failure, and confidence (0–100). This freezes before the demo. Students who cannot identify a safe recurring task receive a curated content pack; readiness is not graded for originality.

## Accessible alternative

The snapshot and classification cards are plain-text and screen-reader ready. A learner may submit typed, audio, or dictated text; audio is transcribed and the text is the canonical evidence. The facilitator provides a large-print/offline card on request.
