# Northstar Support — Synthetic Source Snapshot

**Asset ID:** `S02-DEMO-SOURCE-v1.0`  
**SYNTHETIC:** Course-authored fictional data; not a real company or causal study.  
**Allowed use:** classroom demo only.

## NS-SEC-1 — Periods

Baseline covers 100 cases recorded during calendar Q1. The pilot covers 100 cases recorded during a later four-week window. Case mix was not controlled.

## NS-SEC-2 — Definition

A case is “resolved” when no further agent action is recorded within seven days. Baseline: 20 resolved cases. Pilot: 25 resolved cases.

## NS-SEC-3 — Limitation

The snapshot can support calculation of the two observed rates. It cannot by itself establish that the process caused the difference or that results generalize.
