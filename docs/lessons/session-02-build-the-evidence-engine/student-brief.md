# S02 Student Build Brief — Earn the Right to Claim

**Asset ID:** `S02-STUDENT-BRIEF-v1.0` · **Role:** evidence owner advising the S01 decision owner

## Stakes

Your stakeholder will approve, reject or narrow the proposed experiment using one material claim. A polished memo that cannot survive a source or denominator check is worse than no memo.

Use your safe source plan or the assigned `S02-*-PACK-01`. Inputs include cached source excerpts, `data/s02_synthetic_decision_records.csv`, dictionary, ledger shell and one sealed contradiction.

## Mission

1. turn the decision into three answerable questions; triage ≥3 sources (≥1 primary, ≥1 independent/contrast);
2. preserve exact snapshot/locator and extract 5–12 fields/rows with raw value, normalized value, unit, period, transformation and limitation;
3. make one visible, decision-relevant calculation and plain-language note;
4. label the claim as fact, calculation, inference or assumption; add caveat, counter-hypothesis and decision implication;
5. freeze v0; predict which link the contradiction affects; retain both sources/rows and repair or hold;
6. let a peer reproduce the number without your answer; complete unseen transfer without a research model.

**Success:** peer reproduces the number and claim remains true after contradiction. **Safe success:** evidence remains unresolved and the decision is explicitly held. **Failure:** citation without support, invisible calculation, deleted contradiction, mixed periods/units, causal overclaim, or synthetic result stated as company fact.

Submit dossier, raw snapshot, dataset/dictionary, formula, ledger, contradiction log, thesis v1, peer/transfer records, provenance and recruiter receipt. Public work must label simulation and non-affiliation; raw licensed/private-sensitive material stays private or is excluded.
