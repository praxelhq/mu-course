## Learner promise and proof

In 120 minutes a learner turns an impressive-sounding AI idea into a bounded, testable work sample for a target role. The learner leaves with `work-sample-brief-v1`, a before/after repair receipt, and an unseen transfer response—not merely an idea or prompt.

**Observable outcome:** name role/mode, accountable decision owner, consequential decision, metric, guardrail, evidence, thin mechanism, two acceptance tests, and stop/escalation authority; then repair the brief when one assumption is removed.

**Common mechanism:** `role → work → owner → decision → metric + guardrail → evidence → allocation → thin system → tests`.

**Portfolio contribution:** Work Sample Brief v1, opportunity thesis v0, acceptance tests v0, decision-log entry `DL-001`, and recruiter proof receipt.

## Safety and truth boundary

All company contexts are independent educational simulations. Students may use public/licensed, explicitly consented, or course-supplied synthetic evidence only. No autonomous employment, capital, pricing, customer, operational or regulated action may pass. One named human owns release, appeal or escalation.
