## Anchor case: Chegg

Use only the verified excerpt and tension in the [anchor case bank](../../research/2026-07-17-anchor-case-bank.md#1-chegg--when-the-interface-to-knowledge-changes). Primary source: Chegg 2025 Form 10-K ([SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1364954/000136495426000021/chgg-20251231.htm)). The filing attributes reduced traffic/subscriber acquisition to AI Overviews and adoption of generative-AI services and reports restructuring/revenue facts. It does **not** prove the monocausal sentence “ChatGPT killed Chegg.”

**Instructor tension:** If the diagnosis is distribution but the build improves answer quality, can the AI project work perfectly and the business still lose? Give the fact pattern for four minutes; students must name the owned decision, missing causal evidence and project implication.
