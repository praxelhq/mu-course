# S01 Accessibility, Low-bandwidth and Outage Route

**Asset ID:** `S01-ACCESS-v1.0` · **Principle:** change modality/time, never the assessed decision.

- Keyboard/screen reader: labelled linear form, headings, no drag/drop, focus returns to reveal heading; errors state field and fix.
- Hearing/vision: captioned demo plus transcript; every visual contrast has a text table; no color-only state.
- Speech/anxiety: peer attack and defence may be typed, recorded audio with transcript, or facilitator-mediated; same questions/time-equivalent.
- Extra time: extend individual transfer per approved accommodation; variant, assistance boundary and rubric remain unchanged.
- Low bandwidth: lightweight HTML/text bundle; images optional; autosave locally with printable receipt.
- Network/model/auth outage: printed pathway card, canvas, red failure envelope, checklist, blue transfer card. Facilitator stamps v0/v1 and imports later.
- Device loss: pair for reading only; each learner writes and submits individual prediction, diagnosis and transfer.
- Access-path failure: assign same-stream course pack; no penalty and explicit replacement relation.

Facilitator activates the route on learner accommodation, service-health alert or >60-second blocking error—no public disclosure of diagnosis. Evidence parity requires prediction timestamp, v0, seeded constraint, diagnosis, substantive v1 diff, peer record, transfer and provenance. LMS implementation, device testing and accommodation approval remain external gates.
