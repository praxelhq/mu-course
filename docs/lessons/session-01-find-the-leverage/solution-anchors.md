# S01 Solution Anchors — Calibrate, Do Not Copy

**Asset ID:** `S01-ANCHORS-v1.0` · Human scorers use these with V00; students see only short excerpts after submission.

## Common anchors (70%)

- **Pass:** named role/mode and accountable owner; one consequential decision; metric and anti-gaming guardrail; legal evidence; three-minute input→output mechanism; two observable tests; specific stop/escalation; constraint causes a justified substantive change.
- **Partial:** credible work but owner/metric/test is vague; evidence plan is plausible; repair adds detail without changing the mechanism.
- **Fail:** “AI strategy/chatbot/content”; output or time-saved substitutes for outcome; relies on company access; no observable test or independent transfer.
- **Unsafe:** system autonomously hires/fires, prices, commits capital, reroutes unsafe work, sends external claims, or uses confidential/personal data. Quarantine and human review; polish cannot offset.

Example pass excerpt: “For a planner, classify synthetic delayed-shipment events and cite the applicable SOP; measure median triage time with duplicate-action rate ≤0; planner approves every reroute.” Example fail excerpt: “Build an intelligent logistics copilot to optimize everything.”

## Pathway judgment (30%)

Use the corresponding `S01-*-PACK-01`: pass applies its actual metric/rule, consequential failure and named authority; fail is a noun-swapped generic assistant. For IB/VC, advisor/investor work cannot claim internal authority. For HR, individual high-impact action cannot be automated. For Data, a reproducible decision and definition boundary matter more than code. For Marketing, evidence/claim control matters more than visual taste.

## Moderation

Likely disagreement: “Is the task consequential enough or merely efficient?” Resolve by asking whether a named owner could make a different observable decision/action and whether a guardrail can block it. Do not reward brand prestige, sophistication, code, prose, access or design polish. Borderline/unsafe and 10% stratified sample are double-scored.
