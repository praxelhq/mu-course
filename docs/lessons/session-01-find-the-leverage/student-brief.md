# S01 Student Build Brief — Your First Monday

**Asset ID:** `S01-STUDENT-BRIEF-v1.0` · **Work time:** 74 minutes · **Outcome:** L01-O1–O6

## Your role and stakes

It is your first Monday in the role you want. A leader has heard “we should use AI” and will fund one narrow experiment—not a transformation programme. You must recommend what to test, what not to pretend, and who remains accountable.

You are creating an independent educational work sample. You do **not** work for or represent the named company, and the company has not endorsed the work.

## Supplied inputs

- one pathway card from `content-packs.md` and its synthetic/public evidence note;
- Opportunity Leverage Canvas in `student-handout.md`;
- normal and hidden constraint cards in `fixtures/s01_synthetic_opportunity_cards.json`;
- validator, peer-attack card, and unseen transfer card.

## Mission

Build a one-page Work Sample Brief that lets a fresh reader answer: who decides what, why it matters, what evidence exists, what the thin system does, how it is tested, and when it stops.

Constraints: one primary outcome metric; one guardrail; only legal evidence; a core task demonstrable in three minutes; two observable acceptance tests; one named human approval/escalation owner; explicit non-goals.

At minute 65 your v0 freezes. A constraint removes access, authority, testability, or metric validity. Diagnose before editing; then produce v1 and a before/after receipt. Finish with a different-industry transfer card without your own text.

## Submit

1. `work-sample-brief-v1`; 2. `opportunity-thesis-v0` (150–250 words); 3. acceptance tests; 4. `DL-001`; 5. peer response; 6. transfer response; 7. provenance/disclosure; 8. recruiter receipt.

Public: sanitized role, context, decision, mechanism, tests, limitation and independent-project label. Private: raw notes, flagged risks and any consent record. Never publish personal/confidential data, secrets or invented company facts.

**Success:** all V00 gates pass and the constraint changes a substantive field. **Safe failure:** pause/escalate because evidence or authority is unavailable. **Failure:** “build a chatbot,” vanity metric, imagined internal data, generic human-in-loop, or copied demo.
