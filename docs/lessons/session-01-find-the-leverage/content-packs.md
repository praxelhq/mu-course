### Authenticity/equivalence note

The cards differ in schema, calculation, rule, failure, output, authority and verification. Consulting/IB/VC remain advisor/investor work, not fake internal operator roles. Every card provides 6–9 fields, one quantitative/structured rule, one consequential boundary, one hidden failure and the same proof burden. Data and Finance are not rewarded for coding; Marketing is not graded on taste.
