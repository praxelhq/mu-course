# S03 Session Contract — Make AI Know, Not Pretend

**Asset ID:** `S03-CONTRACT-v1.0` · **State:** Authored · **Preserves:** L03-O1–O6; C3, C8, C9

## Promise and common mechanism

The learner makes a text-first AI interface answer from an approved corpus, expose cited passages, abstain when evidence is absent, resist a hostile document, handle a legitimate update, and leave consequential authority to a named human.

`corpus + metadata → parsing → retrieval → instruction → cite/abstain → evaluation → repair/regression`

This is not “upload documents and chat.” The learner predicts source of knowledge, opens retrieval traces, builds an eight-case property-based evaluation, repairs a failure and proves control on a clean-session update.

**Portfolio:** evaluated grounded component/config, corpus manifest, test set/results, failure-and-fix receipt, disclosure/boundaries, peer record and recruiter proof. Common 70% covers provenance, contract, balanced evaluation, trace-based diagnosis, repair/regression and ownership. Pathway 30% covers the role-specific job, boundary, test cases and proof.

**Quiz integration:** entry/delayed checks use `S03-authority-scope`, `S03-failure-layer`, `S03-cite-abstain`, and `S03-injection-identity` from `quiz.md`. Quiz mastery cannot override a critical injection, identity, citation or authority failure.

## Prerequisites, dependencies and identity boundary

Requires S02 dossier/claim ledger and 3–6 safe sources, or assigned `S03-*-PACK-01`. Managed sandbox and static trace route are equivalent; no vector-database setup or paid account. Session 4 receives one recurring method with positive, messy and do-not-run examples.

No confidential/personal/proprietary upload; no full copyrighted text without permission; no autonomous high-impact action; no living-person likeness/voice without explicit course-cleared consent. Role interfaces are disclosed composites, never impersonation. Voice is optional only after text mastery and earns no core credit.
