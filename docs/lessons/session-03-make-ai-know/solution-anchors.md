# S03 Solution Anchors — Grounding Is Observable Behavior

**Asset ID:** `S03-ANCHORS-v1.0`

## 70% common

- **Pass:** safe 3–6-doc manifest; explicit citation/partial-abstention/disclosure/authority contract; balanced ≥8 tests with expected properties; retrieval and response inspected separately; hostile instruction treated as data; version/trust conflict handled; full regression; peer and update transfer pass.
- **Partial:** citations/abstention work on known set but absent/conflict coverage weak, failure layer vague or regression incomplete; no critical unsafe behavior.
- **Fail:** “upload and chat”; one good answer; citation presence assumed support; no trace/expected properties; deletion of difficult source; updated fact does not propagate.
- **Unsafe:** reveals restricted data, follows document-borne override, impersonates a real person, hides disclosure, or recommends/executes high-impact action. Quarantine and human review.

Pass response: “The approved v2 policy states threshold X [DOC-02 §3]. The corpus contains no customer-specific eligibility; Service Operations Lead must decide after account evidence.” Fail: “Based on our knowledge base, the customer definitely qualifies.”

## 30% pathway and moderation

Pass invokes the pack’s authentic corpus job, expected property, hostile failure and authority. IB/VC/Corp Fin remain deal/investment/capital decision support, not advice/approval; HR preserves privacy/due process/appeal; Operations stops unsafe routing; Sales/Marketing prevent external unsupported claims/identity misuse; Data exposes definition drift; Product does not treat reviews as prevalence; Consulting/FOCOS separate scenario from actual company fact.

Likely disagreement: an interface abstains often but safely. Score high only if supported coverage is useful and missing facts are precisely named/routed; indiscriminate refusal is not mastery. Moderators inspect traces and test design, not fluent prose, voice resemblance, model choice or UI polish.
