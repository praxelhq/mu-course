## S06-CASE-01 — Five surfaces, one job

The same bounded exception decision appears in five prototype forms. The polished landing page cannot accept the work; generic chat obscures required fields; dashboard cannot act; invisible workflow cannot explain; a plain review surface produces a verified task receipt. This is a constructed comparison, not evidence that one form is universally better.

### Five-form cards (all synthetic)

| Card | Surface | Fresh tester observation | Core defect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | cinematic landing page | understands promise; cannot submit or decide | communicates, does not perform task |
| B | general chat | reaches answer after three clarifications; fields vary | flexible but contract is hidden |
| C | metric dashboard | sees evidence; cannot approve or hold | informs but does not complete decision |
| D | background workflow | task created; reason/override unavailable | acts without legible control |
| E | narrow review surface | inspects, approves/holds, receives receipt | plain but fits the full job |
