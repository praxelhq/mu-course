## Entry / in-lesson diagnostic

### S06-D-01A — Form follows the work

- **Version/staleness:** v0.1; review 17 October 2026; durable, no vendor dependency.
- **Outcome:** S6-O1 / C6.
- **Purpose/demand:** formative entry diagnostic; apply.
- **Stream/context:** Marketing; family `S06-D-01`.
- **Difficulty/time:** easy; 45 seconds.
- **Prompt:** A brand manager must compare two campaign variants, inspect the claim evidence behind each, and approve one for a bounded test. Which first prototype form best fits this job?
- **Options:**
  - A. A public portfolio landing page describing the campaign.
  - B. A constrained review surface showing both variants, claim status, and approve/reject action.
  - C. A general chatbot that can discuss any marketing topic.
  - D. A fully autonomous publishing agent.
- **Correct answer:** B.
- **Rationale:** The user must compare evidence and make a controlled decision; a constrained review surface directly supports that core task and preserves approval.
- **Distractors:** A looks polished but does not perform the work. C appears flexible but weakens the locked comparison and claim boundary. D seems efficient but removes the accountable brand/claim approval.
- **Feedback:** “Start from who must do what next. Choose the smallest surface that makes that task and its evidence usable.”
- **Accessibility:** plain text; no visual/spatial or color dependency; “bounded test” means a limited experiment.

### S06-D-01B — Parallel retake

- **Difficulty/time:** easy; 45 seconds.
- **Prompt:** A business owner needs to change one metric assumption, see the resulting scenario range, and decide whether an intervention should proceed. Which first prototype form best fits?
- **Options:**
  - A. A scenario dashboard/model with visible assumptions, range, and decision cue.
  - B. A cinematic video explaining that data matters.
  - C. An unconstrained assistant that invents a fresh model each time.

- **Feedback:** “The form must expose the decision mechanism, not merely describe or decorate it.”
- **Accessibility:** plain-language scenario; no graph interpretation required.

## Delayed-retrieval Form A

### S06-R-01A — Choose a role-authentic form

- **Version/staleness:** v0.1; review 17 October 2026; durable.
- **Outcome:** S6-O1 / C6.
- **Purpose/demand:** delayed retrieval; apply.
- **Stream/context:** Data; family `S06-R-01`.
- **Difficulty/time:** medium; 60 seconds.
- **Prompt:** An analyst's core job is to ingest a daily file, run quality tests, publish a cleaned table, and alert the data owner when a metric definition changes. Which prototype form is the strongest first choice?
- **Options:**
  - A. A data surface/pipeline with versioned input, tests, output, lineage, and alert.
  - B. A consumer-style chatbot that explains the company.
  - C. A marketing landing page with a “Run AI” button.
  - D. A video walkthrough with no replay package.
- **Correct answer:** A.
- **Rationale:** The value is reliable transformation, quality, lineage, and alerting; a replayable data surface directly represents that work.
- **Distractors:** B is tempting because chat feels “AI-native,” but it does not guarantee transformation/tests. C adds interface theatre without the operating job. D can explain a run but cannot reproduce it.
- **Feedback:** “When the value is transformation and quality control, prefer a replayable data surface over a generic app or chatbot.”
- **Accessibility:** all required facts are in text; no coding knowledge required.

### S06-R-02A — Cut to one core task

- **Version/staleness:** v0.1; review 17 October 2026; durable.
- **Outcome:** S6-O2 / C6.
- **Purpose/demand:** delayed retrieval; analyze scope.
- **Stream/context:** Marketing; family `S06-R-02`.
- **Difficulty/time:** medium; 75 seconds.
- **Prompt:** A student proposes a prototype that researches a market, creates a campaign strategy, generates ten videos, publishes them, buys media, and reports ROI. Which revision creates the best Session 6 core task?
- **Options:**
  - A. Keep everything but reduce the number of videos from ten to five.
  - B. Let a brand reviewer compare two evidence-linked variants, reject unsupported claims, and approve one test.
  - C. Remove all acceptance tests so more features can be completed.
  - D. Build only a stylish home page describing future capabilities.
- **Correct answer:** B.
- **Rationale:** B names one user, one bounded decision, evidence, a human action, and an observable completion suitable for a fresh task attempt.
- **Distractors:** A reduces volume but not scope. C trades evidence for output quantity. D is smaller but does not perform the role job.
- **Feedback:** “Cut scope around one decision and one observable task—not merely fewer generated assets.”
- **Accessibility:** “ROI” is not needed to answer; no marketing-specialist knowledge assumed.

### S06-R-03A — Choose evidence that proves the task ran

- **Version/staleness:** v0.1; review 17 October 2026; durable.
- **Outcome:** S6-O6 / C6, C8.
- **Purpose/demand:** delayed retrieval; apply/diagnose.
- **Stream/context:** Data; family `S06-R-03`.
- **Difficulty/time:** medium; 70 seconds.
- **Prompt:** A metric-review prototype must let an owner change an assumption, view the recalculated range, and either approve further analysis or stop. Which evidence set best proves the core attempt?
- **Options:**
  - A. Page views and total time logged in.
  - B. A screenshot of the most attractive chart.
  - C. Attempt ID, input/assumption version, recalculated result, validation state, owner action, and timestamp.
  - D. The number of prompts the student used while building.
- **Correct answer:** C.
- **Rationale:** C binds a specific attempt to its input, result, checks, accountable action, and time; another reviewer can inspect what happened.
- **Distractors:** A measures attention, not task completion. B is static and may hide assumptions/errors. D measures production behavior, not prototype success.
- **Feedback:** “Instrument the decision path: input → result → check → human action. Vanity activity is not task evidence.”
- **Accessibility:** event fields are written in sequence but no diagram is required.

- **Purpose/demand:** parallel delayed-retake; apply.
- **Stream/context:** Finance — Venture Capital; family `S06-R-01`.
- **Difficulty/time:** medium; 60 seconds.
- **Prompt:** A VC associate must review source-linked company evidence, expose a disconfirming question, and recommend advance, hold, or pass for IC review. Which first prototype form fits best?
- **Options:**
  - A. A diligence dashboard/grounded evidence surface with assumptions, gaps, and an IC decision input.

- **Feedback:** “Advisor/investor work needs an inspectable evidence and decision surface—not autonomous advice or memo theatre.”
- **Accessibility:** “IC” is defined here as investment committee; no finance calculation required.

### S06-R-02B — Cut to one core task

- **Version/staleness:** v0.1; review 17 October 2026; durable.
- **Outcome:** S6-O2 / C6.
- **Purpose/demand:** parallel delayed-retake; analyze.
- **Stream/context:** Data; family `S06-R-02`.
- **Difficulty/time:** medium; 75 seconds.
- **Prompt:** A student plans a data prototype that ingests twelve sources, predicts churn, personalizes offers, sends messages, and updates finance forecasts. Which revision is the best bounded core task?
- **Options:**

## Scoring, thresholds, and retake

- The entry diagnostic is unscored and used to decide whether the instructor reteaches form selection before the build.
- Delayed Form A: one point per item; three points total; no partial credit.
- **3/3:** lesson readiness for this construct set. **2/3:** Developing; assign the targeted repair card. **0–1/3:** prerequisite repair plus private facilitator signal.
- Across all delayed checks, 90% indicates retrieval mastery. This quiz alone does not certify C6 or C8.
- After Form A submission, show rationale and distractor feedback. Students below 3/3 take Form B once after reviewing the repair card.
- Record first attempt and retake separately; use the latest result for readiness, never to replace failed applied evidence.
- Randomize option order only; do not mix A/B stems or contexts in one attempt.

## Item-quality and release checks

- Two reviewers independently answer all eight items; disagreement triggers rewrite.
- Verify A/B pairs match construct, reading load, defect visibility, and cognitive demand.
- Confirm Marketing, Data, and VC contexts are role-authentic and not dependent on specialist calculations.
- Check that every wrong option is clearly wrong for the stated job—not merely unfashionable.
- Screen for tool/vendor UI trivia, cultural assumptions, color/spatial dependency, and ambiguous vocabulary.
- Pilot review: difficulty, completion time, omitted responses, distractor selection, discrimination, and differential performance by section/stream where samples permit.
- Retire/version items if prototype evidence policy changes; never edit a live item silently.
