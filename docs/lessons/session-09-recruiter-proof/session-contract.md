# S09 Session Contract — Make the Work Recruiter-Legible

**State:** v1.0 Authored 18 July 2026; validation/pilot/LMS/rehearsal pending  
**Outcomes:** L09-O1–L09-O5; C9 supporting C1/C2/C6/C8

## Promise

A recruiter will not infer judgment from a tool list. In 120 minutes, the learner will turn the same work into a 60–90 second public scan and a private, reproducible proof route; then a random claim defence will expose whether the page is evidence or theatre.

## Outcome, evidence and portfolio

Learner commits target recruiter and lead signal; classifies artifacts public/private/exclude; builds public case page; hardens repo/proof index; runs secrets/rights/access scan; repairs; survives blind recruiter scan and random 90-second proof. Final objects: public recruiter view, private proof pack, manifest, independent-project disclosure and two-minute proof card.

## Dependencies/boundaries

Requires S08 pack, S09-*-PACK-01, S09-FX-MANIFEST-01 and quiz families S09-D-01 and S09-R-01..03. Public reach, likes and visual polish never affect grade. No company endorsement/internal-access claim, PII/secret, unlicensed media or fabricated result. V08 checks reproducibility/provenance; V09 checks safe publication; human scorer judges role signal.
