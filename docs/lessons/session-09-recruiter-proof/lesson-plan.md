# Session 9 Lesson Plan — Make the Work Recruiter-Legible

**Duration:** 120 minutes live  
**Course position:** Evidence-rail synthesis before unseen transfer  
**Primary capabilities:** C9; supporting C1, C2, C6, C8  
**Canonical outcome IDs:** L09-O1–L09-O5  
**Assessment contribution:** Recruiter case page and proof pack, 15% of course grade  
**Shared mechanism:** **Scan → inspect → reproduce**  
**Protected student-work time:** approximately 90 minutes  
**Publication assumption:** Every learner must produce a publish-ready recruiter view and a private proof view. Actual public publication, name display, and URL visibility remain subject to Masters' Union consent policy; a private or pseudonymous link is an equivalent assessed path.

## Session promise

> A recruiter will not reconstruct your learning from ten folders, a long demo, or a Git history. By the end of class, you will create a 60–90 second public scan that earns deeper inspection—and a proof view that lets a skeptical reviewer verify what worked, what failed, what you changed, and what you personally control.

## Observable lesson outcome

Given the three course objects and their evidence trail, the student will publish or freeze a recruiter-ready public case page and a separate proof pack that are role-legible, reproducible, safe, disclosure-complete, and defensible through a randomly selected claim/component question.

## Complete output contract

The Session 9 submission has two views and one manifest.

### A. Public recruiter view: designed for a 60–90 second scan

Required above the first deep link:

1. target role and stream;
2. representative work context and operator/advisor/investor mode;
3. decision owner and consequential decision;
4. evidence-led thesis or recommendation in one sentence;
5. prototype form and the narrow work it performs;
6. one observed task/reliability result stated with sample limits;
7. one failure-and-fix story;
8. individual contribution and next recommendation;
9. live/replay/demo link and proof-view link; and
10. mandatory label: **Independent educational project; not commissioned by or affiliated with the company. Public, licensed, course-supplied synthetic, or consented evidence as labelled; no confidential company data.**

The page may use a learner-controlled display name. It contains no personal tester identity, confidential material, secrets, unlicensed assets, misleading company affiliation, or unqualified “validated/market fit” language.

### B. Private proof view: evaluator and recruiter deep inspection

Required proof index:

- approved Work Sample Brief and acceptance criteria;
- decision evidence brief, source/evidence map, dataset/data dictionary, original calculation, claim ledger, and counter-hypothesis;
- prototype method and execution contract;
- deployment or replay instructions and frozen version;
- tests, run history, failure injection, approval/escalation evidence, and failure log;
- three to five qualified task attempts, issue trail, release decision, v2 rerun;
- Role Communication Pack and claim/asset/disclosure ledger;
- decision log, supplied/generated/adapted/authored declaration, and individual contribution;
- repository link and immutable commit/checkpoint ID;
- limitations, prohibited uses, known failure, and next test;
- privacy, consent, rights, synthetic-data/media, PII/secrets, and link-health scan results.

The proof view can remain private. Any recordings or raw attempts use consented, minimal, anonymized evidence.

### C. Proof manifest

A machine-readable or structured checklist links each public claim to exact proof. It records artifact version, validator version, case/data version, public/private/excluded state, and reviewer access rule.

### Ownership evidence

- one recruiter-lens blind scan;
- one randomly selected claim/component defence rehearsal;
- one correction made from the scan or defence;
- 150-word or equivalent accessible “what I controlled” statement; and
- frozen submission before Session 10.

**Minimum pass:** a recruiter can identify the role, decision, system, result, limitation, and proof path in 90 seconds; a reviewer can open evidence without instructor narration; all essential safety/disclosure checks pass. Public visual polish is not a scoring dimension.

## Instructor demo script: “From tool museum to proof story”

**Demo length:** eight minutes.

1. Show a page with logos, prompts, screenshots, a video, and “5 users.” Give the room 20 seconds. Ask: role? decision? result? ownership? Most answers will be unclear.
2. Reveal the same project's case page:
   - “Product candidate”; support-trust decision;
   - task completion evidence;
   - prototype and safe fallback;
   - approval failure and fix;
   - precise “four assigned attempts” language;
   - next experiment.
3. Click the result and trace it to task-attempt IDs and rerun log.
4. Click one business claim and trace source → calculation → claim ledger → public wording.
5. Show a contributor statement that distinguishes supplied starter, AI-generated code, student decisions, tests, and modification.
6. Run the scanner. Reveal an exposed test key and a logo treatment implying official affiliation.
7. Move the test key private, remove misleading brand treatment, apply the independent-project disclaimer, and rerun.
8. Close: “The public page earns attention. The proof view earns trust. Reproduction and defence establish ownership.”

## Student task brief

Build for a skeptical but time-poor recruiter. Your page is not a course scrapbook and your repository is not proof unless another person can navigate it.

Use this ordering:

1. What role am I demonstrating?
2. What decision did I own or advise?
3. What evidence changed my view?
4. What narrow work does the system perform?
5. What happened in qualified attempts or tests?
6. Where did it fail, and what did I change?
7. What should the recruiter inspect next?
8. What remains uncertain or unsafe to automate?

Never imply company access or endorsement. Label public, independent, observed, calculated, assumed, and synthetic evidence precisely.

| State | Appropriate examples | Never include |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Misconceptions and facilitator responses

| Misconception | Response |
| --- | --- |
| “A recruiter will inspect my entire repository.” | Earn the next click with a role-specific scan; use the proof index to guide inspection. |
| “Public means all evidence should be visible.” | Public and assessable are different. Sensitive or detailed proof may remain private. |
| “A screenshot proves the workflow/app worked.” | Link to live/replayable execution, test, or run evidence. |
| “More tools make me look more AI-native.” | Tool count is irrelevant. Recruiter signal is judgment, reliability, evidence, and transfer. |
| “My commits prove I authored everything.” | Commits show history. Ownership also requires failure diagnosis, unseen modification, and defence. |
| “Synthetic data weakens the project.” | Undisclosed synthetic data weakens trust. Clearly labelled, calibrated synthetic data enables authentic practice without fake access. |
| “Publishing with the company logo makes it feel real.” | It may imply endorsement. Use restrained attribution and mandatory independent-project language. |

## Differentiation

### Supported path

- Use the standard Praxy page and proof-index templates.
- Supply a static demo/replay and downloadable proof bundle if hosting is unreliable.
- Use structured contribution and failure-and-fix prompts.

### Advanced lane

- Add reproducible setup automation, test badges, a private evaluator mode, or a second recruiter lens.
- Instrument consented proof-view engagement after publication.

Advanced polish or custom domains receive no extra grade points.

## Accessibility and inclusion

- Public and proof views meet keyboard, focus, contrast, zoom/reflow, semantic-heading, and screen-reader requirements.
- Every media artifact has captions/transcript; charts/images have alt text or underlying table.
- Equivalent private-link, pseudonymous, static PDF/HTML, or non-public assessment path is available.
- No grade depends on personal branding confidence, professional network, public identity, or live performance.
- Recruiter scan may be submitted as text, accessible page, or equivalent structured document.
- Learner controls display name and approved disclosure choices.

## Session rubric: 70% common + 30% stream judgment

Canonical references: [course common rubric](../../course/course-1-architecture-v2.md#common-rubric-dimensions) and [stream judgment anchors](../../course/stream-coverage-matrix.md#detailed-stream-cards).

| Common criterion | Points | Demonstrated evidence |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Role and decision legibility | 15 | target role, context, decision owner, decision, system, and next action clear within 90 seconds |
| Evidence and result precision | 15 | claims/results are traceable, correctly qualified, and not inflated |
| Proof completeness and reproducibility | 15 | frozen brief, data, build, tests/runs, attempts, release, and limits are navigable |
| Failure, contribution, and ownership story | 15 | specific failure/fix and honest control of supplied/AI-assisted work |
| Safety, provenance, disclosure, accessibility | 10 | public/private boundary and all publish gates pass |
| Stream judgment overlay | 30 | role-specific lead signal and proof answer from the variant table |
| **Total** | **100** |  |

Essential caps:

- secret/PII/unconsented evidence or deceptive affiliation: publication blocked;
- unsupported public result: maximum Developing until corrected;
- broken live system with valid replay and transparent limitation: eligible to pass;
- inaccessible-only format without equivalent: incomplete with repair;
- custom design, domain, or high engagement does not raise the score.

## Scalable human-scoring model

1. **100% deterministic:** required fields, public claim links, URL health, repo/checkpoint, manifest, secrets/PII, rights/consent, disclaimer, source/synthetic labels, captions/alt text.
2. **100% blind peer scan:** legibility data informs revision; peers do not assign course grades.
3. **100% structured random proof prompt:** each student opens a selected proof item and submits a concise response; this is rehearsal and ownership evidence.
4. **Human primary score:** a trained scorer reviews 100% of frozen recruiter packets using the 70/30 rubric and cites criterion evidence.
5. **Moderation:** all scan blockers, contested claims, integrity flags, score borderlines and appeals receive additional review.
6. **Double scoring:** at least 10% of all recruiter packets is selected across streams/sections/bands for blind second scoring with shared anchors.

A focused packet contains the public page, manifest, one selected claim, one failure/fix and contribution statement. The assessment operations baseline budgets approximately six minutes per learner for first-pass scoring, plus moderation and the 10% reliability sample. Universal three-minute live viva is not attempted.

## Failure recovery and fallback

| Failure | Immediate path | Evidence preserved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hosting or external link unavailable | use cached replay/static export and retain incident-linked URL check | legibility and proof architecture |
| Repository permission failure | upload immutable proof bundle/checksum; repair access asynchronously | frozen provenance |
| Scanner false positive | contest with exact evidence; facilitator override logged | fairness and auditability |
| Public consent unresolved | keep publish-ready draft and proof private/pseudonymous | same assessed capability |
| Unsafe item found | quarantine only affected item; retain private remediation history | safety ownership |
| LMS outage | use downloadable manifest, page template, and local export; attach later with incident ID | all decisions and evidence links |

## After-class follow-up

### Required, normally 30–45 minutes

- Resolve remaining publication or reproducibility warnings.
- Freeze the final Session 9 public/proof views and evidence manifest by the announced deadline.
- Complete a three-item delayed retrieval check on proof versus presentation, public/private boundaries, and ownership evidence.
- Download the frozen proof bundle for Session 10. The original chat/project context will not be available during the clean-session sprint.

### Optional extension

- Ask one practitioner/recruiter for a consented 90-second scan and record only the structured response.
- Add an accessible alternate recruiter view or second role lens.

## Session 10 bridge

Session 10 does not reward additional polish. The frozen proof pack becomes the student's allowed reference. The LMS will issue a new company/context and an unseen defect or constraint. Students must diagnose, modify, test, recommend, and defend in a clean session.
