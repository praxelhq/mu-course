## Totals

- Live: **1,200 minutes = 20 hours**.
- Required session-linked pre-work: **208–230 minutes = 3.5–3.8 hours**.
- Required post-work/bridges: **312–402 minutes = 5.2–6.7 hours**.
- Required session-linked asynchronous work: **520–632 minutes = 8.7–10.5 hours**.
- Required pre-course onboarding/baseline: **83 minutes = 1.4 hours** under the standard route.
- Total required asynchronous work including onboarding: **603–715 minutes = 10.1–11.9 hours**.
- Total learner commitment under the contact-hours assumption: **30.1–31.9 hours**.

The totals include the pre-course onboarding/baseline and S10's separate post-course maturity forms. Approved extra-time accommodations are not used to inflate the planning range. Bounded moderator/defence follow-up applies only to sampled, flagged or appealed work and should be scheduled transparently. Optional advanced integrations, real external users, media polish and public promotion are outside required workload and confer no grade advantage.

## If Masters' Union means 20 total hours

Do not compress the current artifact promise invisibly. Apply all of these reductions:

1. remove the separate public case-page build and use a private LMS proof receipt;
2. provide fully assembled source/data/corpus packs rather than learner research;
3. use replayable configured prototypes rather than deployment/integration;
4. reduce task-attempt exchange to one live and one incident-equivalent attempt;
5. retain the unseen transfer sprint but drop performance-growth claims unless parallel pre/post time remains;
6. assess two objects—decision evidence and controlled prototype—rather than the full three-object portfolio.

This reduced route is a different course promise and requires an explicit decision-log entry and rebalanced assessment weights.

## Delivery monitoring

Collect actual completion time by session, pathway, supported/advanced route and accessibility path. Do not interpret extra-time accommodations as poor performance. A required task is re-scoped when the median supported-route learner exceeds its cap or when completion depends on unplanned instructor rescue. Timing changes between sections require an equivalent-version review and change log.
