# Assessment Operations Model

**Planning scale:** 480 learners, eight sections  
**Status:** internal baseline; staffing and SLA require Masters' Union approval

## Primary-scoring load

| Component | Submissions | First-pass minutes | Primary hours | 10% double-score hours | Primary scorer |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Decision evidence brief | 480 | 8 | 64 | 6.4 | trained section/domain scorer |
| AI operating prototype packet | 480 | 10 | 80 | 8.0 | trained section/domain scorer |
| Individual transfer packet | 480 | 8 | 64 | 6.4 | trained scorer blinded to prior artifact score where practical |
| Recruiter proof pack | 480 | 6 | 48 | 4.8 | trained section/domain scorer |
| Participation exceptions | expected 10% | 3 | 2.4 | — | section lead |
| **Baseline** | 1,920 major packets | — | **256** | **25.6** | — |

Add a 20% contingency for flags, appeals, calibration, meetings and incidents: approximately 56 hours. Total planning envelope is about **338 hours** across the course.

With eight section scorers, the baseline is 32 primary hours per scorer across the course. Double scoring and moderation should be assigned to a separate cross-section pool so the same person is not validating their own drift. Pilot timing replaces these estimates.

## Required roles

| Role | Minimum responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Assessment lead | blueprint, gradebook, anchors, scorer training, reliability and final release |
| Eight primary scorers | 100% bounded judgment packets for assigned roster/components |
| Three stream moderators | advisor/investor/Finance; people/risk; commercial/product/operations/data coverage with cross-training |
| Assessment operations coordinator | packet freeze, assignment, SLA, queues, evidence exports and appeal tracking |
| Technical validation owner | validator incidents and evidence-pack reproduction; no strategic grade |
| Accessibility/incident reviewer | equivalent route and non-penalty relief |

Names can overlap only if independence and workload remain credible. One lead instructor alone is not sufficient.

## Workflow and SLA

1. LMS freezes the bounded packet and validator facts.
2. Operations assigns a calibrated human scorer and due date.
3. First-pass scorer records criterion scores and evidence citations.
4. All safety/integrity/borderline/contested cases route to moderation.
5. Ten percent per major component is selected before score release for blind second scoring, stratified by section, stream/subpath, starting band and preliminary band.
6. Differences above the approved criterion/total tolerance are reconciled and drift triggers extra sampling.
7. Grades release only after incident correction, reliability check and assessment-lead sign-off.

Provisional target: feedback within seven calendar days of component freeze and final grade within ten days of Session 10, subject to MU calendar. The 24-hour post-session pulse is operational feedback, not full artifact grading.

## Calibration and reliability

- Before each component, scorers independently rate five anchors spanning bands and relevant stream overlays.
- Target agreement/tolerance must be frozen after pilot; until then, any mastery-band disagreement or >10-point difference on a 100-point component is material.
- Each score retains scorer, packet, validator, rubric, anchor and moderation versions.
- Report inter-rater agreement and disagreement by criterion, section and stream; never force identical curves.
- If a stream lacks enough calibrated expertise, hold that score rather than substitute generic AI judgment.

## Capacity decision required from MU

Confirm scorer names/roles, weekly availability, grade turnaround, moderation authority, academic-integrity process, appeal SLA and compensation/TA model. If approximately 338 hours cannot be staffed, reduce graded components or evidence scope; do not revert to sampled judgment or unreviewed model scoring.
