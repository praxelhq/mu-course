# Course 1 Delivery Readiness and Procurement Checklist

**Owner view:** Praxel, Masters' Union Program Associate, academic team, IT, facilitators  
**Status:** Provisional until delivery dates and supported tools are frozen

## Critical assumptions to close first

- [ ] 20 hours confirmed as live contact or total learner effort.
- [ ] 8–12 hour asynchronous workload accepted or portfolio scope reduced.
- [ ] assessment weights and 70/30 rubric approved.
- [ ] public portfolio/recording/consent policy approved.
- [ ] trained primary-scorer, moderation and double-score capacity approved.
- [ ] section calendar, gaps, room and network confirmed.
- [ ] batch profile and accommodations received.

## Student readiness

### Required

- [ ] current laptop and charger;
- [ ] current Chrome-based browser and ability to download/upload files;
- [ ] headphones and microphone for designated exercises;
- [ ] course LMS/SSO access;
- [ ] centrally provisioned Git/repository access;
- [ ] centrally supported AI/build accounts;
- [ ] spreadsheet access;
- [ ] technology check completed;
- [ ] AI maturity baseline completed;
- [ ] career stream, Finance subpath where applicable, and target-employer/sector preferences submitted;
- [ ] privacy, identity, publication and academic-integrity acknowledgement completed.

### Not required

- coding experience;
- a paid personal AI subscription;
- company insider access;
- proprietary datasets;
- an external professional network;
- prior Git, automation, deployment, image, voice or video experience.

## Timeline

### T-8 to T-6 weeks

- close workload, grading, publication, staffing and tool-budget decisions;
- receive batch profile;
- choose golden paths and owners;
- build/validate three high-contrast golden journeys: Finance, HR and Marketing;
- paper-pilot the common mission grammar.

### T-5 to T-3 weeks

- complete nine pathway packs and calibrated fallback data;
- load-test accounts, credits, network and LMS;
- pilot pre/post forms, validators, task exchange and facilitator dashboards;
- calibrate rubric anchors and moderation.

### T-2 weeks

- freeze tool stack, cases, sources, hidden variants and runbooks;
- publish student technology check and prerequisite tasks;
- run facilitator training and no-network rehearsal;
- confirm accommodations and consent choices.

### T-7 days per session

- publish pre-work and student-visible materials;
- hold hidden tests, answer keys and audit anchors in evaluator view;
- verify all links, accounts, quotas and fallback packs.

### T-30 minutes

- run demo and normal/failure fixtures from classroom network;
- check section roster and heat map;
- stage offline pack and incident contact.

### Within 24 hours after each section

- collect timing, completion, misconception, reliability, accessibility and feedback pulse;
- classify platform incidents separately from learner difficulty;
- record any change in the decision/change log;
- do not change assessed difficulty silently between sections;
- validate an equivalent variant or defer the change to the next common release.

## Release authority

A session releases only when Curriculum, LMS/Engineering, Delivery/Facilitation and Safety/Privacy owners sign off on their relevant gates. Blockers include invalid learning evidence, unsafe behavior, impossible timing, incorrect scoring, inaccessible core evidence, or no reliable fallback.
