| assigned row/inputs from `content-packs.md` | all eleven content packs and allocation/equivalence metadata |
| permitted source/data files | hidden/malformed/duplicate/policy/transfer fixtures |

## Source and case procurement rules

- The central [anchor case bank](../research/2026-07-17-anchor-case-bank.md) identifies primary sources and build use.
- Recheck every external link and volatile company/regulatory fact within 30 days of delivery.
- Archive only permitted excerpts/snapshots; record title, publisher, URL, date accessed, license/use note and owner.
- Separate source fact, instructor inference and course-authored synthetic scenario visibly.
- Never make an assessed answer depend on a live webpage, paywall, search rank or mutable model output.
- Replace a case when it cannot create a build decision within 15 minutes or cannot be supported lawfully and accessibly.

## Required decisions from Masters' Union

The Program Associate can coordinate but the named owner must approve:

1. whether 20 hours means live contact or total student effort;
2. 8–12 hours of structured asynchronous work or reduced portfolio scope;
3. weights, attendance/late rules, feedback/grade turnaround, scorer capacity and appeal SLA;
4. portfolio publication, pseudonymous/private route, consent and recording;
5. allowed vendors, processors/regions, retention/deletion, incident contacts and source extraction;
6. batch stream mix, prior experience, devices, language/support needs and accommodations;
7. facilitator ratio, rooms, network, blocked domains, calendars and section spacing.

## Upload and release checklist

At T-7 days for each session, the Program Associate receives a manifest with visible asset versions and checksums. At T-30 minutes, the section lead runs normal and failure demos from the classroom network, verifies roster/hidden-form assignment, and stages the offline pack. After the section, learner feedback and operational data arrive within 24 hours; assessed difficulty does not change for later sections until an equivalent version is reviewed and logged.

An authored repository is not permission to upload. Student-visible release still requires content, source-rights, accessibility, technical and section-owner sign-off.
