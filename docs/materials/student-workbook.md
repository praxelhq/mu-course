## 14. Final release checklist

- [ ] every material claim traces to evidence/calculation;
- [ ] raw/derived/synthetic/consented sources are labelled;
- [ ] prototype/replay works on evaluator route;
- [ ] expected and safe-failure checks pass;
- [ ] assigned attempts and v2 rerun are linked;
- [ ] secrets/PII/confidential data are absent;
- [ ] identity/media/recording rights and disclosure pass;
- [ ] public and private evidence are separated;
- [ ] accessibility alternative exists;
- [ ] setup/replay instructions work for a new reviewer;
- [ ] supplied/generated/adapted/authored contributions are declared;
- [ ] independent-project label is present;
- [ ] public publication choice is recorded and revocable.
