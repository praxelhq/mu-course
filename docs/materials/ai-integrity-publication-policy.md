# AI Use, Data, Integrity and Publication Policy

**Status:** draft for Masters' Union academic/privacy/legal approval  
**Applies to:** course LMS, external tools, repositories, task attempts, media and portfolio  
**Principle:** AI use is expected. Honest provenance, inspectable judgment and accountable control—not artificial “AI-free” work—define integrity.

## Allowed assistance by default

Students may use the centrally supported AI environment to research within supplied rules, critique framing, extract/transform approved data, generate/review code, draft method/workflow components, create media, debug and rehearse. They may use supplied templates, starter repositories, examples and shared methods when origin is declared.

The learner remains responsible for:

- selecting the role, decision, evidence and authority boundary;
- checking sources, calculations, outputs and risks;
- accepting/rejecting/editing model suggestions;
- testing normal, boundary and failure cases;
- recording supplied/generated/adapted/authored contributions;
- making the recommendation and defending limitations.

## Task-specific restrictions

| Task | Allowed | Not allowed |

| Normal studios | supported AI, templates, paired testing, facilitator hints | fabricated evidence; hidden answer keys; unapproved private data |
| Quizzes | permitted resources stated on the form; Form B after feedback | sharing item/answer bank; using another learner's response |
| Assigned task attempt | supplied role/input only; pseudonymous route; allowed evidence capture | author coaching during attempt; personal login/data; public sharing |

| Maturity pre/post | resources explicitly stated for both parallel administrations | using graded Session 10 output as the post mission |

Assistive technology and approved accommodations are always permitted and are not “AI help” violations.

## Provenance labels

Every consequential component is labelled:

- **supplied:** course case, dataset, template, starter, fixture or model output;
- **generated:** output accepted substantially as produced by a model/tool;
- **adapted:** supplied/generated/open material changed for this contract;
- **authored:** learner-created decision, method, analysis or implementation;
- **observed:** privacy-safe task behavior recorded through the LMS.

Origin, source/license, tool/model where relevant, version and meaningful change are recorded. Commit count, writing style and detector scores are not ownership evidence.

## Evidence and data rules

Allowed evidence is public, licensed, course-supplied synthetic or explicitly consented, and must be labelled. Public availability alone is not permission to copy, scrape or republish.

Students may extract only from the course allow-list and must follow:

- source terms, license and robots/rate guidance supplied by the course;
- minimum necessary fields and bounded request volume;
- snapshot date, exact locator and transformation trail;
- no login bypass, paywall circumvention, anti-bot evasion or collection of personal contact/profile data;
- no republishing copyrighted material beyond permitted excerpt/analysis.

Prohibited inputs include:

- confidential or proprietary company/client information;
- personal credentials, tokens or secrets;
- real resumes, employee/candidate files, performance, compensation, health, grievance or protected-class data;
- unapproved customer/account/CRM records;
- personal tester content or identifiable analytics;
- any data obtained without lawful/authorized access.

If uncertain, stop and use the supplied synthetic/snapshot fallback.

## High-impact and external-action boundary

Course systems do not autonomously:

- rank/select/reject candidates or employees;
- give investment, lending, legal, medical or employment advice;
- approve budgets/capital or change financial records;
- alter operational resources/safety controls;
- contact sales/marketing recipients, post publicly or spend money;
- make a release/publication decision with material harm potential.

Use sandbox/mock actions, explicit human approval, logged rationale, escalation/appeal and safe stop. More autonomy does not earn more credit.

## Identity, voice, images and video

- No unauthorized living-person voice or likeness imitation.
- Controlled identity work uses fictional, course-cleared consenting, public-domain or non-imitative composite material.
- Synthetic/altered media is disclosed to the viewer.
- Recordings require informed consent, a non-recording alternative and a withdrawal/unpublish route.
- Assets/models/templates/music/voice/image sources and rights are logged.
- Deceptive affiliation, endorsement, testimonial or “official campaign” framing is prohibited.

## Task-attempt privacy

Testers use supplied synthetic inputs and pseudonymous identities. They do not:

- sign in with personal email/social account;
- make payments;
- install extensions;
- grant broad connectors/contact access;
- enter personal or third-party data;
- publish or retain another student's content outside the LMS route.

The route must disclose external processor/data/retention before consent. Testers may decline an unsafe, inaccessible, harassing or privacy-violating route without grade penalty. Optional quotes/recordings are not required. Consent withdrawal and deletion/unpublish follow the approved governance process.

## Academic-integrity violations

Potential violations include:

- fabricated source, citation, dataset, calculation, user, interview, attempt, log, consent or performance metric;
- presenting synthetic/generated content as observed or real;
- copying/outsourcing a submission while concealing origin and lacking control;
- accessing/sharing hidden tests, keys, another learner's transfer packet or restricted later-section variant;
- falsifying company affiliation or approval;
- deliberate unsafe data/identity/external action;
- sabotaging peer tests or submitting noise for participation credit.

Similarity and automated integrity signals create a review, never automatic guilt. Shared templates and cases are expected. A named human uses provenance, checkpoints, test/transfer performance and student response under MU policy.

## Publication contract

Public publication is learner-controlled and not required for the grade. A private or pseudonymous recruiter/evaluator view is equivalent.

Every public case page states:

> Independent educational project; not commissioned by or affiliated with the company. Public, licensed, course-supplied synthetic, or consented evidence as labelled; no confidential company data.

Public content must exclude secrets/PII, private tester identity, confidential/restricted evidence, answer keys, unauthorized assets, unqualified financial/employment claims and “validated/market fit” language based on tiny assigned samples.

Required task-attempt wording:

> These assigned attempts test task behavior and boundaries in a small course sample; they do not establish demand or market validation.

Publication can be quarantined for safety repair without erasing private learning evidence. Learners can request unpublish/consent withdrawal through the approved route.

## Incidents, appeals and fair process

- Platform failure is logged separately from learner failure and grants equivalent retry/evidence where verified.
- Students can contest feedback/score by naming criterion and frozen evidence.
- No score changes without a named human, evidence citation, reason and version history.
- Accommodation changes time/modality while preserving the construct.
- Safety/privacy reports are contained promptly and shared on a least-privilege basis.

Final late, absence, misconduct, appeal, retention/deletion and sanction rules follow approved Masters' Union policy and must be published before assessment begins.
