# Student Field Guide — AI for Business: The Role-Ready Operator

**Course:** Masters' Union PGP, Course 1  
**Live format:** ten 120-minute studios  
**Portfolio home:** Praxy  
**Status:** student-facing draft; tool links and MU policies added after approval

## What you will be able to show

You will not finish with a folder of unrelated AI demos. You will perform a representative piece of work for the role you want:

> I investigated a consequential decision, built a bounded AI operating prototype, tested where it failed, repaired it, and published evidence a recruiter can inspect.

Every learner builds three connected objects:

1. **Decision evidence brief:** a sourced, data-bearing recommendation with one original calculation, claim ledger and counter-hypothesis.
2. **AI operating prototype:** an app, workflow, grounded assistant, dashboard/model, data pipeline or supported combination selected for the work—not for fashion.
3. **Recruiter case page and proof pack:** a 60–90 second public story backed by private evidence and a reproducible repository/replay.

## Choose a work mode

- **Operator:** perform an internal or customer task in the style of a target employer. Examples: PM release decision, FP&A scenario review, workforce planning, exception management, sales next action.
- **Advisor/investor:** perform a client, deal or investment task in the style of a Consulting, IB or VC employer.

Your project is defined by:

`target role × representative work context × consequential decision × inspectable evidence`

A favourite company creates motivation and recruiter context. It does not give you permission to claim affiliation, invent internal data or depend on employees.

## The studio rhythm

Expect to:

1. predict before assistance;
2. watch one mechanism opened, not a long lecture;
3. build from a supported scaffold;
4. receive a plausible failure or changed requirement;
5. diagnose the failing layer;
6. repair and rerun;
7. transfer to a new case;
8. save inspectable evidence.

The failure is part of the curriculum. A safe refusal or escalation can be a correct result.

## Grade and evidence

| Component | Weight |
| --- | ---: |
| Decision evidence brief | 20% |
| AI operating prototype | 30% |
| Individual unseen transfer sprint | 25% |
| Recruiter case page and proof pack | 15% |
| Structured participation and peer attack | 10% |

Major components use 70% common capability criteria and 30% role/pathway judgment. A trained human scores every judgment-bearing frozen packet. Automated checks verify observable facts; AI may coach or organize evidence but does not issue the final criterion score. Peers observe task behavior and never grade you.

Lesson quiz rule: 3/3 is ready for that construct set; 2/3 triggers a repair before Form B; 0–1/3 adds facilitator support. Mastery is based on cumulative evidence, not one short quiz.

The pre/post maturity measure is separate from your grade. It includes parallel low-stakes performance forms and confidence calibration.

## What participation means

Participation is evidence of professional operating behavior:

- arrive with the account/file/pre-work gate complete;
- perform assigned peer tests honestly;
- capture observable evidence and one useful comment;
- respond to received evidence;
- protect data, consent, identity and system boundaries;
- disclose sources, templates and AI assistance.

Speaking frequency, popularity, speed, accent, visual style, paid tools and public traffic are not participation.

## Supported path and advanced path

The course provides one supported path for research/building, Git, workflow, deployment/replay, extraction, data and media. Coding is not a prerequisite. You may use another stack if it satisfies the same contracts, but live support is not guaranteed and complexity earns no bonus.

Never buy a tool for the grade. Centrally provisioned or fallback routes must support every assessed outcome.

## Evidence discipline

Label every material statement as one of:

- source fact;
- derived calculation;
- inference;
- assumption;
- model suggestion;
- observed task behavior.

Keep raw/source snapshots, exact locators, transformations, units, dates, formulas and limitations. Public reviews or web content can generate a hypothesis; they do not automatically establish truth, permission or user research.

## Safe operating rules

- Use only public, licensed, course-supplied synthetic or explicitly consented evidence as labelled.
- Public visibility is not permission to scrape or republish. Use the course allow-list and supplied terms/license/rate rules.
- Never upload confidential company information, real employee records, credentials, health/compensation/grievance data or unapproved PII.
- No autonomous high-impact HR, finance, operations, sales or marketing action.
- Consequential action needs a named human owner, approval, escalation or safe stop.
- No unauthorized living-person voice/likeness imitation. Identity/voice work uses fictional, consenting, public-domain or non-imitative material.
- Never fabricate sources, data, users, interviews, logs, metrics, consent or company affiliation.
- Declare supplied, generated, adapted and authored contributions.

Public project label:

> Independent educational project; not commissioned by or affiliated with the company. Public, licensed, course-supplied synthetic, or consented evidence as labelled; no confidential company data.

## Assigned task attempts

The LMS—not your social network—assigns three to five role/input attempts. You will test peers and receive tests.

- Use only supplied synthetic inputs and the pseudonymous route.
- Never sign into a peer prototype with personal email/social account, pay, install an extension or grant broad connector access.
- You may safely decline an unsafe, inaccessible or privacy-violating route without grade penalty.
- An attempt can end in success, partial completion, safe failure or unsafe failure; authentic observation matters.
- Three to five assigned attempts are a small formative sample, not evidence of demand or market validation.

## Public and private proof

Your public view is short, sanitized and learner-controlled. Your private proof view can hold richer evaluator evidence. Public publication, real name and URL visibility are subject to MU consent policy; private/pseudonymous submission is an equivalent graded route.

Never place confidential, licensed-without-permission, personal tester or answer-key material in the public view. You can withdraw optional media/publication through the published route.

## When systems fail

Every core task has a printable, cached, replay or static alternative. Platform incidents are recorded separately from learner performance. Use the incident route; do not hide a failure or improvise with sensitive data. Verified incidents receive equivalent time/evidence, not a lower score.
