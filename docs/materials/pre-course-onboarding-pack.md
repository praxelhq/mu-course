# Pre-Course Onboarding Pack — Student and Facilitator Version

**Version:** 0.1 — 18 July 2026  
**Student time:** 83 minutes under the standard route, completed before Session 1; approved accommodations may extend time without changing the construct  
**Purpose:** remove setup waste, establish safe operating boundaries, capture a versioned baseline under common assigned conditions, and route each student to an authentic career pathway.

## What students see

> This is not a coding course and it is not a tour of AI tools. In ten missions you will investigate one consequential decision for a role you want, build and test an AI operating prototype, and publish a recruiter-legible work sample. You may use AI heavily. You will be asked to prove the evidence, choices, failures, repairs, and limits you personally own.

Completion unlocks Session 1. Diagnostic performance does not affect the course grade.

## Student technology check

The check records only pass/fail/incident for each capability:

- current browser can open the LMS and course repository;
- a supplied ZIP downloads and a supplied text file uploads;
- a supplied CSV opens with rows and columns intact;
- the supported AI workspace can return a one-line response;
- the build/replay shell can open a static sample;
- captions/transcript are visible on a 15-second media sample;
- keyboard-only navigation can reach Submit and Save draft;
- the learner can export a local copy of an evidence receipt.

Students never paste credentials, personal API keys, confidential files, or company records into the check. A failed capability routes to support and does not block the diagnostic when an equivalent static path exists.

## Integrity and data-boundary check

Use six randomized scenarios. The learner must correctly classify all six after feedback.

1. A company's published annual report downloaded from its investor-relations site — **allowed with source, date, and license/use notes**.
2. A friend sends an internal customer export “just for the class” — **prohibited; do not upload or retain**.
3. A course CSV labelled synthetic — **allowed; preserve the label and data dictionary**.
4. A public creator's voice cloned without permission because the output is “educational” — **prohibited unless public-domain/authorized course identity conditions are met**.
5. A user interview recording with documented course consent and a private evidence setting — **allowed within the consent scope; publication is a separate choice**.
6. Automated extraction from a public site whose terms/robots/license have not been checked — **unknown/stop; use the course snapshot or request allow-list review**.

Required acknowledgement:

> I will not fabricate sources, users, logs, task attempts, company access, or affiliation. I will label public, licensed, course-supplied synthetic, and explicitly consented evidence; disclose material AI assistance; preserve human approval for consequential actions; and move uncertain or private material to the stop/escalation route.

## Two onboarding retrieval checks

### OBR-01 — What earns credit?

A student uses a premium model to produce a beautiful app overnight. The core decision is unclear, the data is invented, and no one can reproduce a task. Which statement matches the course?

- A. Premium-tool quality earns strong prototype credit.
- B. The app is sufficient if the student explains the idea confidently.
- C. The artifact needs a consequential decision, inspectable evidence, task behavior, and ownership proof. **Correct**
- D. The work is disallowed because AI was used heavily.

Feedback: AI use is expected. Uninspectable success is not evidence of operator capability.

### OBR-02 — What does the company choice mean?

An IB candidate selects a consumer brand they admire. What is the most authentic next move?

- A. Pretend to work inside the brand's management team.
- B. Build a generic chatbot about the brand.
- C. Choose a target banking role and perform a representative deal/advisory decision using public or supplied evidence. **Correct**
- D. Contact brand executives; privileged access is required.

Feedback: the selected company may be a subject, client, target, or motivating context. It does not confer affiliation or internal access.

## Facilitator/admin setup

Before the onboarding window opens:

- import roster, section, assignment sequence and permitted accommodations;
- assign Part A form and counterbalanced performance mode/form;
- load the current policy and course-contract versions;
- verify help-desk, IT, accessibility and privacy queues;
- test the static diagnostic and paper/upload incident routes;
- suppress scores, answer keys and cohort comparisons from the learner view;
- stage Part B so the brief, sources, policy and dataset open first; freeze initial thesis/confidence before revealing the AI draft;
- set automatic reminders at T-72, T-24 and T-4 hours;
- verify that failure of a tool health check cannot become a grade penalty.

## LMS states and events

States:

`not_started → contract_complete → tech_complete_or_routed → pathway_provisional → policy_mastered → diagnostic_complete → launch_card_complete → session_1_unlocked`

Illegal transitions fail closed. A facilitator may grant an incident-equivalent route but may not mark diagnostic responses correct.

Events:

- `onboarding_started`
- `course_contract_acknowledged`
- `capability_health_checked`
- `support_route_assigned`
- `pathway_selected`
- `policy_item_answered`
- `policy_acknowledged`
- `maturity_part_a_submitted`
- `maturity_part_b_submitted`
- `launch_direction_submitted`
- `session_1_unlocked`

All events bind learner, section, content version, form assignment, timestamp, accessibility/incident route where relevant, and idempotency key. No event contains credentials or confidential source content.

## Accessibility and incident parity

- all video/audio has caption, transcript, and text-equivalent evidence;
- scenario and performance forms support keyboard and screen reader use;
- extended time is applied server-side without exposing the accommodation;
- calculations may be entered in a spreadsheet or accessible table/form;
- a static/paper packet uses the same source set, defect, response fields, and rubric;
- static/paper Part B seals the AI draft as page/envelope 2 until the invigilator records initial thesis/confidence;
- an outage is recorded separately from learner performance and triggers a new equivalent attempt;
- the pathway selector can be completed with an advisor when language or career certainty is a barrier.

## Completion message

> You are ready. You do not need a final project idea before Session 1. Bring one role direction, curiosity about a real decision, and the willingness to reject an impressive idea that cannot be evidenced, bounded, or tested.
