# Course 1 Benchmark Against Praxel's Market Research Simulator

**Review date:** 18 July 2026  
**Benchmark:** the learner-facing experience at `mr.praxel.in` and its visible mission grammar; no claim is made here about unverified private implementation details.  
**Question:** will Course 1 land with the same sense of a coherent, authored simulation rather than a collection of course files?

## Candid verdict

The Course 1 source package is now stronger than the Market Research simulator as a **learning and assessment architecture**: it has a ten-session cumulative portfolio, eleven authentic career pathways, explicit prediction/failure/repair/transfer, student-ownership evidence, human-scoring boundaries, accessible/offline parity, safe publication and recruiter proof.

The Market Research simulator remains ahead as a **currently felt product experience**: a learner can enter one coherent business story, see stages and progress, interact with personas, receive immediate feedback, and experience consequences without reading implementation documents. Course 1 does not match that felt quality until its authored states, fixtures, copy, feedback and artifacts are loaded into a real Mission Room and rehearsed.

Therefore:

- **course logic/source completeness:** Course 1 now meets or exceeds the benchmark;
- **deployed learner delight:** Market Research remains the benchmark until Course 1 is implemented and piloted;
- **launch claim today:** authored source PASS; product/classroom launch not yet proven.

## What to inherit directly

| Market Research strength | Course 1 implementation contract |
| --- | --- |
| one coherent client narrative | one role-ready work sample accumulates across ten episodes; no unrelated tool trophies |
| visible staged progress | ten session missions and within-session states show what is locked, active, repaired and proven |

| immediate quality feedback | mechanical and evidence-citing coaching arrives before the in-session repair; high-stakes judgment remains human |

| bias/checkpoint tension | every session has a plausible failure or changed requirement, prediction before reveal and a repair receipt |
| analysis-to-executive arc | the course moves from decision/evidence through method/workflow/prototype to stakeholder communication and recruiter proof |
| progress/XP/badges | reward only verification, safe stop, repair, counter-evidence, transfer and proof—not clicks, prompts, volume or speed |
| final presentation pressure | Session 10 uses secured unseen transfer and random evidence defence, not a polished showcase alone |

## What Course 1 deliberately adds

1. **Favourite company without fandom:** the company is a motivating employer/client/deal context; the work is defined by role, decision and evidence.
2. **Eleven pathway rows:** nine streams plus separate IB, VC and Corporate Finance work; the common engine does not become a noun-swapped chatbot.
3. **Ownership rail:** prediction, distinct assessment input, failure, before/after repair, clean transfer, provenance and defence distinguish student judgment from generated output.
4. **Three-object portfolio:** evidence brief, operating prototype, recruiter case/proof pack; every session advances one object.
5. **Assessment validity:** mechanical validators, rubric-bound coaching, trained-human primary scoring, moderation, appeals and double scoring.
6. **Safe publication:** public/private/excluded evidence, non-affiliation, identity/consent, secret/PII/rights checks and revocation.
7. **Reliability and inclusion:** no-code and advanced lanes share the grade ceiling; text/static/offline routes preserve the assessed decision.
8. **Learning measurement:** parallel low-stakes pre/post judgment/performance forms and confidence calibration remain separate from grades.

## The five screens/states that must make it feel real

The LMS should not expose a folder of Markdown. For every session, the product renders:

1. **Mission launch:** role, stakes, owner, decision, time and one provocative comparison; prediction commits before tools unlock.
2. **Control room:** supplied evidence, structured working surface, progress states and the one mechanism being manipulated.
3. **Incident reveal:** an explicit new source, malformed input, policy, stakeholder or requirement arrives after the baseline freezes.
4. **Repair receipt:** before/after evidence, tests, decision impact and human boundary become visible; feedback cites the learner's fields/logs.
5. **Proof dock:** artifact/version, recruiter proof action, private/public state, provenance and next-session bridge are saved.

This repeated shell gives consistency across eight sections. Content packs change role, schema, decision rule, failure, action, authority and proof while the interaction grammar remains learnable.

## Session-level “MR-quality” acceptance test

A reviewer with no course context should be able to take a supplied learner account and, within the intended two hours:

- understand the role and consequential decision in under three minutes;
- make a prediction before seeing the answer path;
- complete the supported task without instructor reconstruction;
- observe a system/stakeholder consequence from a choice;
- encounter the intended failure at the correct time;
- receive feedback that points to actual evidence;
- repair and rerun rather than merely rewrite;
- complete a distinct transfer or defence;
- export an inspectable portfolio receipt;
- finish through the accessible/offline route with equivalent evidence when the primary service is removed.

If this cannot happen from a real learner account, the existence of a beautiful source pack does not meet the benchmark.

## Release recommendation

Build one complete golden Mission Room shell and load three high-contrast journeys—Finance, HR and Marketing—before styling all ten independently. Pilot each with cold learners and one facilitator using the exact acceptance test above. Repair the shell and content model, then load the remaining packs. The benchmark is not “the screens look like Market Research”; it is that the learner feels one coherent professional story, makes consequential choices, sees why they were wrong, repairs the system, and can prove the learning afterward.
