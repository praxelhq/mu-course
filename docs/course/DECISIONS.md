# Course 1 Decision Log

Record durable curriculum decisions here. New entries append; superseded decisions remain visible.

## D-001 — Use the repository as the course source of truth

- **Date:** 15 July 2026
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** Maintain architecture, research, lesson plans, LMS specifications, assessments, materials, validators, and review findings in `praxelhq/mu-course`.
- **Reason:** Eight-section delivery and future LMS implementation require versioned, inspectable decisions rather than chat-only context.

## D-002 — Design backward from inspectable portfolio evidence

- **Date:** 15 July 2026
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** Begin with what students can open, demonstrate, explain, and defend; introduce tools only when an artifact needs the capability.
- **Reason:** Tool surveys and theoretical AI strategy do not create credible post-MBA evidence.

## D-003 — Separate artifact success from student ownership

- **Date:** 15 July 2026
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** Every substantial artifact has a success test and a distinct ownership test using prediction, failure diagnosis, clean-session transfer, unseen modification, or defence.
- **Reason:** Generative assistance can produce polished work without durable understanding.

## D-004 — Use one aspirational-company transformation case

- **Date:** 17 July 2026
- **Status:** Superseded in wording by D-014; aspirational context retained
- **Decision:** Each student chooses an aspirational employer and defines one `company × career stream/role × business problem × user/decision owner`. All artifacts accumulate inside that case.
- **Reason:** A connected case is more recruiter-legible than a generic venture or unrelated tool exercises.
- **Consequence:** The real-SME/40-day-client spine in COT v1.2 is superseded. Public/synthetic evidence and qualified proxy users replace privileged company access.

## D-005 — Cover nine streams through equivalent variants

- **Date:** 17 July 2026
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** Consulting, FOCOS, Product, Finance, HR, Supply Chain and Operations, Sales, Marketing, and Data share the same capability and evidence contract. Each receives cases, data, simulation variants, and sample portfolios.
- **Reason:** Nine separate curricula are infeasible and weaken common standards; generic examples privilege some hiring paths.

## D-006 — Integrate the artifact set rather than run seven projects

- **Date:** 17 July 2026
- **Status:** Superseded by D-013
- **Decision:** Retain the opportunity dossier, grounded stakeholder interface, reusable skill, workflow, thin application, stakeholder film, and evidence repository as connected modules of one system.
- **Reason:** Seven independent portfolio-grade builds cannot fit 20 live hours and create artifact theatre.
- **Clarifications:** A clone must be grounded, disclosed, and consent-cleared/synthetic; five users means qualified task completion; the article is evidence-led rather than stylistically imitating a publication; the film is graded on control and business truth, not percentage generated by AI.

## D-007 — Preserve the strongest v1.2 delivery mechanics

- **Date:** 17 July 2026
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** Retain 20 live hours, build-first sessions, pre-work, recurring challenge/failure, portfolio capture, live capability sprint, and final stack defence. Replace the Rambo/rotating-brand/live-SME story.
- **Reason:** The format already protects action, feedback, and demonstrable work; the narrative no longer matches the recruiter-facing portfolio strategy.

## D-008 — Use three studios and two continuous rails

- **Date:** 17 July 2026
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** Intelligence (Sessions 1–3), Systems (4–5), Shipping (6–8), with Evidence and Growth rails across the course; Sessions 9–10 synthesize and transfer.
- **Reason:** Capabilities have dependencies and must accumulate rather than restart by artifact.

## D-009 — Keep tool choices replaceable

- **Date:** 17 July 2026
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** Freeze capabilities, input/output contracts, and validators. Freeze exact tools only near delivery, with one supported path and an advanced lane.
- **Reason:** Tool names change faster than business capabilities and eight sections need supportable consistency.

## D-010 — Use the LMS as an evidence and simulation system

- **Date:** 17 July 2026
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** Build staged authentic workflows, persistent consequences, AI personas, predictions, injected failures, structured submissions, validators, feedback, facilitator heat maps, and publish gates. Use XP/badges only for consequential learning behavior.
- **Reason:** File upload and completion tracking cannot establish ownership or support 480 students.
- **Reference:** Praxel Market Research Simulation demonstrates staged workflow, AI personas, persistent context, quality feedback, unlocks, XP, and behavioral badges; Course 1 will add stronger evidence, transfer, and integrity controls.

## D-011 — Keep pre/post maturity growth separate from grades

- **Date:** 17 July 2026
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** Use parallel scenario questions, messy-business performance missions, and confidence calibration. Do not award grades for raw self-reported growth.
- **Reason:** Repeated self-rating is gameable and does not demonstrate transfer.

## D-012 — Plan for asynchronous portfolio work

- **Date:** 17 July 2026
- **Status:** Proposed; requires MU confirmation
- **Decision:** Plan for 20 live hours plus 8–12 structured asynchronous hours. If 20 hours is total effort, reduce the required public artifact set.
- **Reason:** Research, user testing, deployment, and portfolio-quality revision cannot all be completed credibly inside 20 total hours.

## D-013 — Reduce the universal portfolio to three assessed objects

- **Date:** 17 July 2026
- **Status:** Accepted after adversarial review
- **Decision:** Universally assess a decision evidence brief, one role-authentic AI operating prototype, and a recruiter case page/proof pack. Treat clone/RAG, skill, workflow, app, dashboard, data pipeline, images, voice, and video as studio experiences or prototype/communication components rather than separate universal trophies.
- **Reason:** Seven universal artifacts are not credible in 20 hours, create 3,360 submissions, and structurally privilege Product and Marketing.

## D-014 — Use operator and advisor/investor modes

- **Date:** 17 July 2026
- **Status:** Accepted after adversarial review
- **Decision:** Formal project definition is `target role × representative work context × consequential decision × inspectable evidence`. Operator mode fits internal/customer decisions; advisor/investor mode fits Consulting, IB, and VC client/deal/investment work.
- **Reason:** `company × function × problem` misrepresents authentic work in advisory and investing roles.

## D-015 — Replace mandatory external users with an assigned task-attempt exchange

- **Date:** 17 July 2026
- **Status:** Accepted after adversarial review
- **Decision:** The LMS assigns three to five qualified testers with role cards and input packs. External users are optional enrichment and never a grading advantage.
- **Reason:** Mandatory external recruitment measures social access and consumer-project fit as much as system quality.

## D-016 — Use a 70/30 common-plus-stream rubric

- **Date:** 17 July 2026
- **Status:** Accepted after adversarial review
- **Decision:** Seventy percent of artifact judgment uses common capability criteria; thirty percent uses one-page stream anchors. Finance includes IB, VC, and Corporate Finance exemplars.
- **Reason:** One generic rubric under-values role expertise; nine independent rubrics destroy comparability.

## D-017 — Use bounded human scoring rather than universal live viva or unreviewed AI grading

- **Date:** 17 July 2026
- **Status:** Superseded by release review correction on 17 July 2026
- **Decision:** Mechanically validate 100%, require 100% individual transfer, assign structured peer attacks, and use a trained human as the primary scorer for 100% of judgment-bearing graded evidence. AI may assemble evidence and coach but does not issue final criterion scores. Human moderation covers all flags/borderlines/appeals, and at least 10% of each major component is double-scored across sections.
- **Reason:** A universal live viva is infeasible, but sampling human judgment leaves most 70/30 rubric scores without a valid scorer. Bounded evidence packets make universal asynchronous human scoring feasible and auditable.

## D-018 — Separate maturity growth from the graded final sprint

- **Date:** 17 July 2026
- **Status:** Accepted internally; workload requires MU confirmation
- **Decision:** Administer a separate low-stakes 25–35 minute post performance mission under conditions parallel to the pre mission. Counterbalance operator and advisor/investor forms. The graded 120-minute Session 10 transfer sprint cannot substitute for the growth measure.
- **Reason:** Different time, stakes, scaffolding and context would confound a pre/post learning-growth claim.

## D-019 — Release complete productized packs for every two-hour session

- **Date:** 18 July 2026
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** A lesson plan and quiz are not a session package. Every session must ship the complete source set defined in `docs/operations/session-package-release-contract.md`: pre-work, case/content packs, student and instructor surfaces, demo, simulator, solution anchors, post-work, LMS copy/events, structured fixtures, and accessibility/outage parity.
- **Reason:** Eight-section delivery cannot depend on an instructor reconstructing cases, assets, feedback, or recovery paths from curriculum prose.

## D-020 — Use the cold, initially uninterested student as the experience benchmark

- **Date:** 18 July 2026
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** Internally release a session only when it clears the Cold-Student Experience Rubric: a consequential hook and learner commitment within ten minutes; meaningful agency; a plausible failure or changed requirement; feedback and repair; transfer/ownership evidence; and recruiter-visible proof. The threshold is at least 22/26 with mandatory dimensions at full score.
- **Reason:** “Hands-on” can still mean copying instructions. The course should produce attention, surprise, judgment, durable learning, and demonstrable career evidence—not activity theatre.

## D-021 — Separate authored source readiness from launch claims

- **Date:** 18 July 2026
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** Track session assets through Authored, Validated, Piloted, Loaded, and Rehearsed states. This production run may complete and validate source assets, but must not silently claim learner pilot results, LMS implementation, institutional approval, staffing, provisioning, or room/network rehearsal.
- **Reason:** A complete course package is a prerequisite for implementation and pilot, not evidence that external dependencies have already passed.
